Terms of use

The terms for using getgradify.com and every Gradify site, in plain English. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Who you're dealing with. Gradify is run by Gradify Ltd. Questions about these terms: hello@getgradify.com. How your personal data is handled is on the privacy page.

Using Gradify

By using Gradify you agree to these terms. Gradify has three plans: Free, Gradify Pro and Gradify Ultra. What each includes is on the pricing page. You can use most of Free without an account.

Accounts

  • Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Children under 13 can use Gradify without an account, with a parent or guardian.
  • You sign in with an email link or with Google. Keep access to that email or Google account secure, since anyone with it can sign in as you.
  • One account works across every Gradify site.

Your notes and work

  • Your notes, answers and anything else you add stay yours. You give Gradify permission to store them and process them only to provide Gradify to you: making study packs and flashcards, scheduling your revision and showing your progress.
  • Only add material you have the right to use. Don't add other people's personal details, such as patients' or clients' information.
  • You can download or delete your data from Account in the Gradify app, as the privacy page explains.

Study material, not professional advice

Gradify's courses and the flashcards it makes are for learning and revision. They're written and checked with care, but they can contain mistakes, and they aren't legal, medical, clinical, financial or any other professional advice. Completing a Gradify course is not a professional qualification and doesn't authorise you to practise. Check current, authoritative sources before relying on anything for real decisions, exams set by others, or patients and clients.

Flashcards made from your notes are only as accurate as the notes. Each card shows the line it came from so you can check it, and cards that don't fully match are marked for you to look at.

Gradify AI

Gradify AI isn't switched on yet. When it is, its answers are produced by an AI model and can be wrong, so check anything important against your notes or another source. It's covered by monthly allowances shown on the pricing page.

Plans and payments

  • Gradify Pro is £10 a month and Gradify Ultra is £15 a month, in pounds sterling, including VAT. Gradify Ultra opens when Gradify AI launches. The pricing page shows what each plan includes and whether it's on sale.
  • Paying and renewing. You pay when you subscribe, and your plan renews automatically every month on the same date until you cancel. Payments are taken by Stripe; Gradify never sees your card number.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time from Manage billing on your Account page. You keep your plan until the end of the month you've paid for, then move to Free. Nothing you've made is deleted.
  • Your right to cancel within 14 days. You can cancel within 14 days of subscribing for a refund. Your plan starts straight away when you subscribe, at your request, so if you've used it during those days the refund is reduced in proportion to the days used. To cancel this way, use Manage billing or email hello@getgradify.com.
  • Failed payments. If a renewal payment fails, Stripe tries again over the next few days and emails you. You keep your plan for 3 days after the renewal date while it does; after that you move to Free until it's paid.
  • Changing plan. You can move between Pro and Ultra from Manage billing once both are on sale. Stripe adjusts the price for the rest of the month.
  • Price changes. If a plan's price changes, you'll be told by email at least 30 days before your next renewal at the new price, and you can cancel before then.
  • What Free includes. On Free you can open 10 lessons in each course you start, 5 flashcard sets and 5 mind maps, and use each tool in Tools 5 times. Anything you've already opened stays open. Pro and Ultra have no such limits.
  • Free and devices. On Free you study on one device at a time. Everything you've made stays in your account and can be opened on any device, and you can move your studying to another device once every 7 days. Pro and Ultra work on every device.

Fair use

Please don't misuse Gradify: no attempts to break or overload it, to get at other people's data, to scrape or copy courses in bulk, or to use it for anything unlawful. Gradify Ltd may suspend an account that does, and will tell you why where it can.

Community rules

Community (subject chats, study groups, leaderboards, tutors and mentors) is for signed-in learners aged 13 or over. Be kind and keep to learning. Use a first name or nickname, and never share your address, phone number, email address or social media: Gradify blocks links, email addresses and phone numbers in chats and messages. Don't arrange to meet anyone you only know from Gradify. If you're under 18, involve a parent or guardian before agreeing any tutoring or payment. Report anything that worries you: a message reported by several people is hidden straight away, and we may remove anyone who breaks these rules. Tutors and mentors are other learners, not checked or employed by Gradify.

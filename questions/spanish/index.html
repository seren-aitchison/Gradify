First Steps in Spanish

  1. What is the Spanish name of the letter j? (Foundation recall)
    • ge
    • hache
    • jai
    • jota
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    jota. The letter j is called jota; hache is h and ge is g. Knowing the names lets you spell your name aloud. Revise First Steps in Spanish →

  2. In the word guitarra, which letters are silent or combine into a single sound? (Exam style)
    • Every letter is pronounced separately
    • The g is silent and rr is two sounds
    • The u is pronounced like English w
    • The u is silent and rr is one trilled sound
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    The u is silent and rr is one trilled sound. In gui the u is silent so g keeps its hard sound; rr is a digraph spelling one trilled r: gi-TA-rra. Revise First Steps in Spanish →

  3. Why can an English learner usually read an unseen Spanish word aloud correctly but not an unseen English word? (Stretch)
    • Spanish has fewer vowel letters than English
    • Spanish letters map consistently onto sounds
    • Spanish never borrows words from other languages
    • Spanish words are always shorter than English ones
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Spanish letters map consistently onto sounds. Spanish spelling is close to phonemic, so the letters predict the sounds; English spelling preserves historical forms, so the same letters spell many sounds. Revise First Steps in Spanish →

The Present Tense, Family and Friends

  1. What is the tú form of trabajar? (Foundation recall)
    • trabaja
    • trabajáis
    • trabajas
    • trabajo
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    trabajas. Stem trabaj- plus the tú ending -as gives trabajas. Revise The Present Tense, Family and Friends →

  2. Who is the subject of Compran fruta en el mercado? (Exam style)
    • He or she
    • I
    • They or you (ustedes)
    • We
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    They or you (ustedes). The ending -an marks the third-person plural: they, or ustedes. Revise The Present Tense, Family and Friends →

  3. Which sentence about parents' jobs is correct? (Stretch)
    • Mis padres trabaja en un hospital
    • Mis padres trabajamos en un hospital
    • Mis padres trabajan en un hospital
    • Mis padres trabajas en un hospital
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Mis padres trabajan en un hospital. Mis padres is third-person plural, so the verb takes -an. Revise The Present Tense, Family and Friends →

Daily Life, School and Food

  1. Complete: Mi hermano ___ a las ocho. (levantarse) (Foundation recall)
    • levanta
    • me levanta
    • se levanta
    • se levantan
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    se levanta. Third-person singular subject takes se and the verb ending -a. Revise Daily Life, School and Food →

  2. Which sentence correctly says "We're going to get dressed"? (Exam style)
    • Nos vamos a vestirnos
    • Vamos a nos vestir
    • Vamos a vestirnos
    • Vamos a vestirse
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Vamos a vestirnos. With an infinitive, the pronoun matching nosotros attaches to it: vestirnos. Revise Daily Life, School and Food →

  3. How do you say "I brush my teeth"? (Stretch)
    • Lavo mis dientes
    • Me lavo los dientes
    • Me lavo mis dientes
    • Se lava los dientes
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Me lavo los dientes. With reflexive verbs and body parts Spanish uses the article: me lavo los dientes. Revise Daily Life, School and Food →

Town, Travel and Directions

  1. What is el polideportivo? (Foundation recall)
    • Police station
    • Sports centre
    • Swimming pool
    • Town hall
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Sports centre. El polideportivo is a sports centre; town hall is el ayuntamiento. Revise Town, Travel and Directions →

  2. How do you say "There isn't a station in my village"? (Exam style)
    • No es una estación en mi pueblo
    • No está estación en mi pueblo
    • No hay estación en mi pueblo
    • No hay la estación en mi pueblo
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    No hay estación en mi pueblo. Saying something doesn't exist uses no hay, usually with no article. Revise Town, Travel and Directions →

  3. Which answer gives the most balanced view of a city? (Stretch)
    • Lo bueno es que hay muchos museos; lo malo es que es muy ruidosa
    • Me gusta mi ciudad
    • Mi ciudad es grande y hay museos
    • Odio mi ciudad porque es fea
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Lo bueno es que hay muchos museos; lo malo es que es muy ruidosa. It states a positive and a negative, each with a reason, using lo bueno and lo malo. Revise Town, Travel and Directions →

Talking about the Past

  1. What is the él preterite of escribir? (Foundation recall)
    • escribe
    • escribí
    • escribía
    • escribió
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    escribió. -ir verbs take -ió in the third-person singular preterite. Revise Talking about the Past →

  2. Which form means "she spoke"? (Exam style)
    • habla
    • hablé
    • hablo
    • habló
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    habló. Habló (stress on the last syllable) is the third-person preterite; hablo is "I speak". Revise Talking about the Past →

  3. Which sentence about yesterday's tennis is correct? (Stretch)
    • Ayer mis amigos y yo juego al tenis y luego como pizza
    • Ayer mis amigos y yo jugamos al tenis y luego comemos pizza
    • Ayer mis amigos y yo jugamos al tenis y luego comimos pizza
    • Ayer mis amigos y yo jugaron al tenis y luego comieron pizza
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Ayer mis amigos y yo jugamos al tenis y luego comimos pizza. Mis amigos y yo is nosotros, and ayer requires the preterite for both actions. Revise Talking about the Past →

The Future, the Conditional and Work

  1. What does "hablaré" mean? (Foundation recall)
    • I speak
    • I spoke
    • I was speaking
    • I will speak
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    I will speak. Applying the standard result: -é on the infinitive means I will. Revise The Future, the Conditional and Work →

  2. Which is the correct future form of "vivir" for "they"? (Exam style)
    • Viven
    • Vivían
    • Vivieron
    • Vivirán
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Vivirán. Applying the standard result: Infinitive plus -án. Revise The Future, the Conditional and Work →

  3. Which sentence correctly uses the simple future? (Stretch)
    • Mañana visitar a mi abuela
    • Mañana visitaré a mi abuela
    • Mañana visité a mi abuela
    • Mañana visito a mi abuela ayer
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Mañana visitaré a mi abuela. Applying the standard result: Future for tomorrow. Revise The Future, the Conditional and Work →

The Subjunctive

  1. Which ending does an -ar verb take in the present subjunctive for "they"? (Foundation recall)
    • -an
    • -en
    • -ían
    • -on
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    -en. The -ar verbs swap to the opposite vowel e, so ellos form ends in -en, as in hablen. Revise The Subjunctive →

  2. Which is the present subjunctive of "salir" for "nosotros"? (Exam style)
    • Salamos
    • Salgamos
    • Salíamos
    • Salimos
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Salgamos. Salir has the yo form salgo, so the stem salg- plus -amos gives salgamos. Revise The Subjunctive →

  3. Which is the correct present subjunctive of "dormir" for "nosotros"? (Stretch)
    • Dormamos
    • Dormimos
    • Duermamos
    • Durmamos
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Durmamos. -ir stem-changers change o to u in nosotros, so the correct form is durmamos. Revise The Subjunctive →

Advanced Grammar and Accuracy

  1. Which completes "Este pastel es ___ ti"? (Foundation recall)
    • de
    • en
    • para
    • por
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    para. The cake is intended for you, a recipient, so para is required. Revise Advanced Grammar and Accuracy →

  2. Complete: "Te cambio mi bocadillo ___ tu manzana." (Exam style)
    • a
    • con
    • para
    • por
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    por. An exchange of one thing for another is expressed with por. Revise Advanced Grammar and Accuracy →

  3. Which sentence uses por and para correctly? (Stretch)
    • Salí para Londres y pasé para Oxford
    • Salí para Londres y pasé por Oxford
    • Salí por Londres y pasé para Oxford
    • Salí por Londres y pasé por Oxford para
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Salí para Londres y pasé por Oxford. Para marks the destination London, and por marks the route through Oxford. Revise Advanced Grammar and Accuracy →

The Spanish-Speaking World

  1. In which region is gallego spoken? (Foundation recall)
    • Andalucía
    • Cataluña
    • Galicia
    • Navarra
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Galicia. Gallego is the co-official language of Galicia in north-west Spain. Revise The Spanish-Speaking World →

  2. When do the Reyes Magos traditionally bring presents in Spain? (Exam style)
    • 19 March
    • 23 June
    • 25 December
    • 6 January
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    6 January. The Three Kings bring presents on 6 January, after the parade on 5 January. Revise The Spanish-Speaking World →

  3. Which sentence about languages in Spain is accurate? (Stretch)
    • Castilian is not official in Galicia
    • Catalan is only spoken in Madrid
    • Euskera has no known link to Latin
    • Gallego is a dialect of Basque
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Euskera has no known link to Latin. Basque is a language isolate, unlike Catalan, Galician and Spanish, which come from Latin. Revise The Spanish-Speaking World →

Skills: Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing and Translation

  1. Which word signals that the speaker never does something? (Foundation recall)
    • A veces
    • Nunca
    • Siempre
    • Todavía
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Nunca. Nunca means never, so it turns the sentence into a negative. Revise Skills: Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing and Translation →

  2. A speaker says "Antes jugaba al tenis, pero ahora prefiero el baloncesto". What sport does he prefer now? (Exam style)
    • Basketball
    • Both equally
    • Neither
    • Tennis
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Basketball. Pero ahora signals the change: tennis was in the past and basketball is his current preference. Revise Skills: Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing and Translation →

  3. A question asks "What does Luis think of his school?" Which phrase from the recording would most help you? (Stretch)
    • Hay mil alumnos en mi colegio
    • Lo que más me gusta es que los profes son simpáticos
    • Mi colegio está en el centro de la ciudad
    • Voy al colegio en autobús
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    Lo que más me gusta es que los profes son simpáticos. Only this phrase gives an opinion, which is what the question asks for. Revise Skills: Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing and Translation →

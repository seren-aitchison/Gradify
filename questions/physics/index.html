Foundations of Physics

  1. Which statement best describes the aim of physics? (Foundation recall)
    • To explain the widest range of phenomena with the fewest principles
    • To list every natural phenomenon
    • To prove theories with certainty
    • To replace mathematics with experiment
    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    To explain the widest range of phenomena with the fewest principles. Physics seeks economical, general, testable laws, not certainty or mere cataloguing. Revise Foundations of Physics →

  2. Which regime needs both quantum mechanics and special relativity? (Exam style)
    • A pendulum clock
    • Heat flow through a wall
    • High-energy particle collisions

    Reveal the answer and mark-scheme reasoning

    High-energy particle collisions. When particles move near c and quantum effects matter, you need quantum field theory. Revise Foundations of Physics →

  3. The metre is defined by fixing which quantity? (Stretch)

    • The second and Avogadro's number

    • The wavelength of krypton-86 light

    The speed of light. Since 1983 c is exact, so the metre is the distance light travels in 1/299 792 458 s. Revise Foundations of Physics →

## Eukaryotes and prokaryotes
S: Prokaryotic (bacterial) cells have a cell membrane, cytoplasm and a cell wall, but no nucleus.
LV: GCSE
O: Define prokaryotic cell and use the term correctly
O: Predict and explain telling prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells apart
PRE: Diffusion
H: The idea
P: Plant and animal cells are **eukaryotic cells**. Each has a cell membrane around its cytoplasm, and its genetic material is enclosed in a nucleus.
P: Bacterial cells are **prokaryotic cells**. Like plant and animal cells they have cytoplasm and a cell membrane, which is surrounded by a cell wall. Unlike them, they have no nucleus: their DNA lies loose in the cytoplasm.
P: Many bacteria also have one or more **plasmids**: small rings of DNA separate from the main loop, which carry a few extra genes.
K: **Prokaryotic cell**: a cell with no nucleus, such as a bacterial cell.
H: A bacterium's DNA
P: A bacterium's genetic material is a single loop of DNA, lying free in the cytoplasm rather than inside a nucleus.
H: How big prokaryotic cells are
P: Bacterial cells are much smaller in comparison with plant and animal cells: a typical bacterium is about 1–5 µm long, while most plant and animal cells are about 10–100 µm across.
P: For example:
- *E. coli*, a gut bacterium: about 2 µm long
- A human cheek cell: about 60 µm across

!: "Bacteria have no DNA because they have no nucleus." Bacteria do have DNA, just not inside a nucleus: a loop of it lies in the cytoplasm, often with plasmids as well.
!: "Plasmids are the bacterium's main DNA." The main DNA is the large loop; plasmids are small extra rings with only a few genes.
!: "A bacterial cell wall is made of cellulose, like a plant cell wall." Plant cell walls are made of cellulose; bacterial cell walls are made of a different material.
H: Telling prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells apart
P: To decide what kind of cell you are looking at, check for a nucleus first, then a cell wall.
- **A cell has a cell wall, no nucleus and a single loop of DNA:** it is a prokaryotic cell, such as a bacterium. It has no nucleus: its DNA lies loose in the cytoplasm.
- **A cell has a nucleus and a cell membrane but no cell wall:** it is a eukaryotic animal cell. It has a nucleus, and animal cells have no cell wall.
- **A cell has a nucleus, a cell wall and chloroplasts:** it is a eukaryotic plant cell. It has a nucleus, and only plant (and algal) cells have chloroplasts.
- **A cell is about 2 µm long and has small rings of DNA in its cytoplasm:** it is almost certainly a bacterium. Its size and plasmids are typical of a prokaryotic cell.

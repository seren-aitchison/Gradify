How to revise in less time, from the research on memory. Start with the complete guide to active recall.

  • Active recall vs re-reading: which actually works?Active recall works better.
  • How to use active recall for science subjectsIn science, use flashcards for definitions and facts, draw processes and diagrams from memory on a blank page, and practise calculations as unseen problems.
  • The best active recall schedule before finalsSpend the first four weeks covering every topic with active recall and spaced reviews, then use the last two weeks for timed past papers and your weakest topics.

  • How to revise in two weeksTriage first: list every topic, rank them by how likely they are to come up and how weak you are, and cut what you can't cover.

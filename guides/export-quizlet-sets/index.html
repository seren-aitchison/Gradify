How to export your Quizlet sets

By the Gradify team · Updated for the 2026–27 academic year

Quizlet lets you export sets you created as text: open the set, choose Export from the ⋯ menu, and copy the terms. Paste them into Anki (File → Import) or into Gradify's notes, and Gradify turns them into cards checked against your own notes.

How do I export a Quizlet set?

  1. Open a set you created on the Quizlet website.
  2. Click the ⋯ (more) menu and choose Export.
  3. Choose how terms and definitions are separated (a tab or comma is easiest).
  4. Copy the text.

Export is available for sets you made. Menus change, so if you can't see it, check Quizlet's help centre.

Key takeaway: Export works for your own sets: copy them as text.

How do I import into Anki or Gradify?

Anki: save the text as a .txt file, then File → Import and match the separator. Gradify: paste the text into Notes Studio, ideally alongside your lecture notes so each card can be checked against them.

What should I check after moving?

Formatting and images may not survive. Check cards with formulas, and delete duplicates.

Test yourself: Why paste your lecture notes alongside an exported set? Reveal

So each card can be checked against your own course material, catching errors in old or shared sets.

Questions

Can I export someone else's Quizlet set?

Export is designed for sets you created. For other people's sets, check the creator's permission and Quizlet's terms.

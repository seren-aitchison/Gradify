The 7 best study tools for university students in 2026

By the Gradify team · Based on the learning research named in the text

Anki is best for serious long-term memorisation, Quizlet for quick shared vocabulary sets, Notion for organising a whole degree, and Gradify for turning your own lecture notes into checked flashcards and a revision plan. Most students do well with one note tool and one recall tool.

Prices and plans change; check each tool's own site. We make Gradify, so weigh our view of it accordingly.

Which should you choose?

  • Medicine or a language: Anki, for its shared decks and scheduler. Gradify can make the cards from your own lectures to go alongside.
  • A class sharing vocab: Quizlet.
  • Lots of lecture PDFs and little time: Gradify, to get from notes to recall in a minute.
  • Disorganised across modules: Notion, plus one recall tool.
  • Distracted: Forest, or your phone's focus mode.

What matters more than the tool

The method. Any of these works if you test yourself and space the reviews; none works if you only re-read. Start with active recall.

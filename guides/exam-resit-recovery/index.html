Failed an exam? A resit recovery plan

By the Gradify team · Updated for the 2026–27 academic year

A failed exam is information, not a verdict. Get your feedback and script if you can, find out whether the problem was content, exam technique or time, check what your resit rules are, then build a plan aimed at that specific cause. Most resits are passed by students who change their method, not just their hours.

Core rule: Read the feedback before you plan: the fix depends on the cause.

Tools for this step: Mistake log (find the cause) · Topic prioritiser (rank topics)

What should I do first after failing an exam?

  1. Give yourself a day.
  2. Read the feedback and, if your university allows, request your script.
  3. Check your regulations: when the resit is, whether the mark is capped, and whether compensation applies (see resits and compensation).
  4. Talk to your personal tutor: they've seen this many times.

Key takeaway: Read the feedback before you plan: the fix depends on the cause.

Was it content, technique or time?

Content gaps show up as blank or wrong answers on whole topics. Technique shows up as dropped marks on questions you knew (misread command words, no evaluation). Time shows up as unfinished questions. Log each lost mark in the mistake log to see the split.

How do I plan the resit?

Rank topics with the topic prioritiser, plan hours with the exam countdown, and switch from re-reading to active recall and past papers.

Test yourself: You lost most marks on questions you knew. Content or technique? Reveal

Technique: the fix is reading command words, planning answers and timing, not more content.

Where can I get support?

Your personal tutor, your university's academic skills team and its wellbeing service. If something outside your control affected the exam, ask about extenuating (mitigating) circumstances: deadlines for claims are usually strict.

Common pitfalls

  • More hours of the same method that failed.
  • Missing the extenuating-circumstances deadline.

Questions

Can I resit a failed exam at university?

Usually yes, at least once. Rules on timing, capped marks and how many attempts you get vary by university: check your academic regulations or ask your tutor.

Study methods compared: which fits your workload?

By the Gradify team · Updated for the 2026–27 academic year

The two methods with the strongest evidence are active recall (testing yourself) and spaced repetition (spreading reviews out). Blurting, flashcards and the Leitner system are ways of doing active recall; Pomodoro and time-blocking are ways of organising your time. Use one of each.

Core rule: Pick one recall method and one time method: they do different jobs.

Tools for this step: Study timer (try Pomodoro) · Spaced repetition schedule (try spacing)

Can I combine study methods?

Yes, and you should: for example Pomodoro blocks, each spent on blurting a topic then checking it with flashcards that are scheduled by spaced repetition.

Test yourself: Is Pomodoro an alternative to active recall? Reveal

No: Pomodoro organises time; active recall is what you do inside the time. Use both.

Common pitfalls

  • Treating a time method as a learning method.
  • Switching methods every week.

Questions

What is the most effective study method?

Practice testing (active recall) and distributed practice (spaced repetition) have the strongest research support. Other methods are ways of doing or scheduling those two.

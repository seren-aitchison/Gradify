How to revise in two weeks

By the Gradify team · Based on the learning research named in the text

Triage first: list every topic, rank them by how likely they are to come up and how weak you are, and cut what you can't cover. Then spend each day on active recall of the top topics and finish with timed past papers.

Try it on your own notes: Gradify turns a lecture PDF into checked flashcards, free →

Day 1: triage

Get the syllabus and three past papers. List topics, mark each red, amber or green, and note which come up most. You won't cover everything; decide now what you'll skip.

Days 2 to 9: recall, not notes

Don't rewrite notes. Turn the existing ones into flashcards fast (upload the PDFs), then test yourself: new red topics in the morning, reviews of earlier days in the afternoon.

Days 10 to 13: past papers

A timed paper a day, marked the same day, with every mistake made into a card.

Day 14: light review and sleep

Review weak cards only. Go to bed at a normal time.

How many hours do you actually have?

Check with the exam countdown: at 5 hours a day with one rest day, two weeks is about 60 hours. That's enough for a lot, if every hour is recall.

Questions

Is two weeks enough to revise for an exam?

For most single exams, yes, if you prioritise the most likely topics and spend the time testing yourself rather than re-reading or rewriting notes.

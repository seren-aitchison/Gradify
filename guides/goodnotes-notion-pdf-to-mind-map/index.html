Turn GoodNotes or Notion notes into flashcards and mind maps

By the Gradify team · Updated for the 2026–27 academic year

Export your notes (GoodNotes: Share → Export → PDF; Notion: ⋯ → Export → Markdown or PDF), then upload the file to Gradify's Notes Studio. You get flashcards linked to the lines they came from and an editable mind map, free.

Core rule: Typed or converted text works best: PDFs need selectable text.

Tools for this step: Reading time calculator (size the reading first)

How do I export from GoodNotes?

Open the notebook, tap Share (or Export) and choose PDF. Handwriting needs to be converted to text first: use Gradify's Handwriting to text tool in the app, or GoodNotes' own conversion.

Key takeaway: Typed or converted text works best: PDFs need selectable text.

How do I export from Notion?

Open the page, click ⋯ → Export, and choose Markdown & CSV or PDF. Markdown keeps headings, which helps the mind map.

Then what?

Upload to Notes Studio, check the cards marked to check, and start reviewing. See lecture notes to mind map.

Test yourself: Why do headings in your notes help? Reveal

They become the branches of the mind map and help group cards by topic.

Common pitfalls

  • Uploading handwriting that hasn't been converted to text.
  • Notes with no headings, so no structure.

Related comparisons: Essay outline vs mind map

Questions

Can I make flashcards from handwritten notes?

Yes, once the handwriting is converted to text. Gradify's Handwriting to text tool or your notes app's conversion can do it.

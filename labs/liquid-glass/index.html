What each renderer is

CSS: backdrop blur and saturation, a tint, a 1.5px conic rim whose bright point faces the light, a light-positioned specular, inner light and shadow. Works everywhere with backdrop-filter.

SVG refraction: CSS plus a displacement map made for each surface's exact size and corner radius — a convex bevel that bends the background at the edge — run three times at slightly different strengths for red, green and blue. Chromium only; other browsers fall back to CSS.

WebGL shader: the background becomes a texture and a single fragment shader draws all four surfaces with true refraction through a circular bevel, dispersion, Fresnel, a specular highlight and a contact shadow. Redraws only while something moves; pixel ratio capped at 1.5.

Gizmo AI alternatives

Gizmo turns notes into AI quizzes with a game-style 'lives' system on its free plan, and sells an unlimited plan. If the lives limit gets in the way, alternatives include Anki (no limits, you make the cards), Knowt (free Learn mode) and Gradify (free, cards checked against your notes).

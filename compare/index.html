Compare study tools

Honest comparisons, including where the other tool is better.

  • Gradify vs QuizletUse Quizlet if you want ready-made sets other students have shared, or a class set your teacher made.
  • Gradify vs AnkiAnki is the better choice if you want total control, offline use, add-ons and the huge shared decks used in medicine and languages.
  • Best free Quizlet alternativesThe best free Quizlet alternatives are Anki (most powerful, free on desktop and Android), Gradify (flashcards made from and checked against your own notes) and RemNote (notes that become cards).
  • Free Quizlet Plus alternativesQuizlet's free plan limits some study modes, and Quizlet Plus (and Plus Unlimited) are paid.
  • Knowt alternativesKnowt's free plan covers flashcards, Learn mode, spaced repetition and Quizlet import, with AI features capped and a paid plan for more.
  • Gizmo AI alternativesGizmo turns notes into AI quizzes with a game-style 'lives' system on its free plan, and sells an unlimited plan.
  • TurboLearn and StudyFetch alternativesTurboLearn and StudyFetch both turn lectures and PDFs into notes, flashcards and quizzes, with free plans that limit uploads or AI use each month and paid plans for more.
  • The 7 best study tools for university studentsAnki, Quizlet, Notion, RemNote, Forest, NotebookLM and Gradify.

What do I need on my final exam?

Enter your current grade, the final's weight and the grade you want. The mark you need is (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight.

Best the week before a final

Worked example

You have 65% going into a final worth 40%, and you want 70% overall. (70 − 65 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (70 − 39) ÷ 0.4 = 77.5%.

Got a high number? Plan the revision, not the panic

The fastest way to raise an exam mark is to test yourself rather than re-read. Turn your lecture notes into flashcards, revise them with spaced repetition, and do past papers under timed conditions in the final week. Our active recall guide shows how.

How do I read the result?

  1. The number is the exam mark you need for your target overall grade.
  2. Over 100% means the target can't be reached through the final alone.
  3. Under 0% means you've already reached it: protect it with spaced review.

Questions

How do I calculate what I need on my final?

Subtract your current grade multiplied by the share of the course already done from your target, then divide by the final's weight. With 65% so far, a 40% final and a 70% target: (70 − 65 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = 77.5%.

What if I need more than 100%?

Then the target can't be reached through the final alone. Ask whether any coursework can still be resubmitted or improved, and aim for the highest grade that is reachable.

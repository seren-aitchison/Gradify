Citation and reference formatter

Choose the style and the type of source, then fill in the details. You get the reference-list entry and the in-text citation. Styles differ slightly between universities, so check your department's guide for punctuation before submitting.

Best while writing up references

Worked example

Roediger and Karpicke's 2006 testing-effect paper in Harvard: Roediger, H. L. and Karpicke, J. D. (2006) 'Test-enhanced learning: taking memory tests improves long-term retention', Psychological Science, 17(3), pp. 249-255. In the text: (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006).

How do I read the result?

  1. The first line goes in your reference list.
  2. The in-text form goes in the sentence.
  3. Check italics and punctuation against your department's guide.

Questions

What's the difference between Harvard and APA?

Both are author-date styles. APA puts the year in brackets after the authors and uses an ampersand (&) in brackets; Harvard (Cite Them Right) uses 'and', single quotation marks for article titles and 'Available at:' before links.

Which referencing style should I use?

The one your department or module handbook specifies. Medicine often uses Vancouver, engineering IEEE, psychology APA and many UK subjects Harvard.

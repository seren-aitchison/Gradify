Exam stress calm reset

Press start and breathe with the circle for one minute: in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. A longer out-breath slows your heart rate. Then don't 'revise the module': pick one 10-minute task from the list below, which is easier to start.

Best when you're frozen and can't start

Ready

Then pick just one 10-minute task

  • Do a topic's 3-question quiz
  • Run one 10-minute timer on a single flashcard deck
  • Tick off one item on the final-48-hours checklist
  • Log one question you got wrong and the right rule

Worked example

Four slow breaths a minute (in 4, hold 4, out 6 is 14 seconds a breath) is close to the slow-breathing rate used in many relaxation techniques. It won't remove exam nerves, which are normal, but it can take the edge off enough to start.

How do I read the result?

  1. Follow the circle: in 4, hold 4, out 6.
  2. After a minute, pick one 10-minute task.
  3. If anxiety stops you functioning, talk to wellbeing services.

Questions

How can I calm down before an exam?

Slow your breathing with a longer out-breath, then start with one small, concrete task. If anxiety is stopping you functioning, talk to your university's wellbeing service or your GP.

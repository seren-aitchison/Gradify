Topic prioritiser

List your topics with an exam weight (1 to 5) and your confidence (1 to 5). Topics worth a lot that you're shaky on go first; anything you're unsure of comes next; topics you're confident in get a quick polish at the end.

Best when you don't know where to start

Worked example

Cardiac cycle (weight 5, confidence 2) and Renal physiology (4, 3) are urgent and high-yield; Cell signalling (2, 2) builds confidence next; Homeostasis (3, 5) only needs a polish.

Who it's for

Best when you don't know where to start. Free, no account, and nothing you type leaves your browser: it's saved on this device so it's here next time.

Questions

What should I revise first?

The topics that are worth the most marks and that you're least confident in. They give the biggest gain per hour.

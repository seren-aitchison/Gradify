Cram planner (revision panic mode)

Enter how many hours are left until your exam and list your topics. The cram planner takes off sleep and breaks, then splits the time you really have into four blocks: high-yield core concepts (40%), past-paper gaps (30%), rapid active recall (20%) and a final light review (10%).

Best when the exam is 1 to 3 days away

Worked example

40 hours before a chemistry exam with five topics: two nights of sleep (16 h) leaves 24 waking hours, of which about 14.4 are realistic study (60%). That's 5.8 h on high-yield concepts, 4.3 h on past-paper gaps, 2.9 h of rapid recall and 1.4 h of light review, about 70 minutes of core time per topic.

Who it's for

Best when the exam is 1 to 3 days away. Free, no account, and nothing you type leaves your browser: it's saved on this device so it's here next time.

Questions

Can you revise for an exam in 2 days?

You can make a real difference in 48 hours if you triage: spend most of the time on high-yield topics and past-paper questions, test yourself instead of re-reading, and sleep both nights, because sleep consolidates what you learned.

Should I pull an all-nighter?

No. Sleep is when the brain consolidates memories; an all-nighter trades tomorrow's recall for an extra few hours of tired reading.

Science unit converter

Choose the quantity, the units and a value. You get the converted value, the factor to multiply by, and the sanity check: converting to a smaller unit always gives a bigger number. Most unit mistakes in exams are factor-of-1,000 slips.

Best for chemistry, biology and pharmacology calculations

Worked example

25 cm³ of solution in dm³: 1 cm³ = 0.001 dm³, so 25 × 0.001 = 0.025 dm³. A titration volume left in cm³ makes a concentration 1,000 times too big.

How do I read the result?

  1. The result is the value in the new unit.
  2. The factor is what you multiply by.
  3. Smaller unit, bigger number: if not, something's wrong.

Questions

How do I convert cm³ to dm³?

Divide by 1,000: 1 dm³ = 1,000 cm³. So 250 cm³ = 0.25 dm³.

How many micrograms in a milligram?

1,000: 1 mg = 1,000 µg. Drug doses mix the two, which is why clinicians write 'micrograms' in full.

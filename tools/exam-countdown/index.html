How to use your revision hours

Divide your hours by the number of topics, then add 20% for past papers. Spread each topic over several short sessions rather than one long one: reviewing after 1, 3 and 7 days beats a single cram (see the spacing effect). The spaced repetition schedule generator gives you the dates.

How do I read the result?

  1. Days to go counts calendar days; study days take off your rest days.
  2. Revision hours is study days × hours a day: your real budget.
  3. Divide the hours by topics and keep the last week for past papers.

Questions

How many hours a day should I revise?

Most students sustain 3 to 6 focused hours a day. Beyond that, quality drops. Short blocks of 25 to 50 minutes with breaks work better than marathon sessions.

How long before an exam should I start revising?

For a university exam, start four to six weeks before, so every topic can be reviewed at least three times with gaps in between.

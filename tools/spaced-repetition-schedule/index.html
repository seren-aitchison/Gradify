Spaced repetition schedule generator

Pick the day you first learn a topic and your exam date. You get review dates at expanding gaps (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days) plus the day before the exam, as a list or a calendar file.

Best right after you learn a topic

Why these intervals?

Memory fades fast at first and then more slowly (the forgetting curve). Each successful review makes the next forgetting slower, so the gaps can grow. A fixed schedule is a good start. Gradify's own reviews go further: they use FSRS, which sets each card's next date from how well you actually remembered it.

How do I read the result?

  1. Each date is a review: test yourself first, then check.
  2. The gaps grow because each successful review slows forgetting.
  3. The day before the exam is added as a final light review.

Questions

What are the best spaced repetition intervals?

A simple, well-supported schedule is 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after first learning. Adaptive schedulers such as FSRS do better by setting each item's interval from your own recall.

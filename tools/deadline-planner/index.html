Coursework deadline planner

Enter the deadline, roughly how many hours the piece needs and how many days a week you can work on it. The planner finishes two days early (for formatting, proof-reading and references) and gives you dated milestones: research, first draft, revision, final read-through.

Best when an essay or report is due in weeks

Worked example

A 2,500-word essay needing about 24 hours, due in three weeks, worked on four days a week: 19 days to the buffer means about 11 working days at 2.2 hours each. Research done by day 4, first draft by day 12, revision by day 17, final read-through by day 19, then two clear days.

Who it's for

Best when an essay or report is due in weeks. Free, no account, and nothing you type leaves your browser: it's saved on this device so it's here next time.

Questions

How long does a 2,000-word essay take?

Most students need 15 to 25 hours in total: research and planning, a first draft, revision and proof-reading. Starting three weeks out means one or two hours a day.

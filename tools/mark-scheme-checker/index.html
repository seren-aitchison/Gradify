Mark scheme checker

Paste the mark-scheme points (one per line) and your answer. The checker highlights the points your answer mentions and the keywords you missed. It matches words, so it can't judge whether your reasoning is right: use it to spot missing points, then mark the logic yourself against the scheme.

Best when self-marking past-paper answers

Worked example

A 4-mark answer that mentions the active site changing shape but not hydrogen bonds or enzyme-substrate complexes: 1 point clearly hit, the others missing their key words. Adding 'hydrogen bonds break' and 'fewer enzyme-substrate complexes form' is how 2 marks become 4.

How do I read the result?

  1. Each point shows whether your answer mentions its key words.
  2. Missing words often mean a missing mark.
  3. It matches words, not reasoning: check the logic yourself.

Which tool? Use this after writing an answer; use the question deconstructor before. Question deconstructor →

Who it's for

Best when self-marking past-paper answers. Free, no account, and nothing you type leaves your browser: it's saved on this device so it's here next time.

Questions

Can a tool mark my exam answer?

Not reliably: marking needs judgement. This tool only shows which mark-scheme words your answer uses, which catches missing points quickly.

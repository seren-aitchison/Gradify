Syllabus coverage checker

Paste the specification points, one per line, and paste your notes. The checker marks each point as covered or missing, and lists the missing words. It checks whether the words appear in your notes, not whether you understand them, so treat a tick as 'I have notes on this', then test yourself.

Best a few weeks before exams

Worked example

Three biology points against two sentences of notes: DNA structure and enzyme denaturation are covered; mitosis is missing, so that's tonight's topic.

How do I read the result?

  1. A tick means the words appear in your notes, not that you know them.
  2. Crosses are points with no notes at all: start there.
  3. Missing words show what's absent.

Which tool? Use this to find what you haven't covered; use the mistake log to find why you lose marks on what you have. Mistake log →

Questions

How do I know if I've revised everything?

Go through the official specification line by line and tick each point you can explain from memory. The checker finds the points you have no notes on at all; testing yourself finds the rest.

University finals countdown

Most UK universities hold summer exams from mid-May to early June; January exams run in the first three weeks of January. The date below is an estimate: change it to your first paper from your exam board's or university's published timetable. You'll see the days left, study days after rest days and your real revision hours.

Questions

When do the 2027 exams start?

The date on this page is an estimate from the usual pattern, not the official timetable. Exam boards publish final timetables months ahead. Typical patterns: GCSEs and A levels from mid-May to late June, university summer exams mid-May to early June. Always check your own timetable.

Presentation pacing planner

Enter the topic, the time limit and your main points. You get a slide-by-slide plan with minutes and spoken words for each (at a clear 130 words a minute, so you don't rush) and three questions a tutor or examiner is likely to ask.

Best for group or assessed presentations

Worked example

A 10-minute talk with three points: 1 minute of hook, three 2.5-minute sections of about 325 spoken words each, and a 1-minute summary, leaving half a minute spare. The whole budget is 1,300 spoken words: a script longer than that will overrun.

How do I read the result?

  1. Each slide has minutes and a word count at 130 words a minute.
  2. Over the total words means you'll overrun.
  3. Rehearse the likely questions out loud.

Questions

How many words is a 10-minute presentation?

About 1,300 words at a clear speaking pace of 130 words a minute. Nervous speakers speed up, so plan for less.

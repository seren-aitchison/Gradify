Exam timetable planner

Add each exam with its date, how much it counts (1 to 5) and how hard you find it (1 to 5). Each revision day goes to the exam with the most work still needed per day left, so a heavy exam that's close gets time before a light one that's far away.

Best when 2 to 8 exams are stacked close together

Worked example

Organic (weight 5, difficulty 4) in 10 days, Physical (4, 3) in 6 days and Lab skills (2, 2) in 12 days. Organic gets day 1, then Physical takes the next five days because its exam comes first; Organic gets the four days after Physical is sat, and Lab skills the last two. Keep 15 minutes of flashcards for the others every day.

How do I read the result?

  1. "Start with" is the order the exams get their main revision days.
  2. Each day lists one main exam; keep 15 minutes of flashcards for the others.
  3. A heavy exam that's close gets days first.

Questions

How do I revise for exams close together?

Start with the exam that has the most to learn per day left, keep 15 minutes a day of flashcards for every other subject so nothing is forgotten, and switch fully once each exam is done.

Reading time calculator

Enter the number of pages (or paste the text) and how dense it is. The calculator uses typical reading speeds (about 250 words a minute for light reading, 200 for a textbook and 130 for a dense academic paper) and adds time to make notes and three short reviews.

Best when the weekly reading list looks endless

Worked example

A 24-page journal article at about 550 words a page is 13,200 words; at 130 words a minute that's about 1 h 42 min of reading, plus 51 minutes of notes and three 10-minute reviews: about 3 hours in all.

How do I read the result?

  1. Reading time is at a typical speed for that kind of text.
  2. Notes and reviews are added on top: that's the real cost.
  3. Over two hours? Turn it into a study pack once you've read it.

Questions

How long does it take to read 30 pages?

About an hour for light reading, an hour and a half for a textbook, and two hours or more for a dense academic paper, before making notes.

Viva and project defence prep

Enter your project title, main finding and biggest limitation. You get nine practice questions across methodology and evidence, limitations and edge cases, and next steps and impact: the three areas examiners probe in almost every viva or project defence.

Best before a viva, poster or project check-in

Worked example

For a study where students opted in to quizzes, expect: 'How much does self-selection weaken your 8-point effect?', 'What else did you consider instead of an opt-in design?' and 'What's the single most important follow-up?'

How do I read the result?

  1. Questions are grouped the way examiners probe: method, limits, next steps.
  2. Answer each out loud in under a minute.
  3. The limitation questions matter most.

Questions

What questions are asked in a viva?

Why you chose your methods, how confident you are in your findings, what the limitations are and what you'd do next, plus how your work fits the key literature.

Turn your notes into a one-page revision sheet

Paste your notes and Gradify condenses them into a one-page cheat sheet of the key facts, grouped by topic. You can also print a key-terms sheet with the definitions left blank, so you can test yourself instead of re-reading.

Make a revision sheet → Free · no account needed to try it

How it works

  1. Paste your notes. Any format: lecture notes, textbook summaries, your own shorthand.
  2. Get the sheet. The key facts are grouped under topic headings, on one page.
  3. Print a test version. A key-terms sheet with blank definitions turns the sheet into a quiz.
  4. Revise from it. Make flashcards from the same notes and let spaced repetition pace them.

Before and after

Before
  • 60 pages of lecture PDFs
  • AI summaries you can't check
  • Re-reading and highlighting
After
  • Flashcards, each linked to the line it came from
  • A mind map of the topic
  • A 60-second recall drill and reviews timed to your exam

Why it's different

Condensing notes forces you to decide what matters, and a blank-definitions sheet turns reading into active recall. Use it for checking your own understanding. Follow your exam's rules on what you may bring in.

Built for your course

Works for GCSE, A level (AQA, OCR, Edexcel), IB and university modules, from first-year biochemistry to postgraduate law. Gradify also has free courses in 30+ subjects if you want ready-made lessons: explore them.

Questions

How do I make a good revision sheet?

Keep to one page per topic, group facts under headings, write definitions in your own words, and test yourself by covering the answers.

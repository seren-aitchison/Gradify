Golgi apparatus

The Golgi apparatus is the cell's processing and dispatch centre. It receives proteins and lipids from the ER, modifies them, sorts them by destination and sends them on in vesicles.

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Describe the structure of the Golgi apparatus
  • Explain how it modifies and sorts proteins
  • Explain how lysosomal enzymes are targeted to lysosomes

Before you start

  • Endoplasmic reticulum

Structure

The Golgi is a stack of flattened membrane sacs (cisternae) with two faces: the cis face, which receives vesicles from the ER, and the trans face, from which vesicles leave. Proteins pass through the stack from cis to trans, meeting different enzymes on the way.

Modification

Golgi enzymes trim and add sugars to proteins (continuing glycosylation begun in the ER), add phosphate or sulfate groups, and in some cases cut proteins into their active forms. Plant Golgi also makes non-cellulose polysaccharides for the cell wall.

Sorting and dispatch

  • Secretory vesicles carry products such as hormones and enzymes to the plasma membrane for exocytosis, either continuously or when signalled.
  • Membrane proteins are delivered to the plasma membrane.
  • Lysosomal enzymes are tagged with mannose-6-phosphate, recognised by receptors in the trans Golgi that send them to lysosomes.

In the genetic disorder I-cell disease, the tagging enzyme is missing, so lysosomal enzymes are secreted instead of delivered, and undigested material builds up in the lysosomes.

Investigate with the lab. Find the Golgi apparatus, then the rough endoplasmic reticulum, and trace the route a protein takes between them.

The Golgi modifies proteins and lipids as they pass from its cis to its trans face, and sorts them into vesicles by destination.

Common mistakeThinking the Golgi makes proteins. It receives proteins made on the rough ER and finishes, sorts and ships them.

Summary

  • The Golgi is a stack of cisternae with a receiving cis face and a shipping trans face.
  • It modifies proteins, for example by glycosylation.
  • It sorts products into vesicles for secretion, the membrane or lysosomes.

Key terms

Golgi apparatus
A stack of membrane sacs that modifies, sorts and packages proteins and lipids
Glycosylation
Adding sugar chains to proteins or lipids
Exocytosis
Release of a vesicle's contents by fusion of the vesicle with the plasma membrane

Flashcards

Answer from memory, then open the card to check.

What are the cis and trans faces of the Golgi?

The cis face receives vesicles from the ER; the trans face sends vesicles on to their destinations.

How are enzymes directed to lysosomes?

They are tagged with mannose-6-phosphate in the Golgi, and receptors for this tag send them to lysosomes.

Check your understanding

  1. What is the main function of the Golgi apparatus?
    • Making ATP
    • Modifying, sorting and packaging proteins and lipids
    • Storing DNA
    • Translating mRNA
    Show the answer

    Modifying, sorting and packaging proteins and lipids. The Golgi processes molecules made in the ER and sends them to the right place.

  2. Radioactive amino acids are given to a pancreatic cell. In which order would the label appear?
    • Golgi, then nucleus, then ER
    • Lysosomes, then ER, then Golgi
    • Mitochondria, then Golgi, then nucleus
    • Rough ER, then Golgi, then secretory vesicles
    Show the answer

    Rough ER, then Golgi, then secretory vesicles. This classic pulse-chase experiment by Palade traced proteins from the ER through the Golgi to secretion.

  3. In I-cell disease, lysosomal enzymes are found in the blood, while patients' lysosomes fill with undigested material. What is the underlying fault?
    • The cells have no Golgi apparatus
    • The enzymes are not tagged with mannose-6-phosphate, so they are secreted instead of sent to lysosomes
    • The ER makes no proteins
    • The lysosomes have no membrane
    Show the answer

    The enzymes are not tagged with mannose-6-phosphate, so they are secreted instead of sent to lysosomes. Without the sorting tag, the default route out of the Golgi is secretion.

  4. What does the Golgi apparatus do?
    • Makes proteins by joining amino acids together
    • Modifies, packages and sends proteins to their destinations
    • Releases energy from glucose by respiration
    • Stores the cell's genetic information safely
    Show the answer

    Modifies, packages and sends proteins to their destinations. Proteins from the rough ER are modified and packaged in the Golgi apparatus.

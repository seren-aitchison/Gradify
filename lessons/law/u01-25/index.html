Legal aid

Legal aid is public funding for legal advice and representation for people who cannot afford it. In England and Wales it is governed by the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (LASPO) and administered by the Legal Aid Agency.

Civil legal aid

  • Available only for matters in Schedule 1 to LASPO: for example, some family cases (especially involving domestic abuse or child protection), housing where a home is at risk, debt where a home is at risk, some immigration and asylum, mental health, community care, discrimination and judicial review.
  • Most personal injury, clinical negligence (except some birth injury), contract, employment and private family disputes (without evidence of abuse) are out of scope.
  • Exceptional case funding (s 10) is available where refusing it would breach Convention rights.
  • Usually subject to a means test and a merits test.

Criminal legal aid

  • Advice at the police station: free to anyone detained, whatever their means.
  • Representation: subject to the interests of justice test (for example, risk to liberty, a complex point of law, inability to understand proceedings) and, in most cases, a means test. In the Crown Court defendants may pay contributions.

Debates

LASPO cut civil legal aid sharply. Critics point to more litigants in person, advice deserts and pressure on the courts; supporters point to cost control. Fee levels for criminal legal aid lawyers have been a recurring dispute.

Key idea

Police station advice is free and non-means-tested. Make sure detained clients know that.

Summary

  • LASPO 2012 removed many civil matters from scope.
  • Criminal legal aid is subject to an interests of justice test and means testing.

Flashcards

Answer from memory, then open the card to check.

Which Act governs legal aid in England and Wales?

The Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 (LASPO).

What is the interests of justice test?

The test for criminal legal aid representation: whether, for example, the defendant risks losing liberty, the case raises a substantial point of law, or the defendant cannot follow the proceedings.

Define legal aid.

Public funding for legal advice and representation for people who cannot afford it.

Define a means test.

An assessment of financial eligibility for legal aid.

Which part of LASPO sets out the civil matters within scope?

Schedule 1.

Name four civil matters within scope of legal aid.

Any four of: some family cases involving abuse or child protection, housing or debt where a home is at risk, some immigration and asylum, mental health, community care, discrimination, judicial review.

Check your understanding

  1. Which is free to everyone, regardless of means?
    • Advice on personal injury claims
    • Civil legal aid for contract disputes
    • Legal advice at the police station
    • Private family law advice
    • Representation in the Crown Court
    Show the answer

    Legal advice at the police station. Police station advice is non-means-tested.

  2. A client wants legal aid to sue a builder for breach of contract. What is the best advice?
    • Contract claims are generally outside the scope of LASPO
    • Legal aid covers any civil claim with good merits
    • They qualify if the claim is under £10,000
    • They qualify if they are on means-tested benefits
    • They qualify through exceptional case funding automatically
    Show the answer

    Contract claims are generally outside the scope of LASPO. Contract disputes are not in Schedule 1 to LASPO; other funding is needed.

  3. A tenant faces eviction because of rent arrears, and the landlord's claim is due to be heard next week. Which statement about legal aid is most accurate?
    • Civil legal aid may be available because her home is at risk, subject to means and merits
    • Legal aid is available without a means test because the hearing is urgent
    • Legal aid is only available if she brings a counterclaim
    • No legal aid is available for any housing matter after LASPO
    • Only criminal legal aid can help because arrears are an offence
    Show the answer

    Civil legal aid may be available because her home is at risk, subject to means and merits. Housing matters where the home is at risk remain in scope of LASPO, Schedule 1, subject to the tests (and the Housing Loss Prevention Advice Service offers advice at possession hearings).

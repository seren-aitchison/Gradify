Avogadro's constant

The Avogadro constant, 6.022 140 76 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, connects the atomic scale with the laboratory scale. This lesson looks at how big it is, how it was measured, and how it became a fixed defining constant of the SI.

By the end of this lesson you can

  • State the value and units of the Avogadro constant
  • Use it to find the mass of single atoms and numbers of particles
  • Describe how the constant was determined and why it is now fixed

Before you start

  • The mole

Value and units

NA = 6.022 140 76 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, exactly, by definition since 20 May 2019. It is a number per mole. The related Avogadro number is the same value without units.

How big?

A mole of sand grains would cover the UK to a depth of many kilometres. A mole of water is only 18 g, about a tablespoonful, which shows how small molecules are.

Worked example

Find the mass of one carbon-12 atom.

  1. Molar mass = 12 g/mol, so one atom has a mass of 12 ÷ (6.022 × 10²³) = 1.993 × 10⁻²³ g.

Measuring it

Early estimates came from the kinetic theory of gases (Loschmidt, 1865) and from Perrin's studies of Brownian motion (1908), which helped convince sceptics that atoms were real. The modern value came from counting the atoms in an almost perfect sphere of silicon-28: measuring its volume and the spacing of atoms in the crystal by X-ray diffraction. That work allowed the 2019 SI to fix NA exactly.

Key idea

Nₐ = 6.022 140 76 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, a fixed defining constant of the SI.

Common mistake
Using Nₐ to convert grams to moles. Mass converts to moles with molar mass; Nₐ converts moles to numbers of particles.

Summary

  • Nₐ links moles to numbers of particles.
  • Its exact value has defined the mole since 2019.

Key terms

Avogadro constant
The number of entities per mole, exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹
Defining constant
A physical constant given an exact value to define SI units

Flashcards

Answer from memory, then open the card to check.

What are the units of the Avogadro constant?

mol⁻¹ (per mole).

Why was the Avogadro constant given an exact value in 2019?

To define the mole independently of the kilogram and a physical artefact, so that the SI rests on fixed constants of nature.

Check your understanding

  1. What is the value of the Avogadro constant?
    • 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴ mol⁻¹
    • 3.00 × 10⁸ mol⁻¹
    • 6.022 × 10⁻²³ mol⁻¹
    • 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹
    Show the answer

    6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹. Nₐ is about 6.022 × 10²³ particles per mole.

  2. What is the approximate mass of one water molecule (M = 18.0 g/mol)?
    • 1.1 × 10²⁵ g
    • 18 g
    • 3.0 × 10⁻²³ g
    • 6.0 × 10²³ g
    Show the answer

    3.0 × 10⁻²³ g. Divide the molar mass by Nₐ: 18.0 ÷ (6.022 × 10²³) = 2.99 × 10⁻²³ g.

  3. The silicon sphere method counted atoms by measuring the sphere's volume and the volume per atom. Why was isotopically pure silicon-28 used?
    • Because it doesn't form crystals
    • Because it is the cheapest element
    • Because silicon-28 is radioactive
    • So that the molar mass was known very precisely, avoiding uncertainty from a mixture of isotopes
    Show the answer

    So that the molar mass was known very precisely, avoiding uncertainty from a mixture of isotopes. Natural silicon contains three isotopes; enriching ²⁸Si reduces the uncertainty in the average atomic mass.

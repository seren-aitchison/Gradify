Interim quizzes

Every Gradify course has short quizzes along the way, at the end of each unit, so you know where you stand before you move on.

Practise · Works on the website · Free.

Open Interim quizzes →

What it does

Unit quizzes draw on everything the unit taught. Passing one earns XP once, and your results feed the mastery view that shows which topics are secure and which are fading.

How to use it

  1. Work through a unit's lessons in any course.
  2. Take the unit quiz at the end.
  3. Go back to the lessons behind any questions you missed.

Best for

  • Checking understanding before moving on
  • Spotting weak topics early
  • Building up to the course exam

Questions

Is Interim quizzes free?

Free.

Where can I use Interim quizzes?

Interim quizzes works on the website. One Gradify account works on every Gradify site.

More practise features

  • Flashcard feedThe flashcard feed is an endless scroll of flashcards from your decks and the subjects you choose, with bonus facts and study hacks mixed in
  • Confidence ratingsAfter each flashcard you rate how well you knew it, and Gradify's spaced repetition (FSRS) brings the card back just before you'd forget it, paced to your exam date
  • Adaptive difficultyAdaptive quizzes get harder or easier as you answer: two right in a row and the questions step up, one wrong and they step down
  • Timed quiz modeTimed quiz mode gives you a quiz from any lesson, unit or deck with a fixed time to answer, so you practise against the clock like the real exam
  • Question bankThe question bank puts every practice question on Gradify in one place, with marked feedback on every answer
  • Key-term gamesKey-term games turn the vocabulary of any lesson, unit or your own flashcard deck into three quick games: Match, Speed round and Missing letters

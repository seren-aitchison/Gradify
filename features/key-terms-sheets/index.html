Key-terms sheets

Key-terms sheets give you a printable sheet of every key term in a lesson, with or without the definitions. Print the blank version to test yourself.

Learn · Works on the website and in the app · Free: use it 5 times on the free plan, then Gradify Pro (£10 a month) for unlimited use.

Open Key-terms sheets →

What it does

Each sheet is made from the lesson's own key terms. Print it with definitions to revise from, or without them and write the definitions in from memory, then check against the full sheet.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools in the Gradify app and choose Key-terms sheets.
  2. Pick the lesson.
  3. Print with or without definitions, or save as a PDF.

Best for

  • Definition-heavy subjects like biology, law and psychology
  • Blurting practice on paper
  • A quick check the night before

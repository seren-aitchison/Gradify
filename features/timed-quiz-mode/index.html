Timed quiz mode

Timed quiz mode gives you a quiz from any lesson, unit or deck with a fixed time to answer, so you practise against the clock like the real exam. It's part of Gradify Pro.

Practise · Works in the Gradify app · Part of Gradify Pro (£10 a month).

Open Timed quiz mode →

What it does

Knowing an answer and producing it under time pressure are different skills. Timed mode builds a quiz from any lesson, unit or deck and gives you a fixed time, so you practise pacing as well as recall.

How to use it

  1. Open Quizzes in the Gradify app and choose Timed.
  2. Pick what to be tested on.
  3. Answer before the time runs out, then review what you missed.

Best for

  • Running out of time in exams
  • The last weeks before an exam
  • Building calm under pressure

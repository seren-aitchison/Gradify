Notes Studio

Notes Studio turns a PDF of your notes, or notes you paste in, into flashcards, a mind map and a cheat sheet. Every card links to the line in your notes it came from, and cards that don't fully match are marked to check.

Notes and creation · Works on the website and in the app · Free, with no account needed to try it: 5 flashcard sets and 5 mind maps on the free plan, then Gradify Pro (£10 a month) for unlimited.

Open Notes Studio →

What it does

Upload a PDF with selectable text, a .txt or .md file, or paste your notes. Notes Studio works through them and makes a study pack you can revise straight away with spaced repetition. Because each card shows its source line, you can see why it says what it says and fix anything before you revise. You can try it with no account; your first pack is saved on your device.

How to use it

  1. Open the Gradify app and choose New, or go straight to Notes Studio.
  2. Upload a PDF or paste one lecture or topic of notes.
  3. Check any cards marked to check, then start revising.

Best for

  • Turning a week of lectures into revision in minutes
  • Students who don't trust AI-made cards: every card shows its source
  • Revising from your own course's notes, not someone else's

Bonus facts and boosting

As you revise in the flashcard feed, bonus facts appear between your cards, and any card you miss can be boosted so you see it more often.

Learn · Works in the Gradify app · Free.

Open Bonus facts and boosting →

What it does

Bonus facts are short, related facts that widen what you know about a topic without becoming another card to review. Boosting is your own control on top of spaced repetition: if a card keeps catching you out, boost it and the feed shows it more.

How to use it

  1. Open the flashcard feed in the Gradify app.
  2. Scroll through your cards; read the bonus facts as they come up.
  3. Boost any card you want to see more.

Best for

  • Keeping a long revision session varied
  • Cards you keep getting wrong
  • Curious learners who like the extra detail

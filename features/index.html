Every Gradify feature

Gradify is one account across 31 learning sites, and these are the features that work on all of them. Most are free; the few that need Gradify Pro say so.

Learn

  • Online courses Gradify has free online courses on every one of its subject sites, from GCSE core and primary to medicine, law and physics.
  • Explainer videos Gradify turns any lesson, unit or your own notes into a short narrated explainer video, one scene per section.
  • Tutorials and simulations Gradify's courses include interactive tutorials and simulations you control, from a beating heart and protein structures to circuits and markets.
  • Chant podcasts Chant podcasts turn every unit of a Gradify course into an episode of short, repeated chants of its key facts, played back to back.
  • Audio questions Audio questions reads a question aloud, pauses so you can answer out loud, then reads the answer.
  • Story posters Story posters tell a topic as a picture story, one panel at a time.
  • Dictionaries and thesauruses Gradify's subject dictionaries define every key term used in its lessons, organised by subject.
  • Key-terms sheets Key-terms sheets give you a printable sheet of every key term in a lesson, with or without the definitions.
  • Bonus facts and boosting As you revise in the flashcard feed, bonus facts appear between your cards, and any card you miss can be boosted so you see it more often.
  • 3D visuals on demand Visualise lets you ask to see a concept, such as benzene, an electric field or the heart, and shows the best visual Gradify already has: a 3D molecule, a labelled 3D model, a course simulation or a diagram computed from the physics.

Notes and creation

  • Notes Studio Notes Studio turns a PDF of your notes, or notes you paste in, into flashcards, a mind map and a cheat sheet.
  • Notebook Gradify's Notebook keeps your notes as linked pages, like RemNote or Notion, and any line can become a flashcard.
  • Handwriting to text Handwriting to text turns writing on a phone or tablet, or a photo of a handwritten page, into searchable text.
  • Notes to songs Paste notes in any format and Gradify turns them into a chant to say or sing along to, as well as flashcards, a mind map and a cheat sheet.
  • YouTube to notes Paste a YouTube link and Gradify makes notes in chapters, flashcards and a mind map from the video, plus a chat that answers only from what's said in it.
  • Spidermaps Gradify's mind map creator builds a spidermap from facts you pick from any lesson, which you can then move, rename and add your own ideas to.
  • Downloadable resources Every lesson's resources can be downloaded and printed: notes, key-terms sheets, questions, posters, and flashcards ready to import into Anki.

Practise

  • Flashcard feed The flashcard feed is an endless scroll of flashcards from your decks and the subjects you choose, with bonus facts and study hacks mixed in.
  • Confidence ratings After each flashcard you rate how well you knew it, and Gradify's spaced repetition (FSRS) brings the card back just before you'd forget it, paced to your exam date.
  • Adaptive difficulty Adaptive quizzes get harder or easier as you answer: two right in a row and the questions step up, one wrong and they step down. (Gradify Pro)
  • Timed quiz mode Timed quiz mode gives you a quiz from any lesson, unit or deck with a fixed time to answer, so you practise against the clock like the real exam. (Gradify Pro)
  • Question bank The question bank puts every practice question on Gradify in one place, with marked feedback on every answer. (Gradify Pro)
  • Key-term games Key-term games turn the vocabulary of any lesson, unit or your own flashcard deck into three quick games: Match, Speed round and Missing letters.
  • Interim quizzes Every Gradify course has short quizzes along the way, at the end of each unit, so you know where you stand before you move on.

Progress and rewards

  • Gradify Tree The Gradify Tree grows as you learn, with a branch for every subject you study, so you can see your progress at a glance.
  • Heatmap The heatmap shows every day you've learned across every Gradify site, in one calendar grid, so gaps and streaks are easy to see.
  • XP and levels You earn XP for what you learn on Gradify and level up as it adds up.
  • Streaks Gradify counts two kinds of streak: a daily streak for every day in a row you learn something, and a streak of correct answers in a row.
  • Challenges Gradify sets daily and weekly challenges across your sites, such as a number of questions or lessons, for XP and rewards.
  • Reward scheme Gradify rewards long-term milestones, such as answering 1,000 questions or keeping a 100-day streak, and each one is earned once and kept.
  • Projects for XP Projects for XP are real projects to complete step by step, such as teaching a topic in a 3-minute video.
  • Smart reminders Smart reminders know your deadlines and your streak, and remind you when it's time to revise. (Gradify Pro)
  • Time blocking Plan your week in time blocks on a calendar, and let Gradify auto-plan your revision into your free time from your goals, fading topics and deadlines.

People

  • Gradify AI Gradify AI will be an AI chat in the corner of every page that knows your progress on every Gradify site. (coming soon)
  • Tutors In Gradify's community, learners offer to tutor in subjects they know well, and you can ask one for help inside Gradify.
  • Student mentors Student mentors are students a year or two ahead of you who offer advice, on subjects, applications and what comes next, inside Gradify.
  • Study groups Gradify study groups are private groups with a join code, a chat, members and a weekly leaderboard, for revising together online.
  • Friends and school leaderboards Follow your friends with their friend code and compare progress on friends' and school leaderboards in Gradify.
  • Community chats Gradify has a chat for every subject and site, where learners can ask and answer each other's questions about what they're studying.
  • InternshipGradify InternshipGradify lists well-known internship, placement and work experience schemes in the UK, matched to the subjects you study.

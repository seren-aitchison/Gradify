YouTube to notes

Paste a YouTube link and Gradify makes notes in chapters, flashcards and a mind map from the video, plus a chat that answers only from what's said in it. You can paste the transcript instead.

The full guide, with examples: YouTube to flashcards.

Notes and creation · Works on the website · Free: use it 5 times on the free plan, then Gradify Pro (£10 a month) for unlimited use.

Open YouTube to notes →

What it does

Gradify reads the video's captions, splits them into chapters and makes notes, cards and a map from them in your browser. The chat about the video answers only from the transcript, with the moment each point is made, so it never adds anything the video didn't say.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools in the Gradify app and choose YouTube to notes.
  2. Paste the video link, or its transcript.
  3. Read the chapter notes, then revise the flashcards it made.

Best for

  • Lecture recordings and revision channels
  • Long videos you need the key points from
  • Turning watching into active recall

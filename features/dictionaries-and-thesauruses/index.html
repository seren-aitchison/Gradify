Dictionaries and thesauruses

Gradify's subject dictionaries define every key term used in its lessons, organised by subject. A thesaurus helps you find the right word when you write.

Learn · Works on the website · Free.

Open Dictionaries and thesauruses →

What it does

Every term is defined in the words the lesson uses, so a definition in the dictionary matches what you'll be marked against in that course. Look a term up, see which subject it belongs to, and open the lesson it comes from.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools in the Gradify app and choose Dictionaries and thesaurus.
  2. Pick a subject, or search for a term.
  3. Read the definition, and open its lesson for the full explanation.

Best for

  • Learning the vocabulary of a new subject
  • Definition questions
  • Essay writing, with the thesaurus

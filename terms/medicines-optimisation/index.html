What is medicines optimisation?

Medicines optimisation: A person-centred approach to safe and effective use of medicines

It comes up in 3 Gradify lessons across 2 subjects (Nursing, Pharmacy), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Medicines optimisation in Nursing

A person-centred approach to safe and effective use of medicines

  • Pharmacology and Medicines Management: Medicines optimisation
What NICE guideline covers medicines optimisation?

NICE NG5, Medicines optimisation (2015).

Medicines optimisation in Pharmacy

A person-centred approach to safe and effective medicines use, so people get the best outcomes

  • Foundations of Pharmacy Practice: Introduction to pharmacy
  • Clinical Pharmacy and Medicines Optimisation: Medicines optimisation
What is medicines optimisation?

A person-centred approach to safe and effective medicines use, to get the best outcomes.

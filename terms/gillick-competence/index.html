What is gillick competence?

Gillick competence: Ability of a child under 16 to consent to treatment they fully understand

It comes up in 7 Gradify lessons across 5 subjects (Dentistry, Health and Wellbeing, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Gillick competence in Dentistry

Ability of a child under 16 to consent to treatment they fully understand

  • Restorative Dentistry: Operative, Endodontics and Prosthodontics: Consent and shared decision-making in treatment

Gillick competence in Health and Wellbeing

A child under 16 with enough understanding to consent to their own treatment

  • Health and Social Care Systems: Consent and mental capacity
  • Health Across the Life Course: Adolescent health
What is Gillick competence?

A child under 16 having enough understanding and intelligence to consent to their own treatment.

Gillick competence in Medicine

The capacity of an under-16 to consent if they understand fully what is proposed

  • Neurology and Psychiatry: Psychiatry of childhood and adolescence
  • Professional Practice, Prescribing and Population Health: Consent and capacity
What is Gillick competence?

The capacity of an under-16 to consent to treatment if they understand fully what is proposed.

Gillick competence in Nursing

Sufficient understanding and intelligence for a child under 16 to consent to a proposed treatment

  • Nursing Law, Ethics and Safeguarding: Consent
What is Gillick competence?

Sufficient understanding and intelligence for a child under 16 to consent to a proposed treatment.

Gillick competence in Pharmacy

A child under 16's capacity to consent to their own treatment if they have sufficient understanding and intelligence

  • Foundations of Pharmacy Practice: Consent in pharmacy practice
Define Gillick competence.

A child under 16's capacity to consent to their own treatment if they have sufficient understanding and intelligence.

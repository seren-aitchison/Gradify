What is internal rate of return?

Internal rate of return: The discount rate at which NPV is zero

It comes up in 4 Gradify lessons across 4 subjects (Business and Management, Engineering, Accounting and Finance, Mathematics), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Internal rate of return in Business and Management

The discount rate at which NPV is zero

  • Finance for Managers: Net present value

Internal rate of return in Engineering

The discount rate at which a project's NPV is zero

  • Projects and Professional Practice: Engineering economics

Internal rate of return in Accounting and Finance

The discount rate at which a project's NPV is zero

  • Financial Management: Internal rate of return
What is the internal rate of return?

The discount rate at which a project's NPV is zero.

Internal rate of return in Mathematics

The rate r* with Σ Ct/(1 + r*)ᵗ = 0

  • Financial Mathematics and Economics: Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
What is the internal rate of return?

The discount rate r* that makes a project's NPV zero.

What is Eatwell Guide?

Eatwell Guide: The UK's visual model of a healthy, balanced diet for people aged 2 and over

It comes up in 4 Gradify lessons across 4 subjects (Health and Wellbeing, Nursing, Primary, Sport and Exercise Science), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Eatwell Guide in Health and Wellbeing

The UK's visual model of a healthy, balanced diet for people aged 2 and over

  • Nutrition: The Eatwell Guide
How often should people eat fish according to the Eatwell Guide?

At least twice a week, including one portion of oily fish.

Eatwell Guide in Nursing

The UK's visual guide to the proportions of food groups in a healthy, balanced diet

  • Community, Public Health and Health Promotion: Nutrition
What is the Eatwell Guide?

The UK's visual guide to the proportions of food groups in a healthy, balanced diet.

Eatwell Guide in Primary

The UK government guide showing the proportions of food groups for a healthy diet

  • Plants, Animals and Habitats: Healthy living

Eatwell Guide in Sport and Exercise Science

The UK's visual model of a healthy, balanced diet for people aged 2 and over

  • Sport and Exercise Nutrition: The Eatwell Guide
How often should people eat fish according to the Eatwell Guide?

At least twice a week, including one portion of oily fish.

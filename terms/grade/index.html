What is grade?

Grade: The rate of progression and risk, A to C, based on bone loss for age and modified by smoking and diabetes

It comes up in 6 Gradify lessons across 6 subjects (Dentistry, GCSE Core, Health and Wellbeing, Medicine, Nursing, Veterinary Science), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Grade in Dentistry

The rate of progression and risk, A to C, based on bone loss for age and modified by smoking and diabetes

  • Periodontology: Staging and grading periodontitis
How is grade estimated in the BSP implementation?

Percentage bone loss at the worst site divided by age: under 0.5 grade A, 0.5–1.0 grade B, over 1.0 grade C.

Grade in GCSE Core

A system for rating the certainty of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low

  • Foundations of Health and Wellbeing: Levels of evidence
What are the four GRADE levels of certainty?

High, moderate, low and very low.

Grade in Health and Wellbeing

A system for rating the certainty of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low

  • Foundations of Health and Wellbeing: Levels of evidence
What are the four GRADE levels of certainty?

High, moderate, low and very low.

Grade in Medicine

A system for rating the certainty of evidence and the strength of recommendations

  • Professional Practice, Prescribing and Population Health: Evidence-based medicine

Grade in Nursing

A system for rating the certainty of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low

  • Research, Evidence-Based Practice and Nursing Leadership: Systematic reviews
What does GRADE rate?

The certainty of evidence for each outcome, as high, moderate, low or very low.

Grade in Veterinary Science

A measure of how abnormal tumour cells look and behave

  • Veterinary Pathology: Tumour classification and grading

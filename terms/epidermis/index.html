What is epidermis?

Epidermis: The outer layer of the skin, forming a waterproof barrier of keratinised cells

It comes up in 3 Gradify lessons across 3 subjects (GCSE Core, Health and Wellbeing, Nursing), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Epidermis in GCSE Core

The outer layer of the skin, forming a waterproof barrier of keratinised cells

  • The Human Body: The skin

Epidermis in Health and Wellbeing

The outer layer of the skin, forming a waterproof barrier of keratinised cells

  • The Human Body: The skin

Epidermis in Nursing

The outer, avascular layer of the skin, made of keratinised stratified squamous epithelium

  • Human Anatomy and Physiology: The integumentary system

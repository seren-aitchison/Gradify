What is material risk?

Material risk: A risk that a reasonable person in the patient's position, or this patient, would likely consider significant (Montgomery)

It comes up in 9 Gradify lessons across 4 subjects (Dentistry, Medicine, Nursing, Veterinary Science), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Material risk in Dentistry

A risk that a reasonable person in the patient's position, or this patient, would likely consider significant (Montgomery)

  • Clinical Method and Reasoning: Communicating risk and shared decision-making
  • Restorative Dentistry: Operative, Endodontics and Prosthodontics: Consent and shared decision-making in treatment
  • Professionalism, Ethics and Law: Communication and consent

Material risk in Medicine

A risk that a reasonable person in the patient's position, or this patient, would likely consider significant (Montgomery)

  • Clinical Method and Reasoning: Communicating risk and shared decision-making
  • Acute, Surgical and Palliative Care: Preoperative assessment
  • Professional Practice, Prescribing and Population Health: Consent and capacity

Material risk in Nursing

A risk a reasonable person in the patient's position, or this particular patient, would consider significant

  • Clinical Practice and Care Planning: Shared decision-making
  • Nursing Law, Ethics and Safeguarding: Consent

Material risk in Veterinary Science

A risk that a reasonable person in the patient's position, or this patient, would likely consider significant (Montgomery)

  • Clinical Method and Reasoning: Communicating risk and shared decision-making

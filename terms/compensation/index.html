What is compensation?

Compensation: The secondary respiratory or renal change that moves pH back towards normal after a primary disturbance

It comes up in 7 Gradify lessons across 5 subjects (Dentistry, Mathematics, Medicine, Nursing, Veterinary Science), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Compensation in Dentistry

The secondary respiratory or renal change that moves pH back towards normal after a primary disturbance

  • Foundations of Medical Science: Acid–base physiology

Compensation in Mathematics

Rounding to an easy number then adjusting: 199 × 4 = 200 × 4 − 4

  • School and Examination Mathematics: Mental Arithmetic

Compensation in Medicine

The secondary respiratory or renal change that moves pH back towards normal after a primary disturbance

  • Foundations of Medical Science: Acid–base physiology

Compensation in Nursing

Physiological responses that keep vital functions near normal despite a disturbance

  • Human Anatomy and Physiology: Homeostasis
  • Children's and Young People's Nursing: Introduction to paediatric nursing

Compensation in Veterinary Science

The secondary change in the other system (respiratory or renal) that moves pH back towards normal

  • Veterinary Physiology: Acid–base balance
  • Veterinary Public Health and Professional Practice: Notifiable diseases and disease control

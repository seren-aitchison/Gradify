What is social loafing?

Social loafing: The tendency for individuals to exert less effort when working in a group

It comes up in 5 Gradify lessons across 4 subjects (Business and Management, Professional Skills, Psychology, Teaching), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Social loafing in Business and Management

The tendency for individuals to exert less effort when working in a group

  • Management and Organisational Behaviour: Teams and group working
Name three remedies for social loafing.

Identifying individual contributions, smaller teams and meaningful tasks.

Social loafing in Professional Skills

Reduced individual effort in groups when contributions aren't identifiable

  • Social Psychology: Group dynamics
  • Management and Organisational Behaviour: Teams and group working
How can social loafing be reduced?

Make contributions identifiable, the task meaningful and the group cohesive.

Social loafing in Psychology

Reduced individual effort in groups when contributions aren't identifiable

  • Social Psychology: Group dynamics
How can social loafing be reduced?

Make contributions identifiable, the task meaningful and the group cohesive.

Social loafing in Teaching

The tendency for individuals to exert less effort when working in a group

  • Classroom Practice: Collaborative learning and groupwork
What is social loafing?

The tendency to put in less individual effort when working in a group.

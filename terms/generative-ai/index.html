What is Generative AI?

Generative AI: AI that produces new content such as text, images or code

It comes up in 9 Gradify lessons across 8 subjects (Business and Management, Careers and Employability, Design Thinking and Creativity, Professional Skills, Research and the Dissertation, Sociology, Teaching, University Study Skills), and the meaning shifts a little with the subject.

Generative AI in Business and Management

AI that produces new content such as text, images or code

  • Digital Business and Information Systems: Artificial intelligence in business
What is generative AI?

AI that produces new content such as text, images or code, using large models trained on vast datasets.

Generative AI in Careers and Employability

AI systems that create new content such as text, images or code

  • Career Planning and Resilience: The future of work and automation

Generative AI in Design Thinking and Creativity

AI that produces new content such as text, images or code

  • Sustainable and Ethical Design: Designing with AI
What is hallucination in generative AI?

Plausible but false content produced by an AI model.

Generative AI in Professional Skills

AI that creates new content, such as text or images, from prompts

  • Data and Digital Skills at Work: Artificial intelligence at work

Generative AI in Research and the Dissertation

AI systems that produce text, images or other content in response to prompts

  • Mixed Methods and Evidence Synthesis: Using AI tools in research

Generative AI in Sociology

AI that produces text, images or other content in response to prompts

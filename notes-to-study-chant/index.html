Turn your notes into a study chant

Paste your notes into Notes Studio (in Gradify's LifeOS: choose Notes Studio in the sidebar) and one of the outputs is a chant: your key facts set to a 96 BPM beat with drums, bass and a hook, each line on the beat, plus the lyrics to download. The same notes also become flashcards, a spider map, a cheat-sheet poster and a narrated video.

Open Notes Studio → Free · no account needed to try it

How it works

  1. Open Notes Studio. In LifeOS, choose Notes Studio in the sidebar.
  2. Paste or upload your notes. A PDF or text works.
  3. Play the chant. Each key fact lands on the beat; download the lyrics to practise.
  4. Test yourself. Use the flashcards from the same notes to check you can recall each line without the music.

Before and after

Before
  • 60 pages of lecture PDFs
  • AI summaries you can't check
  • Re-reading and highlighting
After
  • Flashcards, each linked to the line it came from
  • A mind map of the topic
  • A 60-second recall drill and reviews timed to your exam

Why it's different

Rhythm and repetition make lists easier to hold in memory, which is why mnemonic songs work for sequences and lists. They help you remember the words; check you understand them with flashcards and questions.

Built for your course

Works for GCSE, A level (AQA, OCR, Edexcel), IB and university modules, from first-year biochemistry to postgraduate law. Gradify also has free courses in 30+ subjects if you want ready-made lessons: explore them.

Questions

Do songs help you memorise for exams?

For lists and sequences, rhythm and repetition can help recall. Combine them with self-testing so you understand, not just remember, the words.

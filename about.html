About Gradify

Gradify turns your notes into flashcards and mind maps checked against what you wrote, and schedules your revision with spaced repetition. It also has free courses in more than 30 subjects, from GCSE to postgraduate study.

Why we built it

Students spend hours making study material and then revise it the least effective way: re-reading. AI tools can make cards in seconds, but they invent things that aren't in your course. Gradify does the making for you and shows you the evidence for every card, so you can trust what you revise and spend your time on recall.

Who builds Gradify

Gradify was started by Seren Aitchison and is built by a small team in the UK. The same team makes BioFrontier, Eirova, Erya and Vizeon.

How lessons and cards are checked

  • Every flashcard made from your notes links to the line it came from; cards that don't fully match are marked to check.
  • Every course lesson is audited on nine dimensions of teaching quality on every change: see Quality and coverage.
  • We only claim to cover a qualification after checking it against the official specification, outcome by outcome.

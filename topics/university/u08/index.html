Lessons in this school

  1. Maths anxiety and number confidence
    Many students feel anxious about numbers, which can hold them back.
  2. Percentages, ratios and proportions
    Percentages, ratios and proportions appear in every subject, from survey results to recipes for lab solutions.
  3. Rounding, significant figures and estimation
    Rounding and estimation help you present numbers clearly and check your work.
  4. Units and conversions
    Using units correctly is essential in science, health and everyday life.
  5. Using formulae
    Formulae express relationships between quantities.
  6. Types of data
    Different types of data need different summaries and analyses.
  7. Descriptive statistics
    Descriptive statistics summarise data with a few key numbers.
  8. Tables and charts
    Good tables and charts make data easy to understand.
  9. Reading graphs critically
    Graphs in the media and research can inform or mislead.
  10. Correlation and causation
    Two things happening together does not mean one causes the other.
  11. Probability and risk
    Probability and risk help us make decisions under uncertainty.
  12. Statistical significance in plain terms
    Research papers often report "statistically significant" results.
  13. Spreadsheets for students
    Spreadsheets help students organise, calculate and chart data.
  14. Presenting numbers in writing
    Numbers in essays and reports must be accurate, clear and meaningful.

Key terms

Maths anxiety
Feelings of tension or fear that interfere with working with numbers
Percentage change
The change between two values expressed as a percentage of the original
Significant figures
The digits in a number that carry meaning, counted from the first non-zero digit
SI units
The international system of standard units of measurement
Formula
A rule showing the relationship between quantities using symbols
Nominal data
Categorical data with no natural order
Median
The middle value of ordered data
Histogram
A chart showing the distribution of continuous data using adjoining bars
Logarithmic scale
A scale where each step represents a multiplication, used for data spanning large ranges
Correlation
A relationship in which two variables tend to change together
Probability
A measure of how likely an event is, from 0 to 1
P-value
The probability of results at least as extreme as observed if the null hypothesis were true
Function
A built-in spreadsheet formula, such as SUM or AVERAGE
Context
Information that helps readers understand what a number means

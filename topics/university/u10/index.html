Key terms

Unseen exam
An exam with questions not seen before the exam
Spaced practice
Spreading study of a topic over several sessions with gaps between them
Retrieval practice
Recalling information from memory, such as through self-testing
Past paper
An exam paper from a previous year used for practice
Worked example
A step-by-step solution to a problem
Reading time
Time at the start of an exam for reading the paper before writing
Thesis
A one-sentence statement of your answer to the question
Question stem
The part of a multiple-choice question that asks the question
Method marks
Marks awarded for correct working rather than only the final answer
Open-book exam
An exam in which notes or resources may be used
Exam anxiety
Excessive worry and stress about exams that can impair performance
Resit
A second attempt at a failed assessment
Extenuating circumstances
Serious, unexpected events beyond a student's control that affect assessment
Generic feedback
Feedback given to the whole cohort about common strengths and errors

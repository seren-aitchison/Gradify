Lessons in this school

  1. Alcohols
    Alcohols contain the –OH group on a saturated carbon.
  2. Phenols
    Phenols have –OH attached directly to a benzene ring.
  3. Ethers
    Ethers contain an oxygen bonded to two carbon groups.
  4. Aldehydes
    Aldehydes contain the –CHO group.
  5. Ketones
    Ketones have a carbonyl group within the carbon chain.
  6. Carboxylic acids
    Carboxylic acids contain the –COOH group.
  7. Acyl chlorides
    Acyl chlorides are highly reactive carboxylic acid derivatives.
  8. Acid anhydrides
    Acid anhydrides are formed from two carboxylic acid molecules.
  9. Esters
    Esters contain the –COO– linkage.
  10. Amides
    Amides contain the –CONH– group.
  11. Amines
    Amines are organic derivatives of ammonia.
  12. Nitriles
    Nitriles contain the –C≡N group.
  13. Nitro compounds
    Nitro compounds contain the –NO₂ group.
  14. Halogenoalkanes
    Halogenoalkanes contain a halogen on an sp³ carbon.
  15. Aromatic compounds
    This lesson explores substituted benzene compounds and how side chains and ring substituents react.
  16. Thiols and sulfides
    Thiols and sulfides are sulfur analogues of alcohols and ethers.
  17. Organohalogen compounds
    Organohalogen compounds are widely used but some harm the environment.
  18. Carbonyl chemistry
    The carbonyl group links aldehydes, ketones, acids and their derivatives.
  19. Enolates and enols
    Carbonyl compounds can react at the carbon next to the C=O.
  20. Heterocyclic compounds
    Heterocycles contain rings with atoms other than carbon.
  21. Functional group synthesis
    Each functional group can be made in several ways.
  22. Multi-step synthesis
    Complex molecules need several steps.

Key terms

Carbinol carbon
The carbon bearing the –OH group
Phenol
A compound with –OH bonded directly to a benzene ring
Ether
A compound with an R–O–R′ linkage
Aldehyde
A compound with a terminal –CHO group
Ketone
A compound with C=O between two carbon groups
Carboxylic acid
A compound with the –COOH group
Acyl chloride
A compound RCOCl
Acid anhydride
A compound (RCO)₂O
Ester
A compound with the –COO– linkage
Amide
A compound containing –CONH– or –CONH₂
Amine
An organic derivative of ammonia
Nitrile
A compound containing –C≡N
Nitro group
The –NO₂ group
Halogenoalkane
An alkane with a halogen atom in place of hydrogen

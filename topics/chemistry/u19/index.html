Lessons in this school

  1. Laboratory safety
    Safe laboratory work protects people and results.
  2. Experimental planning
    Good experiments start with a clear plan.
  3. Reagent handling
    Careful handling keeps reagents pure and people safe.
  4. Reaction setup
    Setting up a reaction correctly controls conditions and safety.
  5. Reflux
    Heating under reflux lets reactions run hot for long periods without losing solvent.
  6. Distillation
    Distillation separates liquids by boiling point.
  7. Extraction
    Extraction moves a compound from one liquid to another.
  8. Recrystallisation
    Recrystallisation purifies solids.
  9. Filtration
    Filtration separates solids from liquids.
  10. Chromatographic purification
    Chromatography can purify compounds as well as analyse them.
  11. Thin-layer chromatography
    This lesson focuses on using TLC as a practical tool in synthesis to choose solvents and track purification.
  12. Column chromatography
    Column chromatography separates gram quantities of compounds.
  13. Yield calculations
    Yield measures how much product a reaction gives.
  14. Purity assessment
    Chemists must check that products are pure.
  15. Reaction monitoring
    Monitoring a reaction shows when it is complete and whether problems arise.
  16. Organic synthesis planning
    Planning a synthesis involves choosing reactions, conditions and order.
  17. Retrosynthetic analysis
    This lesson practises disconnection strategies, synthons and reagent equivalents in retrosynthesis.
  18. Protecting groups
    This lesson focuses on choosing and using protecting groups in practice, including orthogonal protection.
  19. Multi-step synthesis
    This lesson looks at the practical management of multi-step syntheses in the laboratory.
  20. Green synthesis
    Green synthesis applies sustainability to laboratory work.
  21. Laboratory documentation
    Good records make work reproducible and trustworthy.
  22. Experimental reproducibility
    Reproducible results can be repeated by others.
  23. Research integrity
    Research integrity means doing and reporting science honestly.

Key terms

Hazard
Something with the potential to cause harm
Independent variable
The variable deliberately changed
Weighing by difference
Finding mass transferred from two weighings
Round-bottomed flask
A flask suited to even heating and stirring
Reflux
Continuous boiling with vapour condensed back into the flask
Fractional distillation
Distillation with a column for close-boiling liquids
Partition
Distribution of a solute between two immiscible solvents
Recrystallisation
Purifying a solid by dissolving and crystallising it
Filtrate
The liquid that passes through the filter
Flash chromatography
Column chromatography using pressure to speed elution
Co-spotting
Spotting a sample and standard together on one lane
Fraction
A portion of eluent collected separately
Limiting reagent
The reactant used up first
Melting range
Temperature range over which a solid melts

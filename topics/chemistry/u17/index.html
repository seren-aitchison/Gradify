Lessons in this school

  1. Materials chemistry
    Materials chemistry designs substances with useful properties.
  2. Metals and alloys
    Alloys combine metals to improve properties.
  3. Polymers
    Polymers are long-chain molecules made from repeating units.
  4. Polymerisation
    Polymers are made by addition or condensation polymerisation.
  5. Biopolymers
    Biopolymers are polymers made by living things.
  6. Ceramics
    Ceramics are inorganic, non-metallic solids.
  7. Semiconductors
    Semiconductors have conductivity between metals and insulators.
  8. Nanomaterials
    Nanomaterials have structures between 1 and 100 nm.
  9. Composite materials
    Composites combine two or more materials to get the best of each.
  10. Biomaterials
    Biomaterials are used in contact with the body.
  11. Environmental chemistry
    Environmental chemistry studies chemical processes in air, water and soil.
  12. Atmospheric chemistry
    Atmospheric chemistry studies reactions in the air.
  13. Aquatic chemistry
    Aquatic chemistry studies natural waters.
  14. Soil chemistry
    Soil is a complex mixture of minerals, organic matter, water and air.
  15. Water treatment
    Water treatment makes water safe to drink and clean to release.
  16. Air pollution
    Air pollutants harm health and the environment.
  17. Greenhouse gases
    Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere.
  18. Climate chemistry
    Climate chemistry links chemical processes with Earth's climate.
  19. Industrial chemical processes
    Industrial processes convert raw materials into products at scale.
  20. Chemical manufacturing
    Chemical manufacturing turns basic feedstocks into a huge range of products.
  21. Catalysis in industry
    Most industrial chemicals are made with catalysts.
  22. Petroleum chemistry
    Petroleum provides fuels and chemical feedstocks.
  23. Fertilisers
    Fertilisers supply nutrients for crops.
  24. Sustainable chemistry
    Sustainable chemistry meets present needs without harming future generations.
  25. Green chemistry
    Green chemistry designs processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances.
  26. Circular chemistry
    Circular chemistry keeps materials in use instead of discarding them.
  27. Chemical waste management
    Chemical waste must be handled safely and responsibly.

Key terms

Microstructure
Structure of a material at the scale of grains and phases
Alloy
A mixture of a metal with other elements
Thermoplastic
A polymer that softens on heating
Addition polymerisation
Joining alkene monomers without loss of small molecules
Biopolymer
A polymer produced by living organisms
Ceramic
An inorganic, non-metallic solid
Band gap
Energy gap between valence and conduction bands
Nanomaterial
A material with structures 1–100 nm in size
Matrix
The continuous phase in a composite
Biocompatibility
Ability to perform without harmful body responses
Bioaccumulation
Build-up of a chemical in an organism
Troposphere
The lowest layer of the atmosphere
Dissolved oxygen
Oxygen dissolved in water
Humus
Stable organic matter in soil

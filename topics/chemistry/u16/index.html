Lessons in this school

  1. Molecular structure
    Molecular structure covers bond lengths, angles and symmetry.
  2. Molecular orbitals
    This lesson applies molecular orbital theory to diatomic molecules and conjugated systems.
  3. Quantum chemistry
    Quantum chemistry applies quantum mechanics to chemical systems.
  4. Schrödinger equation
    The Schrödinger equation is the central equation of quantum mechanics.
  5. Particle in a box
    The particle in a box is the simplest quantum model.
  6. Harmonic oscillator
    The harmonic oscillator models molecular vibrations.
  7. Rotational spectroscopy
    Rotational spectroscopy measures molecular rotation using microwaves.
  8. Vibrational spectroscopy
    Vibrational spectroscopy studies how molecules vibrate.
  9. Electronic transitions
    Electronic transitions move electrons between molecular orbitals.
  10. Atomic and molecular spectra
    Atomic and molecular spectra arise from different kinds of energy change.
  11. Statistical thermodynamics
    Statistical thermodynamics links molecular energy levels to bulk properties.
  12. Partition functions
    The partition function summarises how molecules spread over energy levels.
  13. Molecular energy distributions
    Molecules in a gas have a range of speeds and energies.
  14. Chemical kinetics
    This lesson explores advanced kinetics, including collision and transition state theories and complex mechanisms.
  15. Surface chemistry
    Surface chemistry studies processes at the boundaries of solids.
  16. Adsorption
    Adsorption is the binding of molecules to a surface.
  17. Colloids
    Colloids are mixtures with particles between 1 nm and 1 μm.
  18. Interfaces
    Interfaces are boundaries between phases.
  19. Photochemistry
    Photochemistry studies reactions started by light.
  20. Molecular interactions
    Molecules attract and repel each other through non-covalent interactions.
  21. Solvation
    Solvation is the interaction of a solute with solvent molecules.
  22. Computational molecular modelling
    Computers model molecules to predict structures and properties.

Key terms

Point group
A set of symmetry operations describing a molecule
HOMO
Highest occupied molecular orbital
Wavefunction
A mathematical function describing a quantum system
Hamiltonian
The operator for total energy
Particle in a box
A quantum model of a confined particle
Force constant
A measure of bond stiffness
Rotational constant
B, related to the moment of inertia
Normal mode
An independent pattern of molecular vibration
Franck-Condon principle
Nuclei stay fixed during an electronic transition
Line spectrum
A spectrum of sharp lines from atoms
Microstate
A specific arrangement of particles among energy levels
Partition function
q = Σ e^(−εᵢ/kT)
Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution
Distribution of molecular speeds or energies in a gas
Transition state theory
Theory treating an activated complex in equilibrium with reactants

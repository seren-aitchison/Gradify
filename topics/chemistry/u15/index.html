Lessons in this school

  1. Foundations of analytical chemistry
    Analytical chemistry finds out what substances are present and how much.
  2. Qualitative analysis
    Qualitative analysis identifies substances.
  3. Quantitative analysis
    Quantitative analysis measures how much of a substance is present.
  4. Accuracy and precision
    Accuracy and precision describe the quality of results.
  5. Calibration
    Calibration relates instrument signals to concentration.
  6. Titrimetry
    Titrimetry determines amounts by reacting the analyte with a solution of known concentration.
  7. Gravimetric analysis
    Gravimetric analysis measures mass to find amounts.
  8. UV-visible spectroscopy
    UV-visible spectroscopy measures absorption of ultraviolet and visible light.
  9. Infrared spectroscopy
    Infrared spectroscopy identifies functional groups from bond vibrations.
  10. Nuclear magnetic resonance
    NMR spectroscopy probes atomic nuclei in a magnetic field.
  11. Proton NMR
    Proton NMR reveals the hydrogen environments in a molecule.
  12. Carbon-13 NMR
    Carbon-13 NMR reveals carbon environments.
  13. NMR chemical shifts
    Chemical shifts reveal electronic environments.
  14. Spin-spin coupling
    Spin-spin coupling splits NMR signals.
  15. Mass spectrometry
    Mass spectrometry measures the mass-to-charge ratio of ions.
  16. Fragmentation patterns
    Molecular ions often break into fragments.
  17. High-resolution mass spectrometry
    High-resolution mass spectrometry measures masses very precisely.
  18. Raman spectroscopy
    Raman spectroscopy studies molecular vibrations through scattered light.
  19. Fluorescence spectroscopy
    Fluorescent molecules emit light after absorbing it.
  20. X-ray diffraction
    X-ray diffraction reveals the arrangement of atoms in crystals.
  21. Chromatography
    Chromatography separates mixtures.
  22. Thin-layer chromatography
    Thin-layer chromatography is a quick separation technique.
  23. Gas chromatography
    Gas chromatography separates volatile compounds.
  24. High-performance liquid chromatography
    HPLC separates compounds in solution at high pressure.
  25. GC-MS
    GC-MS combines gas chromatography with mass spectrometry.
  26. LC-MS
    LC-MS combines liquid chromatography with mass spectrometry.
  27. Structure elucidation
    Structure elucidation combines evidence from several techniques.
  28. Analytical method validation
    Method validation shows that an analytical method is fit for purpose.

Key terms

Analyte
The substance being identified or measured
Flame test
Identifying metal ions by flame colour
Limit of detection
Smallest amount that can be reliably detected
Accuracy
Closeness to the true value
Calibration curve
A plot of signal against known concentrations
Primary standard
A pure, stable substance used to standardise solutions
Gravimetric analysis
Analysis based on measuring mass
Chromophore
A group that absorbs UV or visible light
Wavenumber
The number of waves per cm, in cm⁻¹
Nuclear spin
A property of some nuclei making them behave like tiny magnets
Integration
The area under a signal, proportional to hydrogen count
Proton decoupling
Removing ¹H coupling to simplify ¹³C spectra
Chemical shift
Position of a signal relative to TMS in ppm
Spin-spin coupling
Splitting of a signal by neighbouring nuclei

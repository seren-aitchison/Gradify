Lessons in this school

  1. Introduction to inorganic chemistry
    Inorganic chemistry studies the elements and compounds other than most carbon compounds.
  2. Main-group chemistry
    Main-group elements occupy the s- and p-blocks.
  3. s-block chemistry
    This lesson explores s-block compounds in depth, including oxides, peroxides, nitrates and carbonates.
  4. p-block chemistry
    This lesson explores important p-block compounds, including boranes, silicates, oxoacids and interhalogens.
  5. Transition metal chemistry
    This lesson explores characteristic reactions of transition metal ions, including precipitation, ligand exchange and redox.
  6. Coordination compounds
    Coordination compounds contain metal ions bonded to ligands.
  7. Ligands
    Ligands donate electron pairs to metal ions.
  8. Coordination numbers
    Coordination number is the number of donor atoms bonded to a metal.
  9. Isomerism in complexes
    Complexes can show geometric and optical isomerism.
  10. Crystal field theory
    Crystal field theory explains how ligands split d-orbital energies.
  11. Ligand field theory
    Ligand field theory improves on crystal field theory by including covalent bonding.
  12. Magnetic properties
    Unpaired electrons make complexes magnetic.
  13. Colour in transition metal complexes
    Many transition metal complexes are coloured.
  14. Oxidation states
    Transition metals show many oxidation states.
  15. Organometallic chemistry
    Organometallic compounds contain metal-carbon bonds.
  16. Catalytic complexes
    Metal complexes catalyse many reactions.
  17. Metal extraction
    Metals are extracted from ores by methods depending on their reactivity.
  18. Industrial inorganic chemistry
    Inorganic chemicals are made on a vast scale.
  19. Bioinorganic chemistry
    Metal ions are essential to life.
  20. Solid-state inorganic chemistry
    Solid-state chemistry studies extended inorganic solids.
  21. Lanthanide chemistry
    The lanthanides fill the 4f subshell.
  22. Actinide chemistry
    The actinides fill the 5f subshell and are all radioactive.

Key terms

Inorganic chemistry
Chemistry of elements and compounds other than most organic compounds
Hydrolysis
Reaction with water breaking a compound apart
Peroxide
A compound containing O₂²⁻
Silicate
A compound built from SiO₄ tetrahedra
Ligand exchange
Replacement of one ligand by another
Coordination sphere
The metal and its directly bonded ligands
Denticity
Number of donor atoms a ligand uses
Coordination number
The number of donor atoms bonded to a metal
Cis isomer
Identical ligands adjacent
Crystal field splitting
Energy gap between split d orbitals
Ligand field theory
A bonding model combining crystal field and MO theory
Paramagnetism
Attraction into a magnetic field caused by unpaired electrons
d-d transition
Promotion of an electron between split d orbitals
Oxidation state
The charge an atom would have if all bonds were ionic

Lessons in this school

  1. Gases
    Gases have properties that differ sharply from liquids and solids.
  2. Gas laws
    The gas laws describe how pressure, volume and temperature of a gas are related.
  3. Ideal gas equation
    The ideal gas equation links pressure, volume, temperature and amount of gas.
  4. Real gases
    Real gases deviate from ideal behaviour under some conditions.
  5. Kinetic molecular theory
    Kinetic molecular theory explains gas behaviour from the motion of particles.
  6. Liquids
    Liquids have particles close together but free to move.
  7. Vapour pressure
    Vapour pressure is the pressure of a vapour in equilibrium with its liquid.
  8. Boiling and melting
    Melting and boiling are changes of state that require energy.
  9. Phase diagrams
    Phase diagrams show which state a substance is in at any temperature and pressure.
  10. Solids
    Solids have fixed shapes because their particles are held in place.
  11. Crystalline solids
    Crystalline solids have long-range order that gives them sharp melting points and flat faces.
  12. Amorphous solids
    Amorphous solids lack long-range order.
  13. Solutions
    A solution is a homogeneous mixture of a solute in a solvent.
  14. Solubility
    Solubility is the maximum amount of solute that dissolves in a solvent.
  15. Concentration
    This lesson extends concentration beyond mol dm⁻³ to other units used in physical chemistry and everyday life.
  16. Colligative properties
    Colligative properties depend on the number of solute particles, not their identity.
  17. Osmosis
    Osmosis is the movement of solvent through a semipermeable membrane.
  18. Diffusion
    Diffusion is the spreading of particles from high to low concentration.
  19. Surface tension
    Surface tension makes liquid surfaces behave like a stretched skin.
  20. Viscosity
    Viscosity is a liquid's resistance to flow.
  21. Intermolecular interactions
    This lesson compares all the interactions between particles and uses them to predict and explain physical properties.
  22. Materials properties
    The properties of materials come from their structure and bonding.

Key terms

Pressure
Force per unit area
Boyle's law
pV is constant at constant temperature
Ideal gas equation
pV = nRT
Ideal gas
A gas obeying pV = nRT exactly
Kinetic energy
Energy of motion, ½mv²
Volatile
Easily evaporating
Vapour pressure
Pressure of vapour in equilibrium with its liquid
Latent heat
Energy absorbed or released during a change of state
Triple point
Conditions where solid, liquid and gas coexist
Crystalline
Having a regular repeating arrangement
Anisotropy
Variation of properties with direction
Amorphous solid
A solid without long-range order
Solute
The substance dissolved
Solubility
Maximum amount of solute that dissolves at a given temperature

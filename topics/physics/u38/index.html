Lessons in this unit

  1. Linear and nonlinear systems
    A linear system's response is proportional to its input, and solutions can be added together.
  2. Nonlinear differential equations
    Nonlinear differential equations describe how systems change when rates depend nonlinearly on the state.
  3. Fixed points and stability
    Fixed points are states where a system stays put.
  4. Phase-space analysis
    Phase-space analysis pictures every possible state of a system as a point and its evolution as a trajectory.
  5. Bifurcation theory
    A bifurcation is a qualitative change in a system's behaviour as a parameter crosses a critical value: equilibria appear, disappear or swap stability, or oscillations begin.
  6. Period-doubling
    Period-doubling is a route to chaos in which an oscillation repeatedly doubles its period as a parameter increases: period 2, 4, 8 and so on, faster and faster, until chaos appears.
  7. Deterministic chaos
    Deterministic chaos is complex, unpredictable-looking behaviour arising from simple rules with no randomness.
  8. Sensitivity to initial conditions
    Sensitivity to initial conditions, the butterfly effect, means that two almost identical starting states end up completely different.
  9. Lyapunov exponents
    Lyapunov exponents measure the average exponential rate at which nearby trajectories separate or converge.
  10. Strange attractors
    A strange attractor is the set a chaotic dissipative system settles onto: bounded, with a fractal structure and chaotic motion on it.
  11. Logistic map
    The logistic map xn+1 = rxn(1 − xn) is a one-line model of population growth that shows the whole route from steady behaviour to chaos.
  12. Lorenz system
    The Lorenz system is three simple equations derived from atmospheric convection that became the icon of chaos.
  13. Fractals
    Fractals are shapes with detail at every scale, often self-similar, and with non-integer dimensions.
  14. Self-organisation
    Self-organisation is the spontaneous emergence of order from local interactions, without a designer or external template.
  15. Synchronisation
    Synchronisation is the tendency of coupled oscillators to fall into step.
  16. Coupled nonlinear oscillators
    Coupled nonlinear oscillators exchange energy and phase in rich ways: normal modes, beats, localisation, resonance and chaos.
  17. Pattern formation
    Pattern formation is how uniform states break into spatial structures such as stripes, spots, spirals and hexagons.
  18. Reaction-diffusion systems
    Reaction-diffusion systems combine local chemical reactions with diffusion, producing waves, spirals and stationary Turing patterns.
  19. Solitons and nonlinear waves
    Solitons are solitary waves that keep their shape while travelling and survive collisions intact, because nonlinearity exactly balances dispersion.
  20. Chaos in mechanical and electrical systems
    Chaos is not just theory: it appears in pendulums, rotating machines, electronic circuits, lasers and orbits.
  21. Complex systems and emergent behaviour
    Complex systems consist of many interacting parts whose collective behaviour is not obvious from the parts alone.

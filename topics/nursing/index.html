Study plans

  • Adult nursingThe shared core, then adult nursing across medical, surgical and community settings, acute and critical care, older adults and end-of-life care, with field scenarios, placement preparation and reflection.
  • Children's nursingThe shared core, then children's and young people's nursing: development, family-centred care, consent and parental responsibility, safeguarding, paediatric assessment, neonates, childhood conditions and medicines safety.
  • Mental health nursingThe shared core, then mental health nursing: assessment, therapeutic relationships, conditions, risk and crisis, mental health and capacity law, de-escalation and recovery, with physical health care for people with mental illness.
  • Learning disability nursingThe shared core, then learning disability nursing: communication, reasonable adjustments, capacity and advocacy, positive behaviour support, health inequalities, epilepsy and complex needs, families and inclusion.
  • Pre-registration year 1 (all fields)Professional practice, the body, assessment and core skills: the foundations every field of nursing builds on.
  • Recognising and responding to deteriorationAssessment, observations, escalation, acute and critical care, and the conditions behind deterioration.
  • Medicines managementPharmacology, the administration process, calculations and medicines safety. Education only: you give medicines under your local policy and supervision.
  • Newly qualified nurseAccountability, delegation, prioritisation, escalation, medicines safety and handover for your first post.
  • Internationally educated nurses: UK practiceThe NMC Code, UK law and policy across the four nations, assessment conventions and medicines governance in the UK.
  • Nursing associatesThe foundations for nursing associates, within the NMC's standards of proficiency for nursing associates and your local scope.
  • CPD and revalidationRefresh professional standards, evidence-based practice, leadership and medicines knowledge, and record it as CPD.

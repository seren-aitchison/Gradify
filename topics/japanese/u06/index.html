Lessons in this school

  1. Family and relationships
    Japanese has two sets of family words: humble ones for your own family and respectful ones for other people's.
  2. Describing people: appearance and personality
    Describing people combines adjectives, the ～て います form and noun-modifying clauses.
  3. Hobbies, sport and free time
    Talking about hobbies is a core GCSE topic.
  4. Health, the body and illness
    Being able to describe symptoms and understand advice is important when living or travelling in Japan.
  5. School life in Japan
    Japanese schools differ from UK schools in several ways.
  6. Part-time work and future plans
    Talking about work and the future is a GCSE Higher topic.
  7. Technology and social media
    Technology and social media are part of daily life and a common GCSE topic.
  8. Home and housing
    Japanese homes have distinctive features, from the genkan to tatami rooms.
  9. Town, directions and transport
    Getting around a Japanese town needs vocabulary for places, directions and transport.
  10. Holidays and travel in Japan
    Travel in Japan offers hot springs, traditional inns and famous sights.
  11. Weather and seasons
    Japan has four distinct seasons plus a rainy season and typhoons.
  12. Japanese food and eating out
    Japanese food and table manners are an important part of the culture.
  13. Shopping and clothes
    Japanese uses different verbs for wearing different items of clothing.
  14. The environment and recycling
    Japan has detailed rules for sorting rubbish and strong recycling habits.
  15. Volunteering and global issues
    GCSE Higher tasks include global issues and helping others.
  16. Festivals and celebrations: describing an event
    Japanese festivals and celebrations mark the year.

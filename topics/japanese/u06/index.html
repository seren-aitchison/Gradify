Five sample flashcards

Answer each from memory, then open it to check. Still foggy? Each card has the plain-English version.

What do you call your own older sister when speaking to others?

あね (ane). From “Family and relationships” →

In plain English: Japanese has two sets of family words: humble ones for your own family and respectful ones for other people's. Key idea: Use humble terms such as ちち, はは, あに and あね for your own family and respectful terms such as おとうさん and おかあさん for others' families; count family members with にん and describe relationships with なかが いい and けんかを します.

How do you say "I have a stomach ache"?

おなかが いたいです (onaka ga itai desu). From “Health, the body and illness” →

In plain English: Being able to describe symptoms and understand advice is important when living or travelling in Japan. Key idea: Describe symptoms with ～が いたいです, ねつが あります and かぜを ひきました; give advice with た-form + ほうが いいです and ない-form + ほうが いいです; say おだいじに to someone who is ill.

What does SNS mean in Japanese?

Social media. From “Technology and social media” →

In plain English: Technology and social media are part of daily life and a common GCSE topic. Key idea: Technology vocabulary includes スマホ, パソコン, SNS and メッセージを おくる; describe use with frequency, discuss pros and cons with べんり, あぶない and ～すぎる, and give balanced opinions with が and と おもいます.

How do you say "I stayed at a hotel"?

ホテルに とまりました (hoteru ni tomarimashita). From “Holidays and travel in Japan” →

In plain English: Travel in Japan offers hot springs, traditional inns and famous sights. Key idea: Travel vocabulary includes りょこう, かんこう, りょかん, おんせん and おみやげ; describe trips with past forms, たり, いちばん ～のは and opinions, and express future wishes with また いきたいです.

How do you ask "May I try it on?"

しちゃくしても いいですか (shichaku shitemo ii desu ka). From “Shopping and clothes” →

In plain English: Japanese uses different verbs for wearing different items of clothing. Key idea: Japanese uses きる for upper-body clothes, はく for trousers and shoes, かぶる for hats, かける for glasses and する for accessories; ask to try on with しちゃくしても いいですか and choose with これに します.

Quick quiz: test yourself on Everyday Life: GCSE Themes

9 questions from the course, from foundation recall to stretch. Pick an answer, then say whether you knew it or guessed.

  1. Which word means "my father" when speaking about your own family (chichi)? (Foundation recall)
    Show the answer

    ちち. ちち is the humble term for your own father.

  2. How do you say a person is tall (se ga takai desu)? (Foundation recall)
    Show the answer

    せが たかいです. A person's height is described with せが たかい, literally "their height is high".

  3. Which verb goes with しゃしん (shashin, photos)? (Foundation recall)
    Show the answer

    とる (toru). Photos are taken with the verb とる, so taking photos is しゃしんを とる.

  4. Which word refers to someone else's younger sister (imōtosan)? (Exam style)
    Show the answer

    いもうとさん. いもうとさん is the respectful term for another person's younger sister.

  5. Which word means "serious and hard-working" (majime)? (Exam style)
    Show the answer

    まじめ. まじめ is a な-adjective meaning serious and hard-working.

  6. How do you say "I'm in the tennis club" (tenisubu ni haitte imasu)? (Exam style)
    Show the answer

    テニスぶに はいって います. Being a member of a club uses ～ぶに はいって います, literally "have entered the club".

  7. How would you say "I get on well with my older brother" (ani to naka ga ii desu)? (Stretch)
    Show the answer

    あにと なかが いいです. なかが いい means to get on well with someone.

  8. Which verb is used for wearing a hat (bōshi o kabutte imasu)? (Stretch)
    Show the answer

    かぶって います. Hats and caps use the verb かぶる, so the phrase is ぼうしを かぶって います.

  9. Which answer develops a hobby description best for GCSE? (Stretch)
    Show the answer

    しゅうまつに ともだちと テニスを します。とても たのしいです. Adding when, who with and an opinion gives a fuller answer.

Key takeaways

  • Use humble terms such as ちち, はは, あに and あね for your own family and respectful terms such as おとうさん and おかあさん for others' families; count family members with にん and describe relationships with なかが いい and けんかを します.
  • Describe appearance with topic は body part が adjective, as in せが たかい and かみが ながい, use ～て います for build and clothing, and describe personality with やさしい, おもしろい, あかるい, まじめ and しんせつ.
  • Use する for most sports and games, ひく for piano and guitar and かく for drawing; talk about hobbies with しゅみは ～ことです, frequency words and たり, and describe clubs with ～ぶに はいって います.
  • Describe symptoms with ～が いたいです, ねつが あります and かぜを ひきました; give advice with た-form + ほうが いいです and ない-form + ほうが いいです; say おだいじに to someone who is ill.
  • Japanese education has elementary, junior high and senior high schools, a school year starting in April, uniforms, student cleaning, club activities and school lunches; talk about subjects with すき, とくい and にがて and compare with より.
  • Talk about part-time work with アルバイトを して います and じきゅう; express intentions with dictionary form + つもりです, fixed plans with よていです and ambitions with noun + に なりたいです.

Common mistakes in Everyday Life: GCSE Themes

  • Calling your own mother おかあさん when talking about her to others. Use はは; おかあさん is for addressing her or for others' mothers.
  • Saying あには たかいです to mean tall. This can mean "expensive"; for height, say せが たかいです.
  • Using する with the piano, as in ピアノを します. The verb is ひく: ピアノを ひきます.
  • Saying あたまが いたいでした for a past headache. い-adjectives change: あたまが いたかったです.
  • Assuming the Japanese school year starts in September. It starts in April.
  • Saying いしゃを なりたいです. "Become" takes に: いしゃに なりたいです.

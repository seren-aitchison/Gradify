Lessons in this school

  1. Verb groups: ru-verbs, u-verbs and irregular verbs
    Every Japanese verb belongs to one of three groups, and the group decides how the verb changes.
  2. The masu form: present and future
    The masu form is the polite verb form used with people you do not know well, in class and at work.
  3. Negative and past masu forms
    Once you know the masu form, its negative and past forms follow one pattern for every verb.
  4. Mashou and masen ka: suggestions and invitations
    Japanese uses verb endings to make suggestions and invitations politely.
  5. The object particle o
    Particles are short words that show the role of each noun in a sentence.
  6. Ni and e: direction and destination
    To say where you are going, Japanese uses the particles に or へ with verbs of movement.
  7. De: place of action and means
    The particle で has several uses.
  8. Ni for time and to for together
    The particle に can mark a specific time, and と can mean "with" a person.
  9. Arimasu and imasu: existence and location
    Japanese has two verbs for "there is" or "to be in a place": one for things and one for living beings.
  10. Wa and ga: a first look
    The particles は and が both appear before the subject, but they do different jobs.
  11. Telling the time
    Telling the time in Japanese uses numbers with counters for hours and minutes, with some irregular readings.
  12. Days of the week and dates
    Days, months and dates in Japanese follow patterns with some irregular readings.
  13. Daily routine
    Describing a daily routine brings together verbs, particles and time expressions.
  14. Frequency adverbs
    Frequency adverbs say how often you do something.
  15. Hoshii and tai: wants
    Japanese has two main ways to say what you want: ほしい for things and ～たい for actions.

Key terms

The plain, basic form of a verb, ending in an -u sound
Masu form
The polite form of a verb ending in ます
ません

ましょう

Particle
A short word that shows a noun's role in a sentence
Destination
The place someone moves towards
Place of action
Where an activity takes place, marked by で
Specific time

Lessons in this school

  1. The JLPT: levels and format
    The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test is the most widely taken Japanese exam in the world.
  2. GCSE Japanese: the four skills
    GCSE Japanese assesses listening, speaking, reading and writing.
  3. Listening strategies
    Listening exams test understanding at natural speed.
  4. Reading strategies for exams
    Exam reading requires understanding texts accurately within a time limit.
  5. JLPT vocabulary and grammar questions
    The JLPT language knowledge section uses several question types for kanji, vocabulary and grammar.
  6. Speaking: role plays
    GCSE speaking role plays test your ability to handle everyday situations.
  7. Speaking: photo description and conversation
    The picture-based task and conversation give chances to show range and opinions.
  8. Writing: short texts in kana and kanji
    Short writing tasks test accurate use of kana, kanji and basic grammar.
  9. Writing: longer texts and genkou youshi
    Longer writing tasks require organised paragraphs, a range of grammar and correct layout.
  10. Translation from Japanese
    Translating from Japanese into English tests precise understanding.
  11. Translation into Japanese
    Translating from English into Japanese tests accurate grammar, word order and script.
  12. Pronunciation and pitch in speaking exams
    Clear pronunciation helps examiners understand you.
  13. Common errors and how to avoid them
    Many learners make the same mistakes in Japanese.

Key terms

JLPT
Japanese-Language Proficiency Test
Tier
Foundation or Higher level of entry
Distractor
An option mentioned in the audio that is not the answer
Skimming
Reading quickly for the main idea
漢字読み
Kanji reading questions
Role play
A speaking task acting out an everyday situation
Picture-based task
A speaking task describing and discussing a photo
Bullet point
A required content point in a writing task
原稿用紙
Genkō yōshi, squared manuscript paper
Omitted subject
A subject left out in Japanese and understood from context
Word order
The order of elements in a sentence
Mora
A unit of timing in Japanese; each beat takes roughly equal time
Error log
A personal record of repeated mistakes

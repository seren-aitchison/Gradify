Study plans

  • GCSE JapaneseKana, the first kanji, core grammar from desu to the te-form and plain forms, the GCSE themes and exam skills.
  • JLPT N5Hiragana, katakana, around a hundred kanji, the masu form, particles, adjectives and counters.
  • JLPT N4The te-form, plain forms, potential, volitional, conditionals, giving and receiving, the passive and basic keigo.
  • JLPT N3Aspect, hearsay and appearance, purpose and reasoning, keigo in use, kanji to around 650 and reading longer texts.
  • Grammar in orderFrom X wa Y desu to the causative-passive and N3 nuance, one structure at a time.
  • Japanese for travelKana, greetings, numbers, prices, directions, transport, food, polite shop Japanese and etiquette.
  • Japan: culture and societyHistory, religion, festivals, the arts, popular culture, work, an ageing society, dialects and the writing system.

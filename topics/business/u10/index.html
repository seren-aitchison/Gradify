Lessons in this school

  1. Information systems in organisations
    Every organisation runs on information.
  2. Digital transformation
    Digital transformation is more than buying new technology.
  3. E-commerce business models
    E-commerce has created new ways to make money online.
  4. Platforms and network effects
    Some of the world's most valuable firms are platforms that connect users rather than make products.
  5. Data-driven decision-making
    Many firms now aim to base decisions on evidence rather than instinct.
  6. Business intelligence and dashboards
    Business intelligence tools turn data from across a firm into reports and dashboards that managers can act on.
  7. Correlation and regression for forecasting
    Businesses often want to predict one variable from another, such as sales from advertising.
  8. Artificial intelligence in business
    Artificial intelligence is changing how businesses operate and compete.
  9. Cybersecurity for business
    Cyber attacks are among the most common threats UK businesses face.
  10. Data protection and UK GDPR
    Businesses hold large amounts of personal data, and the law controls how they use it.
  11. Digital ethics
    Digital technologies raise ethical questions that the law does not always answer.
  12. Enterprise systems: ERP and CRM
    Large firms use enterprise systems to integrate their processes and data.
  13. Cloud computing
    Cloud computing lets businesses rent computing power and software over the internet.
  14. Fintech and digital payments
    Financial technology is changing how businesses pay, get paid and raise money.
  15. Agile project management
    Agile methods have spread from software development to many kinds of projects.

Key terms

Information system
A set of people, procedures, data, software and hardware that collects, processes and distributes information
Digitisation
Converting analogue information into digital form
B2B
Business-to-business trade
Platform
A business that creates value by enabling interactions between different user groups
Descriptive analytics
Analysis of what has happened
Business intelligence
Tools and processes that gather and analyse data to support decisions
Correlation coefficient
A measure of the strength and direction of a linear relationship, from −1 to +1
Machine learning
A form of AI in which systems learn patterns from data
Phishing
Fraudulent messages designed to trick people into revealing information or making payments
Personal data
Information relating to an identified or identifiable living individual
Dark pattern
A design that manipulates users into choices they would not otherwise make
Enterprise resource planning
Software that integrates core business processes in a single system
Software as a service
Applications delivered over the internet on a subscription basis
Fintech
Technology used to deliver financial services in new ways

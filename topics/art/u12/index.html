Lessons in this school

  1. Museums and galleries
    Museums and galleries collect, preserve and display art for the public.
  2. Curating and exhibitions
    Curators select and arrange art to create meaning for audiences.
  3. The art market
    Art is bought and sold in a global market worth tens of billions of pounds.
  4. Public art and patronage
    Art is funded not only by buyers but by patrons, governments and sponsors.
  5. Copyright basics for artists
    Copyright protects artists' work and controls how it is copied and used.
  6. Appropriation, influence and fair dealing
    Artists have always borrowed from others, but where does influence end and infringement begin?
  7. Cultural property and restitution
    Many museum collections include objects taken during colonialism or war.
  8. Forgery and authenticity
    Forgeries have fooled experts and collectors for centuries.
  9. Representation and ethics
    Artists make choices about whom they represent and how.
  10. Building a portfolio
    A portfolio shows your skills, ideas and development as an artist.
  11. Applying to art school
    There are several routes into art and design higher education.
  12. Careers in art and design
    Art and design skills lead to many careers across the creative industries and beyond.
  13. Documenting and presenting your work
    Good documentation lets your work be seen, assessed and remembered.

Key terms

Public museum
An institution holding a permanent collection for public benefit
Curator
A person who researches, selects and interprets works for collections or exhibitions
Primary market
The first sale of an artwork, usually through a gallery
Patronage
Financial support or commissioning of art by individuals or institutions
Copyright
The legal right controlling copying and use of an original work
Appropriation
Using existing images or objects in a new artwork
Restitution
The return of cultural objects to their places or peoples of origin
Forgery
A work made or altered to deceive people about its origin
Cultural appropriation
Using elements of another culture without understanding, credit or benefit to it
Portfolio
A curated selection of work showing an artist's skills and development
UCAS
The organisation that manages applications to UK undergraduate courses
Portfolio career
A career combining several part-time or freelance roles
Artist statement
A short text explaining an artist's work, ideas and methods

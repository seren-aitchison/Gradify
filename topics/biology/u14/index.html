Lessons in this school

  1. Experimental design
    Good experimental design produces reliable answers to biological questions.
  2. Hypothesis development
    A hypothesis is a testable explanation that guides research.
  3. Controls
    Controls make sure that results are caused by the variable being tested.
  4. Sampling
    Sampling selects part of a population to estimate its properties.
  5. Microscopy
    Microscopy is a core laboratory skill in biology.
  6. Histology
    Histology is the microscopic study of tissues.
  7. Cell culture
    Cell culture grows cells outside the body under controlled conditions.
  8. Molecular biology techniques
    Molecular biology techniques extract and manipulate nucleic acids.
  9. PCR
    This lesson focuses on the practical side of PCR: designing primers, setting up reactions and troubleshooting.
  10. Gel electrophoresis
    This lesson focuses on the practical skills of running and analysing agarose gels.
  11. Western blotting
    Western blotting detects specific proteins in a mixture.
  12. ELISA
    This lesson focuses on running and analysing ELISAs in the laboratory, including standard curves.
  13. Chromatography
    Chromatography separates mixtures based on how components interact with two phases.
  14. Spectrophotometry
    Spectrophotometry measures how much light a solution absorbs.
  15. Sequencing
    This lesson focuses on planning a sequencing experiment in the laboratory, from sample to data.
  16. Flow cytometry
    Flow cytometry measures properties of thousands of individual cells per second.
  17. Statistical analysis
    Statistics help decide whether results reflect real effects or chance.
  18. Data visualisation
    Good graphs communicate data clearly and honestly.
  19. Research ethics
    Research ethics protects participants, animals and the environment.
  20. Scientific communication
    Scientists share findings through papers, posters, talks and public engagement.
  21. Literature reviews
    Literature reviews summarise and evaluate existing research.
  22. Reproducibility
    Reproducibility is the ability to obtain consistent results when research is repeated.
  23. Research integrity
    Research integrity means conducting research honestly and responsibly.
  24. Laboratory safety
    Laboratory safety protects people from chemical, biological and physical hazards.

Key terms

Independent variable
The factor deliberately changed
Hypothesis
A testable proposed explanation
Negative control
A condition expected to show no effect
Random sampling
Selection with equal chance for every individual
Eyepiece graticule
A scale in the eyepiece for measuring
Fixation
Preserving tissue to prevent decay
Cell line
Cells that can divide indefinitely in culture
Lysis
Breaking cells open
Primer dimer
Primers binding to each other instead of the template
Loading dye
A dense, coloured solution helping load and track samples
SDS-PAGE
Gel electrophoresis separating proteins by size
Sandwich ELISA
An ELISA using capture and detection antibodies
Mobile phase
The moving solvent in chromatography
Absorbance
A measure of light absorbed by a sample

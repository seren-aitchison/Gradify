Lessons in this school

  1. The United Kingdom
    The United Kingdom is made up of four countries.
  2. Continents and oceans
    The world is made up of seven continents and five oceans.
  3. Countries and capitals
    There are about 195 countries in the world, each with its own capital and government.
  4. Latitude, longitude and time zones
    Lines of latitude and longitude help us find any place on Earth, and they explain why time is different around the world.
  5. Comparing places
    Geographers compare places to understand how they are similar and different.
  6. Settlements
    A settlement is a place where people live.
  7. Land use and trade
    People use land in different ways and trade goods across the world.
  8. Rivers
    Rivers carry water from the land to the sea and shape the landscape.
  9. Mountains
    Mountains are high, steep landforms found on every continent.
  10. Volcanoes and earthquakes
    Volcanoes and earthquakes happen where the Earth's plates meet.
  11. Climate zones and biomes
    Different parts of the world have very different climates and living things.
  12. Maps and compasses
    Maps help us find our way and understand places.
  13. Grid references
    Grid references help us find exact places on a map.
  14. Fieldwork
    Fieldwork means investigating geography outdoors.

Key terms

United Kingdom
The country made up of England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
Continent
One of the seven very large areas of land on Earth
Country
An area of land with its own government and borders
Latitude
Distance north or south of the Equator, in degrees
Physical geography
Natural features such as rivers, mountains and climate
Settlement
A place where people live
Import
A good brought into a country from abroad
Source
The place where a river begins
Fold mountain
A mountain formed when plates push together and crumple rock upwards
Tectonic plate
A large moving piece of the Earth's crust
Climate
The average weather of a place over many years
Key
A list explaining the symbols on a map
Easting
A vertical grid line, numbered along the bottom of the map
Fieldwork
Investigating geography by collecting data outdoors

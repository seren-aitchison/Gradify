Lessons in this school

  1. Changes within living memory
    Some changes have happened so recently that people alive today remember them.
  2. The Stone Age to the Iron Age
    For thousands of years before the Romans, people in Britain lived in the Stone Age, the Bronze Age and the Iron Age.
  3. Roman Britain
    The Romans ruled much of Britain for nearly 400 years.
  4. Anglo-Saxons and Scots
    After the Romans left, Anglo-Saxons settled in England and Scots moved into what became Scotland.
  5. Vikings
    From the late 700s, Vikings from Scandinavia raided and then settled in Britain.
  6. A local history study
    History happened in your own area too.
  7. A theme beyond 1066
    Some topics are best studied across many centuries.
  8. Ancient Egypt
    Ancient Egypt was one of the earliest great civilisations, built along the River Nile.
  9. Ancient Greece
    The ancient Greeks developed ideas that still shape our world, from democracy to the Olympic Games.
  10. The Maya
    The Maya built a great civilisation in Central America, with cities, writing and astronomy.
  11. Events beyond living memory
    Some important events happened long before anyone alive today was born.
  12. Significant people
    Some people's actions changed the world.
  13. Sources and evidence
    Historians use sources as evidence to find out about the past.
  14. Timelines and chronology
    Chronology means putting events in order of time.

Key terms

Living memory
The time that people who are still alive can remember
Prehistory
The time before written records
Invasion
When an army enters another country to take control
Anglo-Saxons
Peoples from northern Europe who settled in England after the Romans left
Viking
A Scandinavian raider, trader or settler of the late 700s to 1000s
Census
An official count of the population, recording who lived in each household
Continuity
Something that stays the same over time
Pharaoh
A king of ancient Egypt
City-state
An independent city and the land around it with its own government
Maya
A civilisation of Central America, and its people today
Eyewitness
A person who saw an event happen
Significant person
Someone whose actions changed things for many people and are remembered
Primary source
A source from the time being studied
Chronology
The order in which events happened

Lessons in this school

  1. Everyday materials
    Everything around us is made from materials such as wood, plastic, metal and glass.
  2. Properties of materials
    Materials have properties such as hardness, flexibility and transparency.
  3. Solids, liquids and gases
    Materials can be solids, liquids or gases.
  4. The water cycle
    The water cycle is the journey water takes between the land, the sea and the sky.
  5. Rocks and soils
    Rocks are natural materials that make up the Earth's surface, and soils are made partly from rocks.
  6. Changes of materials
    Materials can change in reversible and irreversible ways.
  7. Pushes and pulls
    A force is a push or a pull.
  8. Friction
    Friction is a force that acts when two surfaces rub together.
  9. Gravity
    Gravity is the force that pulls things towards the Earth.
  10. Air and water resistance
    Air resistance and water resistance are forces that slow down objects moving through air and water.
  11. Magnets
    Magnets can attract or repel each other and attract some materials.
  12. Levers, pulleys and gears
    Levers, pulleys and gears are simple machines that let a small force have a bigger effect.

Key terms

Object
A thing, such as a cup, chair or door
Property
A way of describing what a material is like or how it behaves
Solid
A state of matter that keeps its own shape
Evaporation
When a liquid turns into a gas, such as water into water vapour
Igneous rock
Rock formed when melted rock cools and hardens
Dissolve
When a solid mixes into a liquid so completely that it seems to disappear
Force
A push or a pull
Friction
A force between two surfaces that slows movement
Gravity
A force of attraction that pulls objects towards the Earth
Air resistance
A force that slows down objects moving through air
Magnet
An object that produces a force attracting magnetic materials
Lever
A rigid bar that turns around a pivot to help move a load

Lessons in this school

  1. Number bonds
    Number bonds are pairs of numbers that add up to a total, such as 7 and 3 making 10.
  2. Adding in your head
    Many additions are quicker in your head than on paper.
  3. Subtracting in your head
    Subtraction can mean taking away or finding the difference between two numbers.
  4. Column addition
    Column addition is the written method for adding bigger numbers.
  5. Column subtraction
    Column subtraction is the written method for taking away bigger numbers.
  6. Checking with the inverse
    Addition and subtraction undo each other, and so do multiplication and division.
  7. What multiplication means
    Multiplication is a quick way of adding equal groups.
  8. Times tables
    Knowing the times tables by heart, up to 12 × 12, makes almost every other kind of maths easier.
  9. Factors and multiples
    Multiples are the numbers in a times table; factors are the numbers that divide exactly into a number.
  10. What division means
    Division means splitting into equal parts.
  11. Written multiplication
    To multiply bigger numbers on paper we use short multiplication (by a one-digit number) and long multiplication (by a two-digit number).
  12. Short division and remainders
    Short division is the written method for dividing a big number by a one-digit number, sometimes called the bus stop method.

Key terms

Number bond
A pair of numbers that add up to a given total
Mental calculation
Working out an answer in your head, sometimes with quick jottings
Difference
How much bigger one number is than another, found by subtracting
Column addition
A written method that adds numbers in place value columns
Column subtraction
A written method that subtracts numbers in place value columns
Inverse operation
An operation that undoes another, such as subtraction undoing addition
Multiply
To find the total of a number of equal groups
Times table
The list of multiples of a number, such as 3, 6, 9, 12 for the 3 times table
Factor
A whole number that divides exactly into another number
Divide
To split into equal parts or equal groups
Short multiplication
A written method for multiplying by a one-digit number
Short division
A written method for dividing by a one-digit number, working from the left

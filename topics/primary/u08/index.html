Lessons in this school

  1. Parts of a plant
    Flowering plants are made of parts that each do an important job.
  2. What plants need to grow
    Plants need the right conditions to grow well.
  3. How seeds and bulbs grow
    Many plants grow from seeds and some grow from bulbs.
  4. Pollination and seed dispersal
    Flowers make seeds through pollination, and plants spread their seeds in clever ways.
  5. Animal groups
    Animals can be sorted into groups by their features and what they eat.
  6. The human body and senses
    Our bodies have many parts, and our five senses help us find out about the world.
  7. Teeth and eating
    Our teeth help us bite and chew food, and we need to look after them.
  8. Skeletons and muscles
    Skeletons and muscles help bodies move, stay upright and stay protected.
  9. The digestive system
    The digestive system breaks food down so the body can use it.
  10. The heart and circulation
    The heart pumps blood around the body through blood vessels.
  11. Healthy living
    Eating well, being active, sleeping enough and keeping clean help us stay healthy.
  12. Habitats
    A habitat is the natural home of a living thing.
  13. Food chains
    A food chain shows what eats what in a habitat.
  14. Classifying living things
    Scientists classify living things into groups based on their features.
  15. Life cycles
    All living things go through a life cycle of birth, growth, reproduction and death.
  16. Evolution and inheritance
    Living things change over very long periods of time through evolution.

Key terms

Roots
The parts of a plant that hold it in the soil and take in water
Fair test
An investigation where only one thing is changed and everything else is kept the same
Germination
When a seed starts to grow, first a root and then a shoot
Pollination
The transfer of pollen from an anther to a stigma
Vertebrate
An animal with a backbone
Senses
The ways we find out about the world: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch
Incisor
A flat, sharp front tooth used for biting and cutting
Skeleton
The frame of bones that supports and protects the body
Digestion
Breaking food down into small pieces the body can use
Artery
A blood vessel that carries blood away from the heart
Balanced diet
Eating the right amounts of different types of food to stay healthy
Habitat
The natural home of a plant or animal
Producer
A green plant that makes its own food and starts a food chain
Classify
To sort living things into groups based on their features

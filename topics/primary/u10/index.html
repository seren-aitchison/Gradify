Lessons in this school

  1. Light and dark
    We need light to see things, and dark is simply the absence of light.
  2. Shadows
    Shadows form when an opaque object blocks light.
  3. How we see
    We see things because light travels in straight lines into our eyes.
  4. How sounds are made
    Sounds are made when things vibrate.
  5. Pitch and volume
    Sounds can be high or low and loud or quiet.
  6. Electrical safety
    Electricity is useful but can be very dangerous.
  7. Simple circuits
    A circuit is a complete loop that lets electricity flow.
  8. Conductors and insulators
    Some materials let electricity flow through them and others do not.
  9. Circuit symbols
    Scientists use standard symbols to draw circuit diagrams.
  10. Seasons and weather
    The weather changes from day to day and the seasons change through the year.
  11. Day and night
    Day and night happen because the Earth spins.
  12. The Sun, Earth and Moon
    The Earth orbits the Sun, and the Moon orbits the Earth.
  13. The solar system
    The solar system is the Sun and everything that orbits it.

Key terms

Light source
Something that gives out its own light, such as the Sun or a torch
Shadow
A dark area formed where an opaque object blocks light
Ray
A straight line with an arrow showing the path of light
Vibrate
To move backwards and forwards very quickly
Pitch
How high or low a sound is
Mains electricity
Electricity supplied through sockets in buildings, which is very powerful
Circuit
A complete loop that electricity can flow around
Electrical conductor
A material that lets electricity flow through it
Circuit diagram
A drawing of a circuit using standard symbols
Season
One of the four parts of the year: spring, summer, autumn and winter
Rotate
To spin around a central point or line
Orbit
The curved path of one object around another in space
Planet
A large, round object that orbits a star and has cleared its path

Lessons in this school

  1. Creative collaboration
    Most design work is done in teams, so designers need to collaborate well.
  2. Workshops and facilitation
    Workshops bring people together to explore problems, generate ideas and make decisions.
  3. Giving and receiving critique
    Critique helps designers see their work through others' eyes and improve it.
  4. Working with stakeholders
    Design projects succeed only if stakeholders understand, support and act on the work.
  5. Sketching and visual communication
    Designers communicate ideas visually to themselves, teams, clients and makers.
  6. Storytelling in design
    Stories help designers understand users and persuade others to act.
  7. Presenting and pitching
    Designers regularly present work and pitch ideas to clients, managers and investors.
  8. Design portfolios
    A portfolio shows what a designer can do and how they think.
  9. Documentation and developer handoff
    A design only becomes real when others can build it accurately.
  10. Design systems
    A design system is a shared set of reusable components, patterns, standards and guidance.
  11. Intellectual property basics
    Intellectual property rights protect creations of the mind, such as designs, inventions, brands and artworks.
  12. Copyright
    Copyright protects original creative works automatically.
  13. Registered designs
    Registered designs protect the appearance of products.
  14. Patents
    Patents protect new inventions in exchange for publishing how they work.
  15. Trade marks
    Trade marks protect the signs that identify a business's goods or services.
  16. Protecting and licensing ideas
    Designers need practical strategies to protect their work and earn from it.

Key terms

Psychological safety
A shared belief that a team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking
Facilitator
A person who guides a group through a process without imposing their own answers
Critique
A structured analysis of how well a design meets its objectives
Stakeholder
Anyone who can affect or is affected by a project
Isometric drawing
A pictorial drawing using axes at 30 degrees to the horizontal
Scenario
A short narrative of a persona using a product in a specific context
Pyramid Principle
Minto's approach of leading with the main message, followed by supporting arguments
Portfolio
A curated collection of work presented to show skills and thinking
Developer handoff
Passing designs and documentation to those who build them
Design system
A shared set of components, patterns, tokens, guidance, code and governance
Intellectual property
Creations of the mind, such as designs, inventions, brands and works, that the law allows people to own
Copyright
An automatic right protecting the expression of original creative works
Registered design
A registered right protecting the appearance of a product
Patent
A registered right protecting an invention for up to 20 years in exchange for publication

Lessons in this school

  1. Spain's regions and languages
    Spain is divided into autonomous communities, several of which have their own co-official language alongside Spanish.
  2. Festivals and traditions in Spain
    Spain is famous for its festivals, from religious processions to tomato fights.
  3. The Civil War, Franco and the transition to democracy
    The twentieth century shaped modern Spain through civil war, dictatorship and a peaceful move to democracy.
  4. Mexico: history and culture
    Mexico has the largest Spanish-speaking population in the world and a rich mix of Indigenous and Spanish heritage.
  5. The Andes and Indigenous cultures
    The Andes run through countries such as Peru, Bolivia and Ecuador, where Indigenous cultures and languages remain strong.
  6. The Southern Cone
    The Southern Cone of South America includes Argentina, Chile and Uruguay.
  7. Central America and the Caribbean
    Six Central American countries and three Caribbean territories have Spanish as a main language.
  8. Spanish in the United States
    The United States has one of the largest Spanish-speaking populations in the world.
  9. Family and society today
    Family life in Spain and Latin America has changed a great deal in recent decades.
  10. Technology and cyberspace
    Technology and social media are central GCSE topics.
  11. Equality, migration and diversity
    Spain and Latin America have long histories of migration and growing debates about equality.
  12. Art and architecture
    The Spanish-speaking world has produced some of the most famous artists and buildings in history.
  13. Literature and film in Spanish
    Spanish-language writers and directors are celebrated around the world.
  14. Music and dance
    Music and dance are central to culture across the Spanish-speaking world.
  15. Regional variation: vocabulary and pronunciation
    Spanish is spoken by more than 500 million people, and it varies between countries and regions.
  16. Voseo
    In several Latin American countries, people say vos instead of tú, with different verb forms.

Key terms

Comunidad autónoma
A self-governing region of Spain
Fiesta
A festival or celebration, often in honour of a patron saint
Guerra Civil
The Spanish Civil War, 1936 to 1939
Tenochtitlan
The Aztec capital, on the site of modern Mexico City
Tawantinsuyu
The Quechua name for the Inca Empire
Cono Sur
The Southern Cone of South America
Centroamérica
Central America, between Mexico and Colombia
Hispano / latino
A person of Spanish-speaking or Latin American origin
Apellido
Surname; Spanish speakers usually have two
Redes sociales
Social media
Emigrar
To leave your own country to live in another
Cuadro
Painting
Realismo mágico
Magical realism, mixing everyday reality with fantastic events
Cante
Flamenco singing

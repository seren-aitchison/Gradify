Lessons in this school

  1. The academic publishing system
    Academic publishing shares research findings and builds careers.
  2. Choosing a journal
    Choosing the right journal increases the chance of acceptance and reaching the right readers.
  3. Writing a journal article
    A journal article is shorter and more focused than a thesis chapter.
  4. Peer review
    Peer review is how experts evaluate research before publication.
  5. Responding to reviewers
    Responding well to reviewers greatly increases the chance of acceptance.
  6. Predatory journals
    Predatory journals exploit researchers by charging fees without proper peer review.
  7. Research metrics
    Metrics such as citations and impact factors are widely used but often misused.
  8. Conference presentations
    Presenting at conferences shares research, builds networks and develops communication skills.
  9. Academic posters
    Posters present research visually and invite conversation.
  10. Public engagement
    Public engagement involves sharing research with and learning from people outside academia.
  11. Research impact
    Research impact is the difference research makes beyond academia.
  12. The Research Excellence Framework
    The Research Excellence Framework assesses the quality of research in UK universities.
  13. Knowledge exchange and policy engagement
    Knowledge exchange connects research with organisations, practitioners and policymakers.
  14. Communicating research online
    Online communication helps researchers share work, find collaborators and reach wider audiences.

Key terms

Preprint
A version of a paper shared publicly before peer review
Scope
The topics and types of work a journal publishes
IMRaD
Introduction, methods, results and discussion structure
Single-anonymous review
Reviewers know the authors, but authors do not know the reviewers
Response letter
A letter replying to each reviewer comment and describing changes
Predatory journal
A journal that charges fees but does not provide genuine peer review or editorial services
h-index
The largest number h such that h papers have at least h citations each
Key message
The one main point the audience should remember
Poster session
A conference event in which researchers stand by their posters to discuss their work
Public engagement
Interaction between researchers and the public for mutual benefit
Research impact
An effect on, change or benefit to society beyond academia
REF
The Research Excellence Framework, the UK's research assessment system
Knowledge exchange
Two-way sharing of knowledge between universities and other organisations
The Conversation
A news site publishing articles by academics for general readers

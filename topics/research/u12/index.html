Lessons in this school

  1. Preparing to submit
    Submitting a thesis is the culmination of years of work.
  2. Examiners and the viva
    The viva is an oral examination of the thesis.
  3. Preparing for the viva
    Preparing well for the viva builds confidence and helps candidates show their expertise.
  4. In the viva
    How candidates handle the viva can make a real difference.
  5. Viva outcomes and corrections
    The viva ends with a recommendation about the thesis.
  6. Academic careers
    Many doctoral graduates consider academic careers.
  7. Postdoctoral research
    Postdoctoral posts are often the first job after a doctorate.
  8. Fellowships and grant writing
    Winning research funding is central to many research careers.
  9. Careers beyond academia
    Most doctoral graduates build careers outside academia.
  10. Transferable skills and the RDF
    The Researcher Development Framework describes the knowledge, behaviours and attributes of successful researchers.
  11. Teaching in higher education
    Many doctoral researchers teach while studying, and teaching is central to most academic roles.
  12. Networking and your research profile
    Networks and a visible research profile open doors to collaborations, jobs and opportunities.

Key terms

Intention to submit
Formal notice that a thesis will be submitted, allowing examiners to be appointed
Viva voce
An oral examination of a thesis
Mock viva
A practice viva with supervisors or colleagues
Defend
To justify research choices with reasons and evidence
Minor corrections
Small changes to be completed in a short time before the award
Postdoctoral researcher
A researcher employed on a project after completing a doctorate
Principal investigator (PI)
The lead researcher responsible for a funded project
Case for support
The main part of a grant application explaining the research and why it matters
Transferable skills
Skills that can be used in many different roles and sectors
Researcher Development Framework (RDF)
Vitae's framework describing researchers' knowledge, behaviours and attributes
Constructive alignment
Aligning learning outcomes, teaching activities and assessment
Networking
Building and maintaining professional relationships

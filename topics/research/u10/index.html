Lessons in this school

  1. Academic writing style
    Academic writing at postgraduate level is precise, evidence-based and clear.
  2. Writing habits and productivity
    Writing a thesis is a long project that depends on steady habits.
  3. Building an argument
    A thesis must make a coherent argument, not just report information.
  4. Paragraphs and signposting
    Well-structured paragraphs and signposting guide readers through complex arguments.
  5. The structure of a thesis
    Theses follow recognisable structures that vary by discipline and approach.
  6. Writing the introduction
    The introduction sets up the whole thesis.
  7. Writing the methodology chapter
    The methodology chapter explains and justifies how the research was done.
  8. Writing results and findings
    The results or findings chapter presents what the research found.
  9. Writing the discussion
    The discussion interprets findings and shows what they mean for the field.
  10. Writing the conclusion
    The conclusion draws the thesis together and leaves a lasting impression.
  11. Writing the abstract
    The abstract is often the first and only part of a thesis people read.
  12. Referencing and citation styles
    Accurate referencing gives credit, allows readers to find sources and supports your argument.
  13. Using sources and avoiding plagiarism
    Integrating sources well shows critical engagement and avoids plagiarism.
  14. Editing and revising
    Good writing comes from revision.
  15. Working with feedback
    Feedback from supervisors and peers is essential to improving a thesis.
  16. Writing in English as an additional language
    Many doctoral researchers write in English as an additional language.

Key terms

Hedging
Using cautious language to reflect the strength of evidence
Free writing
Writing continuously without stopping to edit, to generate ideas
Thesis statement
The central claim a piece of writing argues for
Signposting
Words and phrases that show the structure and direction of writing
Thesis by publication
A thesis built from published or publishable papers with linking chapters
CARS model
Swales' Create a Research Space model for introductions
Methodology
The overall approach to research and its justification
Effect size
A measure of the size of a difference or relationship
Contribution to knowledge
The original addition a study makes to its field
Recommendation
A specific action suggested by the findings
Abstract
A short summary of a whole piece of research
Citation
A reference in the text to a source
Paraphrase
Restating a source's ideas in your own words and structure
Reverse outlining
Summarising each paragraph to check a text's structure

Lessons in this school

  1. What is mixed methods research?
    Mixed methods research combines quantitative and qualitative approaches within one study or programme.
  2. Convergent designs
    In a convergent design, quantitative and qualitative data are collected and analysed separately and then merged.
  3. Explanatory sequential designs
    An explanatory sequential design starts with quantitative research and follows it with qualitative research to explain the results.
  4. Exploratory sequential designs
    An exploratory sequential design starts with qualitative research and uses the findings to build a quantitative phase.
  5. Integration and joint displays
    Integration is the defining feature of mixed methods research.
  6. Quality in mixed methods research
    Mixed methods studies must meet quality standards for each strand and for their integration.
  7. Meta-analysis
    Meta-analysis statistically combines the results of several studies to estimate an overall effect.
  8. Qualitative evidence synthesis
    Qualitative evidence synthesis brings together findings from multiple qualitative studies.
  9. Realist reviews
    Realist reviews synthesise evidence to explain how and why complex interventions work in different contexts.
  10. Secondary data analysis
    Secondary data analysis uses data collected by others to answer new questions.
  11. Big data and computational methods
    Big data and computational methods allow researchers to analyse very large, often digital, datasets.
  12. Using AI tools in research
    Generative AI tools are increasingly used in research, from searching literature to drafting text.

Key terms

Mixed methods research
Research combining and integrating qualitative and quantitative approaches
Convergent design
A design in which both strands are collected in parallel and then merged
Explanatory sequential design
A quantitative phase followed by a qualitative phase that explains its results
Exploratory sequential design
A qualitative phase followed by building and testing a quantitative feature
Integration
Intentionally bringing quantitative and qualitative strands together
GRAMMS
Six reporting guidelines for mixed methods studies
Meta-analysis
Statistical combination of results from several studies
Meta-ethnography
A method that translates concepts across qualitative studies into a new interpretation
Programme theory
An explanation of how and why an intervention is expected to work
Secondary data analysis
Analysis of data collected by others, often for different purposes
Big data
Very large, fast-moving and varied datasets
Generative AI
AI systems that produce text, images or other content in response to prompts

Five sample flashcards

Answer each from memory, then open it to check.

What is One Health?

An integrated approach that aims to balance and optimise the health of people, animals and ecosystems. From “One Health” →

Which body carries out animal disease investigation and control in Great Britain?

The Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA). From “Notifiable diseases and disease control” →

Under which Act are veterinary surgeons regulated in the UK?

The Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966. From “The RCVS and the regulation of the profession” →

When can a vet disclose confidential client information without consent?

When justified by serious risk to animal welfare or public health, a legal requirement, or the public interest. From “Clinical records and confidentiality” →

What can a suitably qualified person do?

Prescribe and supply certain veterinary medicines, such as POM-VPS products. From “Working with veterinary nurses and the wider team” →

Lessons in this school

  1. One Health
    The health of people, animals and the environment are closely linked.
  2. Zoonoses
    Zoonoses are diseases that pass between animals and people.
  3. Food safety and meat inspection
    Vets protect public health and animal welfare in the food chain.
  4. Notifiable diseases and disease control
    Controlling serious animal diseases requires legal powers, planning and rapid action.
  5. Surveillance and the role of APHA
    Surveillance detects disease threats early and shows whether control is working.
  6. Epidemiology for veterinarians
    Epidemiology studies the patterns and causes of disease in populations.
  7. The RCVS and the regulation of the profession
    The Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons regulates vets and veterinary nurses in the UK.
  8. The RCVS Code of Professional Conduct
    The RCVS Code of Professional Conduct sets the standards vets and veterinary nurses must meet.
  9. Consent and communication with clients
    Clients must give informed consent before their animals are treated.
  10. Clinical records and confidentiality
    Good records and confidentiality protect animals, clients and vets.
  11. Certification
    Vets sign many certificates, from pet travel to export health certificates.
  12. Animal welfare and the law
    Animal welfare is protected by law and assessed using scientific frameworks.
  13. Working with veterinary nurses and the wider team
    Veterinary practice depends on effective teamwork.
  14. Clinical governance and evidence-based veterinary medicine
    Clinical governance helps practices improve care, and evidence-based veterinary medicine helps vets choose the best treatments.
  15. Wellbeing and professional life
    Veterinary work is rewarding but demanding, and mental health in the profession is a serious concern.
  16. The Day One Competences
    The RCVS sets out the competences new vets need on their first day in practice.

Key terms

One Health
An integrated approach to the health of people, animals and ecosystems
Zoonosis
An infection that can pass between animals and people
Official veterinarian
A vet authorised to carry out official controls, such as in slaughterhouses
Protection zone
An area around an infected premises with the strictest controls
Passive surveillance
Surveillance based on reports and samples submitted by vets and farmers
Prevalence
The proportion of a population with a disease at a given time
RCVS
The Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, the UK regulator of vets and veterinary nurses
Under care
The relationship that must exist before a vet can prescribe POM-V medicines
Informed consent
Agreement to treatment based on understanding of risks, benefits, alternatives and costs
Confidentiality
The duty not to disclose client information without consent
Export health certificate
An official document confirming that exported animals or products meet health requirements
Duty of care
The legal duty to meet an animal's welfare needs
Registered veterinary nurse
A veterinary nurse on the RCVS register
Clinical audit
Comparing practice against standards and improving care

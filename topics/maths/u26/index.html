Lessons in this school

  1. Mathematical Reasoning
    Mathematical reasoning is the disciplined thinking that turns observations into conjectures and conjectures into proofs.
  2. Mathematical Proofs
    A mathematical proof is a convincing argument that a statement follows from definitions, axioms and accepted results.
  3. Deductive Reasoning
    Deductive reasoning draws conclusions that must be true if the premises are true.
  4. Inductive Reasoning
    Inductive reasoning generalises from examples to broader claims.
  5. Abstract Reasoning
    Abstract reasoning strips away specific details to reveal common structure, so one argument applies to many situations.
  6. Quantitative Reasoning
    Quantitative reasoning uses numbers, units, proportions and data to understand and decide.
  7. Problem Solving
    Mathematical problem solving is the art of tackling unfamiliar problems without a ready-made method.
  8. Estimation
    Estimation finds approximate answers quickly using rounding, known reference values and sensible assumptions.
  9. Approximation
    Approximation replaces exact quantities or functions by simpler ones that are close enough for the purpose.
  10. Error Propagation
    Error propagation tracks how uncertainties in measured inputs carry through a calculation into uncertainty in the result.
  11. Research Methodology
    Research methodology is the systematic approach to asking and answering questions.
  12. Literature Review
    A literature review surveys what's already known about a topic, finding the key results, methods and open questions.
  13. Hypothesis Formulation
    A hypothesis is a precise, testable statement about what you expect to find.
  14. Model Validation
    Model validation checks whether a model represents the real system well enough for its intended use.
  15. Model Verification
    Model verification checks that a computational model correctly implements its intended mathematics: the code solves the equations right.
  16. Computational Experiments
    Computational experiments use computers to explore mathematical questions, test conjectures and simulate systems.
  17. Reproducible Research
    Reproducible research means others can regenerate your results from your data and code.
  18. Mathematical Visualisation
    Mathematical visualisation turns functions, data and abstract structures into pictures that reveal patterns, guide intuition and communicate results.
  19. Mathematical Communication
    Mathematical communication shares ideas so that others can understand, check and use them.
  20. Mathematical Writing
    Mathematical writing presents definitions, results and arguments in clear, precise prose with well-chosen notation.
  21. Scientific Reporting
    Scientific reporting presents research so that others can understand, evaluate and reproduce it.

Key terms

Conjecture
A statement believed to be true, supported by evidence but not yet proved
Lemma
A smaller result proved to help prove a larger theorem
Valid argument
An argument where the conclusion must be true whenever all premises are true
Inductive generalisation
Inferring a general rule from observed cases, such as "all observed swans are white, so all swans are white"
Abstraction
Isolating the essential properties shared by many examples and studying them in general
Percentage point
An absolute difference between percentages, such as 10% to 12% being a rise of 2 percentage points (20% relative)
Heuristic
A rule of thumb that often helps find a solution, such as drawing a diagram or solving a simpler problem
Order of magnitude
The power of 10 nearest a quantity, such as 103 for 2500
Linear approximation
f(a + h) ≈ f(a) + f′(a)h for small h
Absolute uncertainty
±δx, the likely size of the error in a measured quantity x
Research question
A precise, answerable question that defines the aim of a study
Primary source
An original research article reporting new results
Falsifiable
Capable of being shown false by some possible observation or counterexample
Out-of-sample validation
Testing a model on data not used to build or fit it

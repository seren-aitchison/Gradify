Lessons in this school

  1. The GPhC registration assessment
    The registration assessment is the General Pharmaceutical Council's exam that trainees must pass to join the register.
  2. The assessment framework
    The registration assessment framework sets out what can be assessed and how much weight each area carries.
  3. Single best answer questions
    Single best answer (SBA) questions ask you to choose the most appropriate option from several plausible ones.
  4. Extended matching questions
    Extended matching questions (EMQs) share a list of options across several scenarios.
  5. Using the BNF and resources efficiently
    Fast, accurate use of the BNF and other references is essential in practice and in revision.
  6. Revision strategy
    Passing the assessment depends on steady, active revision over months rather than cramming.
  7. The foundation training year
    The foundation training year is the final stage of initial education and training: a supervised year in practice before registration.
  8. Learning outcomes and evidence
    Trainees must show they meet the GPhC learning outcomes by the end of initial training.
  9. Working with your designated supervisor
    Your designated supervisor guides, assesses and supports you through the foundation year.
  10. From trainee to registered pharmacist
    After passing the assessment and completing training, you apply to join the GPhC register.
  11. GPhC revalidation
    Registered pharmacists must revalidate every year to show they keep their practice up to date.

Key terms

Registration assessment
The GPhC's national exam taken during or after foundation training, which must be passed to register as a pharmacist
Assessment framework
The GPhC document setting out the outcomes, topics and weightings for the registration assessment
Single best answer (SBA)
A question with a scenario, a lead-in question and several options, where more than one may be partly right but one is best
Extended matching question (EMQ)
A set of questions sharing a theme and an option list, each needing the best match
BNF monograph
The BNF entry for a drug: indications, doses, cautions, interactions, side effects and more
Retrieval practice
Testing yourself to strengthen memory, rather than rereading
Foundation trainee pharmacist
A pharmacy graduate completing the supervised training year before registration
Supervised learning event
A structured activity (such as a case-based discussion or observed consultation) used to give feedback and evidence progress
Feedback
Information about performance aimed at improvement
Fitness to practise declaration
A declaration about health, character and conduct required for registration
Revalidation
The annual process for maintaining GPhC registration by submitting records of learning and reflection

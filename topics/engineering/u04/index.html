Lessons in this school

  1. Normal stress and strain
    Stress is internal force per unit area, and strain is deformation per unit length.
  2. Shear stress and strain
    Shear stress acts parallel to a surface.
  3. Hooke's law and elastic constants
    In the elastic range, stress is proportional to strain.
  4. Axially loaded members
    Bars, rods and columns under axial load stretch or shorten.
  5. Thermal stress
    Temperature changes make materials expand or contract.
  6. Torsion of circular shafts
    Shafts transmit power by twisting.
  7. Bending stress in beams
    Bending creates tension on one side of a beam and compression on the other.
  8. Shear stress in beams
    Beams carry shear force as well as bending moment.
  9. Beam deflection
    Beams must be stiff enough not to deflect too much in service.
  10. Combined loading
    Many members carry more than one type of load at once.
  11. Stress transformation and Mohr's circle
    Stresses at a point depend on the orientation of the plane considered.
  12. Failure theories
    Failure theories predict when a material will yield or fracture under combined stresses.
  13. Buckling of columns
    Slender columns can fail by buckling sideways at stresses well below their crushing strength.
  14. Thin-walled pressure vessels
    Pressure vessels such as tanks, pipes and boilers hold fluids under pressure.
  15. Strain energy
    When a material deforms elastically, it stores energy.

Key terms

Normal stress
Force per unit area acting perpendicular to a cross-section
Shear stress
Force per unit area acting parallel to a surface
Young's modulus
The ratio of normal stress to normal strain in the elastic range, a measure of stiffness
Axial deformation
Change in length of a member under axial load
Coefficient of thermal expansion
The strain per degree of temperature change
Polar second moment of area
J, a measure of a cross-section's resistance to torsion
Neutral axis
The line in a bent section where bending stress is zero, through the centroid
First moment of area
Q, the area above a point multiplied by the distance of its centroid from the neutral axis
Deflection
The vertical displacement of a beam under load
Superposition
Adding the effects of separate loads to find the combined effect
Principal stresses
The maximum and minimum normal stresses at a point, on planes with zero shear
Von Mises stress
An equivalent stress combining principal stresses, compared with yield strength
Buckling
Sudden sideways deflection of a slender member under compression
Hoop stress
Circumferential stress in a pressure vessel wall, pr/t for a cylinder

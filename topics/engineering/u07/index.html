Lessons in this school

  1. Fluid properties
    A fluid deforms continuously under shear.
  2. Pressure and manometry
    Pressure in a fluid at rest increases with depth.
  3. Hydrostatic forces on surfaces
    Dams, tanks and gates must resist the force of the fluid they hold.
  4. Buoyancy and stability
    Ships, buoys and submarines rely on buoyancy and must remain stable.
  5. Conservation of mass and continuity
    Mass is neither created nor destroyed in a flow.
  6. The Bernoulli equation
    The Bernoulli equation relates pressure, velocity and height along a streamline.
  7. The momentum equation and jet forces
    Changing the momentum of a flowing fluid requires a force.
  8. Flow measurement
    Engineers need to measure flow in pipes, channels and air streams.
  9. Viscous flow and the Reynolds number
    Flow can be smooth and layered or chaotic and mixing.
  10. Pipe flow and friction losses
    Friction in pipes causes a loss of head that pumps must overcome.
  11. Minor losses and pipe systems
    Bends, valves, entrances and exits add further losses to a pipe system.
  12. Pumps and system curves
    Pumps supply the energy to move fluid through systems.
  13. Boundary layers, drag and lift
    Bodies moving through fluids experience drag and, if shaped suitably, lift.
  14. Dimensional analysis and similitude
    Dimensional analysis reduces the number of variables in a problem and allows small-scale models to predict full-scale behaviour.

Key terms

Dynamic viscosity
The constant relating shear stress to velocity gradient
Gauge pressure
Pressure measured relative to atmospheric pressure
Centroid
The geometric centre of an area
Buoyant force
The upward force equal to the weight of displaced fluid
Volume flow rate
Volume of fluid passing a section per unit time
Total head
The sum of pressure, velocity and elevation heads
Momentum flux
The rate at which momentum is carried by a flow, ρQV
Discharge coefficient
The ratio of actual to ideal flow through a meter
Reynolds number
The dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous forces
Friction factor
A dimensionless coefficient in the Darcy–Weisbach equation
Loss coefficient
A dimensionless factor K for head loss at a fitting
Operating point
The intersection of pump and system curves
Boundary layer
A thin region near a surface where viscous effects are significant
Dimensional homogeneity
Each term in an equation having the same dimensions

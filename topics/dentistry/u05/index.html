Lessons in this school

  1. The periodontium in health
    The periodontium is the set of tissues that invest and support the teeth: gingiva, periodontal ligament, cementum and alveolar bone.
  2. Gingivitis
    Gingivitis is inflammation of the gingiva without loss of attachment.
  3. Pathogenesis of periodontitis
    Periodontitis is a chronic inflammatory disease in which a dysbiotic biofilm triggers a host response that destroys the tooth's attachment and bone.
  4. Risk factors for periodontitis
    Not everyone with plaque develops periodontitis.
  5. Periodontitis and systemic health
    Periodontitis is associated with several systemic diseases, notably diabetes, cardiovascular disease and adverse pregnancy outcomes.
  6. Periodontal examination: BPE and charting
    The Basic Periodontal Examination (BPE) is the screening tool used in UK practice to decide who needs further periodontal assessment.
  7. The 2017 classification of periodontal diseases
    The 2017 World Workshop, run by the American Academy of Periodontology and the European Federation of Periodontology, produced a new classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases.
  8. Staging and grading periodontitis
    Staging describes how severe and complex a patient's periodontitis is; grading describes how fast it has progressed and how much risk the patient carries.
  9. Necrotising periodontal diseases and periodontal abscess
    Some periodontal conditions present acutely, with pain and sometimes systemic upset.
  10. Oral hygiene instruction and behaviour change
    The patient's own daily plaque removal decides the long-term success of periodontal care.
  11. Professional mechanical plaque removal
    Professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) is the removal of supragingival and gingival plaque and calculus by the dental team, together with correction of plaque-retentive factors.
  12. Non-surgical periodontal therapy
    Non-surgical periodontal therapy is the core treatment for periodontitis.
  13. Adjuncts to periodontal therapy
    Adjuncts are treatments added to mechanical therapy in the hope of better results: antiseptics, local and systemic antimicrobials and host-modulating agents.
  14. Periodontal surgery: an overview
    When pockets persist after good non-surgical therapy, or when tissues need reshaping or regenerating, periodontal surgery may help.
  15. Supportive periodontal care
    Periodontitis is a chronic disease: once treated, it needs lifelong supportive care to keep it stable.
  16. Peri-implant health and disease
    Dental implants can develop inflammatory diseases similar to gingivitis and periodontitis: peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis.

Key terms

Supracrestal tissue attachment
The junctional epithelium and connective tissue attachment coronal to the alveolar crest, formerly called biologic width
Gingivitis
Inflammation of the gingiva without loss of connective tissue attachment
Dysbiosis
A disruption of the balanced microbial community into one that promotes disease
Risk factor
A characteristic shown in longitudinal studies to increase the probability of disease
Bacteraemia
The presence of bacteria in the bloodstream, which occurs transiently with chewing and brushing in periodontitis
BPE
Basic Periodontal Examination: a sextant-based screening code from 0 to 4 with an asterisk for furcations
Staging
Classification of periodontitis by severity and complexity of management, stages I to IV
Stage
The severity and complexity of periodontitis, I to IV, based mainly on bone loss at the worst site
Necrotising gingivitis
Acute painful gingival necrosis with punched-out papillae, bleeding and halitosis
Modified Bass technique
Brushing with bristles at about 45 degrees into the gingival sulcus using short vibratory strokes
Professional mechanical plaque removal
Removal of supragingival and gingival plaque and calculus by the dental team, with correction of retentive factors
Subgingival instrumentation
Mechanical disruption and removal of subgingival biofilm and calculus from root surfaces
Adjunct
A treatment added to mechanical periodontal therapy to enhance its effect
Intrabony defect
A vertical bony defect with walls of bone around the root, suitable for regeneration

Lessons in this school

  1. The GDC and its Standards for the Dental Team
    The General Dental Council regulates the dental team in the UK.
  2. The dental team and scope of practice
    The dental team includes several registered professions, each with defined roles.
  3. Communication and consent
    Valid consent is a legal and ethical requirement before any dental treatment.
  4. Mental capacity and consent
    Some patients may lack the capacity to make decisions about their dental care.
  5. Raising concerns and the duty of candour
    Dental professionals must speak up when patients are at risk and be honest when things go wrong.
  6. Fitness to practise
    When concerns are raised about a dental professional, the GDC may investigate their fitness to practise.
  7. Record keeping and data protection
    Good records support patient care and protect both patients and professionals.
  8. Complaints
    Handling complaints well can resolve problems and improve care.
  9. Clinical governance and audit
    Clinical governance is the framework through which dental services maintain and improve quality.
  10. Evidence-based dentistry
    Evidence-based dentistry combines research evidence with clinical expertise and patients' values.
  11. Safeguarding adults
    Dental teams may be the first to notice signs that an adult is being abused or neglected.
  12. Equality, diversity and inclusion
    Dental teams must treat everyone fairly and make care accessible.
  13. Health and safety in dental practice
    Dental practices contain many hazards, from sharps to radiation.
  14. Continuing professional development
    Dental professionals must keep their knowledge and skills up to date.
  15. Ethics in dental practice
    Dental professionals face ethical dilemmas in everyday practice.

Key terms

General Dental Council
The UK regulator of dental professionals
Scope of practice
The range of tasks a registrant group can carry out with training and competence
Valid consent
Consent that is voluntary, informed and given by a person with capacity
Capacity
The ability to make a particular decision at a particular time
Duty of candour
The duty to be open and honest with patients when things go wrong
Fitness to practise
Having the skills, knowledge, health and character to practise safely
Contemporaneous
Made at the time of the event
Complaints procedure
A practice's process for receiving and resolving complaints
Clinical governance
A framework for accountability and continuous improvement of care quality
Evidence-based dentistry
Integrating best evidence, clinical expertise and patient values in decisions
Safeguarding
Protecting people's right to live safely, free from abuse and neglect
Protected characteristic
A characteristic protected from discrimination under the Equality Act 2010
COSHH
Regulations controlling substances hazardous to health
Verifiable CPD
CPD with clear aims, objectives, outcomes, quality control and documentary evidence

Lessons in this school

  1. Data literacy
    Almost every job now involves reading, using or questioning data.
  2. Spreadsheet essentials
    Spreadsheets are the most widely used data tool at work.
  3. Descriptive statistics for work
    A few simple statistics summarise most workplace data.
  4. Charts and data visualisation
    The right chart makes a pattern obvious; the wrong one hides or distorts it.
  5. Telling a story with data
    Data persuades when it is part of a clear message.
  6. Spotting misleading statistics
    Statistics can mislead by accident or by design.
  7. Digital collaboration tools
    Teams now share files, chat and meet through digital platforms.
  8. Information management and version control
    Finding the right, current file wastes a surprising amount of working time.
  9. Cyber security at work
    Most cyber attacks on organisations exploit everyday behaviour.
  10. Data protection: UK GDPR basics
    Almost every employee handles personal data.
  11. Artificial intelligence at work
    Generative AI tools are changing how many jobs are done.
  12. Digital accessibility
    Digital content must work for everyone, including disabled people.
  13. Your online professional presence
    What people find about you online shapes how colleagues, clients and employers see you.
  14. Digital wellbeing
    Constant connection can blur work and home and wear people down.

Key terms

Data literacy
The ability to read, understand, create, communicate and question data
Absolute reference
A cell reference, written with dollar signs, that does not change when a formula is copied
Median
The middle value of sorted data
Chart junk
Decoration in a chart that does not convey information
Big idea
A one-sentence statement of your key point and why it matters
Confounder
A third factor that affects both variables and creates a misleading association
Asynchronous collaboration
Working together without needing to be online at the same time
Record
Information created or received as evidence of business activity
Phishing
Fraudulent messages designed to trick people into revealing information or installing malware
Personal data
Information relating to an identified or identifiable living person
Generative AI
AI that creates new content, such as text or images, from prompts
Alt text
A short text description of an image read by screen readers
Digital footprint
The trail of information about a person that exists online
Technostress
Stress caused by working with digital technology

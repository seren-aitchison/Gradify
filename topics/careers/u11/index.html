Lessons in this school

  1. Career planning models
    Career planning models give a structure for thinking about where you are and where you want to go.
  2. Career management skills
    Career management skills are the abilities that let you manage your own learning and work throughout life.
  3. Action planning
    An action plan turns career goals into steps you actually take.
  4. Lifelong learning and CPD
    Learning continues long after education ends.
  5. Changing career
    Many people change career at least once.
  6. The future of work and automation
    Automation and artificial intelligence are changing which tasks people do at work.
  7. Career resilience
    Career resilience is the capacity to adapt to change and recover from setbacks at work.
  8. Dealing with rejection
    Rejection hurts, and how you interpret it affects your motivation to keep going.
  9. Imposter feelings
    Many capable people feel like frauds who will be found out, especially in new roles.
  10. Uncertainty and planned happenstance
    Careers are shaped by chance as well as planning.
  11. Career wellbeing and burnout
    Burnout is a response to chronic workplace stress that has not been managed.
  12. Getting careers guidance
    Professional careers guidance is available throughout life, often for free.

Key terms

DOTS model
A framework of Decision learning, Opportunity awareness, Transition learning and Self awareness
Career management skills
Competences for managing learning, work and transitions throughout life
GROW model
A coaching structure of Goal, Reality, Options and Will
CPD
Continuing professional development, planned learning throughout a career
Test-and-learn
Exploring a career by trying small experiments and learning from them
Automation
The use of technology to perform tasks previously done by people
Career resilience
The capacity to adapt to change and recover from career setbacks
Explanatory style
The habitual way a person explains bad events, as permanent, pervasive or personal
Impostor phenomenon
Persistent feelings of being a fraud despite evidence of success
Planned happenstance
Krumboltz's idea that people can create and use unplanned opportunities
Burnout
An occupational phenomenon of exhaustion, cynicism and reduced efficacy from unmanaged chronic work stress
Careers guidance
Professional support to help people make and act on career decisions

Lessons in this school

  1. The personal financial plan
    A financial plan brings together your goals, money and investments into one picture.
  2. An investment policy statement
    An investment policy statement sets out how you will invest and why.
  3. Choosing an asset allocation
    Asset allocation, the mix of shares, bonds and cash, is the most important decision for most investors.
  4. Choosing funds for a portfolio
    Once you have an asset allocation, you need funds to put it into practice.
  5. Lump sums and regular investing
    Should you invest a large sum all at once or spread it over time?
  6. Investing early in adult life
    The years from 18 to 30 are when time is the investor's greatest asset.
  7. Mid-life: competing goals
    In mid-life, people often juggle mortgages, children, caring and retirement saving.
  8. Approaching retirement
    The ten years before retirement are a critical time for planning.
  9. Drawdown and sustainable withdrawals
    Pension drawdown lets retirees keep their pension invested and withdraw income flexibly.
  10. Annuities
    An annuity turns a pension pot into a guaranteed income for life or a set period.
  11. Reviewing and monitoring a portfolio
    A portfolio needs regular, but not constant, attention.
  12. When to get regulated financial advice
    Some decisions are complex enough that paying for advice is worthwhile.
  13. Record keeping and paperwork
    Good records make investing, tax returns and later-life planning far easier.

Key terms

Financial plan
A written plan linking goals, income, savings and investments
Investment policy statement
A written plan setting out investment objectives, constraints and strategy
Asset allocation
The mix of asset classes, such as shares, bonds and cash, in a portfolio
Ongoing charges figure
The annual cost of running a fund as a percentage of its value
Lump-sum investing
Investing a whole amount at once
Compounding
Earning returns on previous returns
Junior ISA
A tax-free savings or investment account for under-18s, accessible at 18
State Pension age
The age at which you can start receiving the State Pension
Flexi-access drawdown
A way of taking pension income while the fund remains invested
Annuity
A financial product that pays a guaranteed income, usually for life, in exchange for a lump sum
Rebalancing
Restoring a portfolio to its target asset allocation
Defined benefit transfer
Moving guaranteed pension benefits into a defined contribution arrangement
Contract note
A document confirming the details of an investment purchase or sale

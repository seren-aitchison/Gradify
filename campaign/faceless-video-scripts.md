## Production & Export Specifications for Video Creators

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio:** `1080 x 1920` (9:16 vertical video).
2. **Safe Zones:** Keep key text and captions within the central `1080 x 1400` area (leaving 250px clearance at bottom for platform UI buttons and caption overlays).
3. **Typography:** Set on-screen text in **Geist SemiBold** or **Inter Bold**, pure white (`#ffffff`) with subtle drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`).
4. **Color Grading:** Preserve Gradify's true black OLED backdrop (`#000000`). Boost saturation slightly on the brand spectrum gradient (`#4641ff` to `#ffc002`).
5. **Captions:** Dynamic kinetic subtitles (word-by-word or 2-words at a time) with active word highlighted in Gradify violet (`#8c27ca`) or electric coral (`#ff5600`).

## Building the email list (opt-in)

1. MailerLite → Forms → create an embedded or pop-up form for the group "Gradify", with **double opt-in** on.
   Consent text: "Email me about Gradify: new features, study tips and offers. Unsubscribe any time."
2. Put its link in posts and bios alongside the offer link.
3. Before collecting: `privacy.html` currently says launch-list addresses are used for one email and never for
   marketing. Add a short section on the newsletter (MailerLite as the sender, what's sent, how to unsubscribe), and
   have it reviewed.
4. Send the draft campaign (`204591722271868781`) only to people who joined through that form.

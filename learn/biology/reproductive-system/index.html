Reproductive System

The testes and ovaries produce gametes and sex hormones; the menstrual cycle is controlled by FSH, LH, oestrogen and progesterone, with ovulation around day 14, and contraception and IVF work by manipulating these hormones.

Biology · Human Anatomy and Physiology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe the male and female reproductive organs
  • Describe the hormonal control of the menstrual cycle
  • Describe methods of contraception and fertility treatment

Anatomy

  • Male: testes produce sperm and testosterone; sperm pass through the sperm duct; glands add fluid to form semen.
  • Female: ovaries produce eggs and hormones; oviducts carry eggs; the uterus supports a developing fetus.
  • Fertilisation usually occurs in the oviduct.
  • Meiosis produces haploid gametes in both sexes.

The menstrual cycle

  • Lasts about 28 days.
  • FSH stimulates a follicle to mature; the follicle releases oestrogen.
  • A surge of LH triggers ovulation around day 14.
  • Progesterone maintains the uterus lining; if no pregnancy, it falls and menstruation occurs.

Hormonal contraceptives, such as the combined pill, contain oestrogen and progesterone that inhibit FSH and LH, preventing ovulation. Barrier methods, such as condoms, also reduce the spread of sexually transmitted infections. In vitro fertilisation (IVF) uses FSH and LH to stimulate egg production; eggs are fertilised in the lab and embryos are implanted. The first IVF baby, Louise Brown, was born in 1978 in the UK.

Worked example

Hormones and ovulation.

  1. At the start of the cycle, FSH from the pituitary stimulates a follicle.
  2. Rising oestrogen thickens the uterus lining.
  3. High oestrogen triggers an LH surge.
  4. Ovulation occurs, and the follicle becomes the corpus luteum, which releases progesterone.
Common mistakeThinking the pill works mainly as a barrier. Hormonal contraceptives mainly prevent ovulation.

In 3 lines: Male and female organs. FSH, LH, oestrogen, progesterone. Contraception and IVF.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which organ produces sperm?
    • The prostate gland
    • The sperm duct
    • The testes
    • The urethra
    Show the answer

    Answer: The testes. Sperm are made in the testes by meiosis, then stored and matured nearby.

  2. How does the combined contraceptive pill prevent pregnancy?
    • It kills sperm in the uterus
    • It blocks the oviducts physically
    • It thickens the uterus lining
    • It inhibits FSH and LH so ovulation does not occur
    Show the answer

    Answer: It inhibits FSH and LH so ovulation does not occur. Oestrogen and progesterone suppress pituitary hormones.

  3. Why are FSH and LH used in IVF?
    • To stimulate the ovaries to produce several eggs
    • To prevent the ovaries releasing eggs
    • To kill unhealthy sperm
    • To make the uterus lining thinner
    Show the answer

    Answer: To stimulate the ovaries to produce several eggs. Hormones increase the number of eggs collected.

Key terms

Ovulation
Release of an egg from the ovary
FSH
Follicle-stimulating hormone
Corpus luteum
The structure formed from the follicle after ovulation

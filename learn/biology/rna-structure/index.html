RNA Structure

mRNA carries codons with a cap and tail in eukaryotes, tRNA folds into a cloverleaf with an anticodon and amino acid site, rRNA forms and catalyses in ribosomes, and other RNAs such as microRNAs regulate genes.

Biology · Genetics and Molecular Biology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe the structure of mRNA
  • Describe the structure of tRNA and how it carries amino acids
  • Describe the role and structure of rRNA in ribosomes

mRNA and tRNA

  • mRNA: a single strand carrying a copy of a gene's code as codons (three bases).
  • In eukaryotes, mRNA has a 5′ cap and a poly-A tail for stability.
  • tRNA: a single strand folded into a cloverleaf by internal base pairing.
  • Each tRNA has an anticodon that pairs with an mRNA codon and an amino acid attachment site at the 3′ end.

rRNA and other RNAs

  • rRNA combines with proteins to form ribosomes.
  • rRNA catalyses peptide bond formation: the ribosome is a ribozyme.
  • Other RNAs regulate genes, such as microRNAs, which can silence mRNAs.
  • RNA can fold into complex 3D shapes because one strand can pair with itself.

RNA's single strand and ribose 2′-hydroxyl group make it less stable than DNA, which suits temporary roles. The ability of RNA to both store information and catalyse reactions supports the RNA world hypothesis, that early life used RNA before DNA and proteins. Enzymes called aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases attach the correct amino acid to each tRNA.

Worked example

Matching tRNA to mRNA.

  1. An mRNA codon reads AUG.
  2. The tRNA anticodon UAC pairs with it.
  3. This tRNA carries methionine.
  4. So the codon AUG brings methionine to the ribosome.
Common mistakeSaying tRNA carries codons. tRNA carries anticodons; codons are on mRNA.

In 3 lines: mRNA carries codons. tRNA has anticodons. rRNA catalyses in ribosomes.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which type of RNA carries amino acids to the ribosome?
    • mRNA
    • tRNA
    • rRNA
    • microRNA
    Show the answer

    Answer: tRNA. Transfer RNA carries specific amino acids.

  2. Why is the ribosome described as a ribozyme?
    • It is made only of protein
    • It contains DNA
    • Its rRNA catalyses peptide bond formation
    • It digests RNA
    Show the answer

    Answer: Its rRNA catalyses peptide bond formation. rRNA, not protein, catalyses the key reaction.

  3. Why does RNA's ability to store information and catalyse reactions matter for theories of early life?
    • It proves DNA came first
    • It shows proteins are unnecessary today
    • It means RNA is always double stranded
    • It supports the RNA world hypothesis
    Show the answer

    Answer: It supports the RNA world hypothesis. One molecule type could have performed both roles.

Key terms

Codon
A sequence of three mRNA bases coding for an amino acid
Anticodon
A three-base sequence on tRNA complementary to a codon
Ribozyme
An RNA molecule that catalyses a reaction

Horizontal Gene Transfer

Horizontal gene transfer moves genes between bacteria by transformation, transduction and conjugation, spreading antibiotic resistance and virulence and shaping evolution, as shown first by Griffith's experiment.

Biology · Microbiology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Define horizontal gene transfer
  • Describe transformation, transduction and conjugation
  • Explain the importance of horizontal gene transfer

Three mechanisms

  • Transformation: uptake of free DNA from the environment.
  • Transduction: transfer of DNA by a bacteriophage (virus).
  • Conjugation: direct transfer, usually of a plasmid, through a pilus.
  • In contrast, vertical transfer passes genes from parent to offspring.

Importance

  • Spreads antibiotic resistance between species.
  • Spreads genes for toxins and virulence.
  • Allows rapid adaptation to new environments.
  • Has shaped bacterial evolution, making the "tree of life" more like a web.

Griffith's 1928 experiment with Streptococcus pneumoniae showed transformation: harmless bacteria became virulent after mixing with heat-killed virulent bacteria. Avery, MacLeod and McCarty showed in 1944 that DNA was the transforming substance. Horizontal gene transfer is a major reason why resistance genes, such as those for carbapenemases, spread rapidly worldwide.

Worked example

Griffith's experiment.

  1. Mice injected with live smooth (virulent) bacteria died.
  2. Mice injected with live rough (harmless) bacteria survived.
  3. Heat-killed smooth bacteria alone did not kill mice.
  4. A mixture of heat-killed smooth and live rough bacteria killed mice: rough bacteria had been transformed.
Common mistakeThinking bacteria only gain genes from parent cells. They can gain genes from other species by horizontal transfer.

In 3 lines: Transformation, transduction, conjugation. Spreads resistance. Griffith and Avery.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which mechanism uses a virus to transfer DNA?
    • Transformation
    • Conjugation
    • Binary fission
    • Transduction
    Show the answer

    Answer: Transduction. Bacteriophages carry DNA between bacteria.

  2. Why is horizontal gene transfer important for antibiotic resistance?
    • It spreads resistance genes between bacterial species
    • It stops resistance spreading between unrelated hosts
    • It only occurs in viruses
    • It removes plasmids
    Show the answer

    Answer: It spreads resistance genes between bacterial species. Resistance can jump between unrelated bacteria.

  3. What did Avery, MacLeod and McCarty show?
    • Proteins carried genetic information
    • DNA was the substance that transformed bacteria
    • Viruses cause transformation
    • RNA is the genetic material of bacteria
    Show the answer

    Answer: DNA was the substance that transformed bacteria. Destroying DNA, but not protein, stopped the transformation of bacteria.

Key terms

Transformation
Uptake of free DNA by a bacterium
Transduction
Transfer of DNA by a bacteriophage
Conjugation
Direct transfer of DNA through a pilus

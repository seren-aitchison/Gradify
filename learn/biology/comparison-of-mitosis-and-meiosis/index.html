Comparison of Mitosis and Meiosis

Mitosis involves one division without homologous pairing or crossing over and produces two identical diploid cells for growth and repair, while meiosis involves two divisions with pairing and crossing over and produces four varied haploid cells for sexual reproduction.

Biology · Cell Division and Development · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Compare the processes of mitosis and meiosis
  • Compare the products of mitosis and meiosis
  • Relate each type of division to its role in organisms

Differences in process

  • Number of divisions: mitosis one; meiosis two.
  • Pairing of homologues: only in meiosis (prophase I).
  • Crossing over: only in meiosis.
  • What separates: chromatids in mitosis; homologues then chromatids in meiosis.

Differences in products

  • Mitosis: two diploid cells, genetically identical to the parent.
  • Meiosis: four haploid cells, genetically different from each other and the parent.
  • Role of mitosis: growth, repair, asexual reproduction.
  • Role of meiosis: gamete production and genetic variation in sexual reproduction.

Both processes begin after DNA replication in S phase and share stages such as prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase. Meiosis II resembles mitosis, except that the cells are haploid. In plants, mitosis also produces gametes in the haploid gametophyte stage, so the link between meiosis and gametes varies between groups.

Worked example

Comparing products.

  1. A diploid cell with 8 chromosomes divides by mitosis.
  2. Result: two cells, each with 8 chromosomes, identical to the parent.
  3. The same type of cell divides by meiosis.
  4. Result: four cells, each with 4 chromosomes, genetically different.
Common mistakeSaying meiosis produces two cells. It produces four.

In 3 lines: One versus two divisions. Identical versus different. Growth versus gametes.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which process produces genetically identical cells?
    • Meiosis I
    • Mitosis
    • Meiosis II
    • Fertilisation
    Show the answer

    Answer: Mitosis. Mitosis gives daughter cells identical to the parent.

  2. A cell with 10 chromosomes divides by meiosis. How many chromosomes does each product have?
    • 10
    • 20
    • 5
    • 2
    Show the answer

    Answer: 5. Meiosis halves the chromosome number, so each product has 5 chromosomes.

  3. Which stage of meiosis most resembles mitosis?
    • Prophase I, with homologue pairing
    • Metaphase I, with pairs at the equator
    • Anaphase I, with homologues separating
    • Meiosis II, in which sister chromatids separate
    Show the answer

    Answer: Meiosis II, in which sister chromatids separate. Meiosis II separates chromatids as mitosis does.

Key terms

Mitosis
Nuclear division producing two identical diploid nuclei
Meiosis
Nuclear division producing four haploid nuclei
Genetic variation
Differences in alleles between individuals

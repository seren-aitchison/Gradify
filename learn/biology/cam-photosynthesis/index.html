CAM Photosynthesis

CAM plants open stomata at night to fix CO₂ into malate stored in vacuoles and close them by day, releasing CO₂ for the Calvin cycle; this temporal separation saves water but limits growth.

Biology · Plant Biology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe the CAM pathway
  • Explain how CAM saves water
  • Compare CAM with C3 and C4 photosynthesis

The pathway

  • CAM stands for crassulacean acid metabolism.
  • At night, stomata open; PEP carboxylase fixes CO₂ into malate, stored in vacuoles.
  • During the day, stomata close; malate releases CO₂ for the Calvin cycle.
  • Examples: cacti, pineapples, agave and many succulents.

Comparison

  • C4 separates the steps in space (different cells); CAM separates them in time (night and day).
  • CAM plants lose much less water because stomata open in cool night air.
  • CAM plants usually grow slowly, limited by vacuole storage.
  • Some plants switch between C3 and CAM depending on water availability.

CAM plants can use as little as a tenth of the water of C3 plants per unit of carbon fixed. Leaf acidity rises overnight as malate accumulates and falls during the day. CAM has evolved in many plant families, especially in deserts and as epiphytes, such as many orchids and bromeliads.

Worked example

A cactus over 24 hours.

  1. At night, the cactus opens its stomata in cool air.
  2. It fixes CO₂ into malate, and its tissues become acidic.
  3. During the hot day, stomata stay closed.
  4. Malate releases CO₂ for the Calvin cycle while water is conserved.
Common mistakeThinking CAM plants photosynthesise at night. They take in CO₂ at night but use light in the day.

In 3 lines: CO₂ fixed at night. Calvin cycle by day. Saves water, grows slowly.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Where do CAM plants store malate overnight?
    • In the xylem
    • In chloroplast thylakoids
    • In root hairs
    • In vacuoles
    Show the answer

    Answer: In vacuoles. Malate accumulates in the large vacuoles.

  2. How does CAM differ from C4 photosynthesis?
    • CAM separates steps in time; C4 separates them in space
    • CAM uses no RuBisCO
    • C4 opens stomata only at night
    • CAM occurs only in bundle sheath cells
    Show the answer

    Answer: CAM separates steps in time; C4 separates them in space. CAM uses night and day; C4 uses two cell types.

  3. Why does opening stomata at night save water?
    • Stomata are smaller at night
    • Cooler, more humid night air reduces evaporation
    • Plants absorb water through stomata at night
    • Night air contains no water vapour
    Show the answer

    Answer: Cooler, more humid night air reduces evaporation. The gradient for water loss is lower at night.

Key terms

CAM
Crassulacean acid metabolism, a water-saving pathway
Malate
A 4-carbon acid storing fixed CO₂
Epiphyte
A plant growing on another plant

Dihybrid Crosses

An AaBb individual produces four gamete types; AaBb × AaBb gives 9:3:3:1 and AaBb × aabb gives 1:1:1:1 when genes assort independently, while linkage, epistasis and chance cause deviations tested by chi-squared.

Biology · Genetics and Molecular Biology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Determine gametes for a dihybrid cross
  • Construct a Punnett square for a dihybrid cross
  • Explain deviations from expected dihybrid ratios

Gametes and Punnett squares

  • An AaBb individual produces four types of gamete: AB, Ab, aB, ab, in equal proportions if genes assort independently.
  • A 4 × 4 Punnett square gives 16 combinations.
  • AaBb × AaBb gives phenotypes in a 9:3:3:1 ratio.
  • AaBb × aabb (a dihybrid test cross) gives 1:1:1:1.

Deviations

  • Linked genes on the same chromosome produce more parental combinations and fewer recombinants.
  • Epistasis: one gene affects the expression of another, changing ratios such as 9:3:4.
  • Chance in small samples.
  • The chi-squared test checks whether deviations are significant.

Mendel's dihybrid crosses with seed shape and colour gave 315 round yellow, 108 round green, 101 wrinkled yellow and 32 wrinkled green, close to 9:3:3:1. Independent assortment occurs because homologous pairs line up independently at metaphase I of meiosis. Each gene alone still shows a 3:1 ratio.

Worked example

Mendel's dihybrid result.

  1. Round (R) and yellow (Y) are dominant.
  2. RrYy × RrYy gives 9 R_Y_ : 3 R_yy : 3 rrY_ : 1 rryy.
  3. Mendel observed 315 : 108 : 101 : 32.
  4. Dividing by 32 gives about 9.8 : 3.4 : 3.2 : 1, close to 9:3:3:1.
Common mistakeWriting only two gamete types for AaBb. There are four: AB, Ab, aB and ab.

In 3 lines: Four gamete types. 9:3:3:1 and 1:1:1:1. Linkage and epistasis alter ratios.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What ratio results from the dihybrid test cross AaBb × aabb?
    • 9:3:3:1
    • 3:1 only
    • All dominant
    • 1:1:1:1
    Show the answer

    Answer: 1:1:1:1. Four gamete types combine with ab to give four equal phenotypes.

  2. Which could explain far more parental combinations than expected in a dihybrid cross?
    • The two genes are linked on the same chromosome
    • The two genes are on different chromosomes
    • Both genes have codominant alleles
    • The cross was perfectly independent
    Show the answer

    Answer: The two genes are linked on the same chromosome. Linked genes are inherited together unless crossing over separates them.

  3. What does a 9:3:4 ratio in a dihybrid cross suggest?
    • Perfect independent assortment with no interaction
    • Epistasis between the two genes
    • Complete linkage of the genes
    • A mutation in every offspring
    Show the answer

    Answer: Epistasis between the two genes. Epistasis merges phenotype classes, altering 9:3:3:1.

Key terms

Dihybrid cross
A cross following two genes
Epistasis
Interaction in which one gene affects another's expression
Linked genes
Genes close together on the same chromosome

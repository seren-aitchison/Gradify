Tissue Formation

Animal tissues are epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous; cadherins and junctions hold cells together, the extracellular matrix supports them, organs combine several tissues, and loss of adhesion contributes to cancer spread.

Biology · Cell Division and Development · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe the four main types of animal tissue
  • Explain the roles of cell adhesion and the extracellular matrix
  • Explain how tissues combine to form organs

Tissue types

  • Epithelial tissue: sheets of cells covering surfaces and lining cavities, such as skin and gut lining.
  • Connective tissue: supports and binds, with cells in an extracellular matrix, such as bone, cartilage and blood.
  • Muscle tissue: contracts, including skeletal, cardiac and smooth muscle.
  • Nervous tissue: transmits signals, including neurones and glial cells.

Building tissues

  • Cell adhesion molecules, such as cadherins, hold similar cells together.
  • Cell junctions: tight junctions seal, desmosomes anchor and gap junctions allow communication.
  • The extracellular matrix (ECM), including collagen, provides support and signals.
  • Organs contain several tissue types working together, such as the stomach with epithelium, muscle, connective and nervous tissues.

Cells sort themselves according to the cadherins they express, which helps tissues form distinct boundaries during development. Loss of E-cadherin allows cancer cells to detach and invade, contributing to metastasis. Tissue engineering combines cells with scaffolds to grow replacement tissues, such as skin grafts.

Worked example

Cell sorting.

  1. Two types of embryonic cells are mixed together.
  2. Over time, cells with the same cadherins stick together.
  3. The two cell types sort into separate groups.
  4. This shows how adhesion helps form tissue boundaries.
Common mistakeTreating blood as not a tissue. Blood is a connective tissue with cells in a fluid matrix.

In 3 lines: Four tissue types. Adhesion and junctions. Tissues form organs.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which tissue type covers surfaces and lines cavities?
    • Muscle tissue
    • Nervous tissue
    • Epithelial tissue
    • Connective tissue
    Show the answer

    Answer: Epithelial tissue. Epithelia form sheets over surfaces and linings.

  2. Which cell junction allows direct communication between neighbouring cells?
    • Tight junction
    • Desmosome
    • Cadherin bridge
    • Gap junction
    Show the answer

    Answer: Gap junction. Gap junctions form channels between cytoplasms.

  3. How does loss of E-cadherin contribute to cancer spread?
    • Cancer cells detach from neighbours and invade
    • It makes cancer cells stick more tightly
    • It stops cells dividing
    • It forms new blood vessels
    Show the answer

    Answer: Cancer cells detach from neighbours and invade. Reduced adhesion lets cells leave the primary tumour.

Key terms

Tissue
A group of similar cells performing a function
Cadherin
A cell adhesion protein linking similar cells
Extracellular matrix
The material surrounding cells in tissues

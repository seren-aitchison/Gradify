Thin Lenses

A thin lens refracts light at two curved surfaces to bring rays to a focus (converging) or spread them (diverging). Lenses form images in eyes, cameras, projectors and glasses.

Physics · Optics · about 2 minutes

Types

  • Converging (convex): thicker in the middle; positive focal length; forms real images of distant objects.
  • Diverging (concave): thinner in the middle; negative focal length; virtual images only.
  • Power P = 1/f (dioptres, D): +2 D lens has f = 50 cm.
  • Thin lenses in contact combine by adding powers: P = P1 + P2.

Lensmaker's equation

1/f = (n − 1)(1/R1 − 1/R2)
  • Focal length depends on curvature and refractive index (and so on wavelength: chromatic aberration).
  • A lens's power changes in water (smaller n difference): why vision blurs underwater.

Ray rules

  • Parallel ray → through focal point.
  • Ray through centre → undeviated.
  • Ray through focal point → emerges parallel.
  • Where two of these rays meet is the image. Inside F the rays spread out and only appear to come from a point: a virtual, upright, magnified image (a magnifying glass).
Worked example

A biconvex lens (n = 1.5) with radii 20 cm and −20 cm. Focal length?

  1. 1/f = 0.5(1/20 + 1/20) = 0.05 ⇒ f = 20 cm
Common mistakeThinking a converging lens always forms a real image. An object inside F gives a virtual, upright, magnified image on the same side as the object.

Investigate with the lab. Make a real, inverted image the same size as the object with a converging lens. Then try a diverging lens.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. A −4 D lens has focal length:
    • 4 m
    • −4 cm
    • 25 cm
    • −25 cm
    Show the answer

    Answer: −25 cm. f = 1/P.

  2. Two lenses of +3 D and +2 D in contact have power:
    • +5 D
    • +6 D
    • +1 D
    • +1.2 D
    Show the answer

    Answer: +5 D. Powers add.

  3. Where must the object be for a converging lens to form a real image the same size?
    • At the focal point
    • At twice the focal length
    • Inside the focal length
    • At infinity
    Show the answer

    Answer: At twice the focal length. At u = 2f, the image forms at v = 2f with magnification 1.

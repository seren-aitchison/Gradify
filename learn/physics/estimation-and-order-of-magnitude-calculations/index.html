Estimation and Order-of-magnitude Calculations

An order-of-magnitude estimate gets the answer to within a factor of about 10 using rough inputs. It tests whether a result is sensible, guides experiment design, and is a core physicist's skill (a "Fermi problem").

Physics · Foundations of Physics · about 2 minutes

How to estimate

  • Break the problem into factors you can guess (Fermi decomposition).
  • Use round numbers and powers of ten; errors in different factors tend to cancel.
  • Keep track of units throughout.
  • Sanity-check against something known.

Useful numbers

  • Human: mass ~70 kg, height ~1.7 m, power output ~100 W, walking speed ~1.5 m s-1.
  • Earth: radius 6.4 × 106 m, mass 6 × 1024 kg, g ≈ 10 m s-2; Earth–Sun 1.5 × 1011 m.
  • Atom ~10-10 m; nucleus ~10-15 m; visible light ~5 × 10-7 m.
  • Water: density 103 kg m-3; specific heat 4.2 kJ kg-1 K-1.
Worked example

Estimate the number of air molecules in a classroom.

  1. Volume ≈ 10 m × 8 m × 3 m ≈ 240 m3
  2. At room conditions 1 mol occupies ~0.024 m3, so n ≈ 104 mol
  3. N ≈ 104 × 6 × 1023 ≈ 6 × 1027 molecules

Order of magnitude

The order of magnitude of x is the power of ten nearest to it (often taken as round(log10 x)). Two quantities "differ by three orders of magnitude" when their ratio is about 1000.

Common mistakeDon't give an estimate to several significant figures. "About 6 × 1027" is honest; "5.97 × 1027" is false precision.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Roughly how many seconds are in a year?
    • 3 × 105
    • 9 × 109
    • 3 × 107
    • 5 × 106
    Show the answer

    Answer: 3 × 107. 365 × 24 × 3600 ≈ 3.2 × 107 s.

  2. Estimate the kinetic energy of a sprinter at top speed.
    • ~40 J
    • ~400 kJ
    • ~4 MJ
    • ~4 kJ
    Show the answer

    Answer: ~4 kJ. ½ × 70 kg × (10 m s-1)2 ≈ 3.5 kJ.

  3. About how many atoms are in a human body?
    • 1027 to 1028
    • 1023
    • 1033
    • 1018
    Show the answer

    Answer: 1027 to 1028. ~70 kg mostly water (~0.018 kg mol-1, 3 atoms per molecule): ~4000 mol × 3 × 6 × 1023 ≈ 7 × 1027.

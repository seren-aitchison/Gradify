Unit Conversions and Prefixes

SI prefixes scale units by powers of ten; conversions multiply by factors equal to one. Doing this carefully, with units written in, removes a whole class of errors.

Physics · Foundations of Physics · about 2 minutes

The prefixes to know

  • quecto q 10-30, ronto r 10-27, yocto y 10-24, zepto z 10-21, atto a 10-18, femto f 10-15, pico p 10-12, nano n 10-9, micro μ 10-6, milli m 10-3
  • kilo k 103, mega M 106, giga G 109, tera T 1012, peta P 1015, exa E 1018, zetta Z 1021, yotta Y 1024, ronna R 1027, quetta Q 1030
  • centi (10-2), deci (10-1) and hecto (102) are used but not in the 103n pattern.

Conversion factors

Write the conversion as a fraction equal to one and multiply, cancelling units like algebra.

Worked example

Convert 72 km h-1 to m s-1.

  1. 72 km h-1 × (1000 m / 1 km) × (1 h / 3600 s)
  2. = 72 000 / 3600 m s-1 = 20 m s-1

Powers of units

A prefix applies to the unit before any power: 1 cm3 = (10-2 m)3 = 10-6 m3, not 10-2 m3. So 1 m3 = 106 cm3 = 1000 litres.

  • Energy: 1 eV = 1.602 × 10-19 J; 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ; 1 cal = 4.184 J.
  • Angle: π rad = 180°. Pressure: 1 atm = 101 325 Pa; 1 bar = 105 Pa.
Common mistakeSquared and cubed units trip people up: 1 mm2 = 10-6 m2, not 10-3 m2.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What is 4.7 μF in farads?
    • 4.7 × 10-3 F
    • 4.7 × 10-6 F
    • 4.7 × 10-9 F
    • 4.7 × 106 F
    Show the answer

    Answer: 4.7 × 10-6 F. Micro means 10-6.

  2. A wire has a cross-section of 0.50 mm2. What is it in m2?
    • 5.0 × 10-4 m2
    • 5.0 × 10-3 m2
    • 5.0 × 10-7 m2
    • 5.0 × 10-10 m2
    Show the answer

    Answer: 5.0 × 10-7 m2. 1 mm2 = (10-3 m)2 = 10-6 m2, so 0.50 mm2 = 5.0 × 10-7 m2.

  3. What is a density of 1.0 g cm-3 in kg m-3?
    • 1.0 kg m-3
    • 0.001 kg m-3
    • 100 kg m-3
    • 1000 kg m-3
    Show the answer

    Answer: 1000 kg m-3. g → kg divides by 103; cm-3 → m-3 multiplies by 106: net × 103.

Scalar and Vector Quantities

A scalar has magnitude only; a vector has magnitude and direction and adds by the parallelogram rule. Deciding which a quantity is determines how it combines with others.

Physics · Foundations of Physics · about 2 minutes

Examples

  • Scalars: mass, time, temperature, energy, power, charge, speed, distance, pressure, potential.
  • Vectors: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, momentum, electric and magnetic fields, torque, angular momentum.
  • Some scalars can be negative (charge, potential, work); negative does not mean "vector".

Adding vectors

R = A + B; |R|2 = A2 + B2 + 2AB cos θθ is the angle between A and B

Resolve into perpendicular components and add those: Rx = Ax + Bx, Ry = Ay + By; then |R| = √(Rx2 + Ry2) and tan φ = Ry/Rx.

Worked example

A boat heads north at 4.0 m s-1 across a river flowing east at 3.0 m s-1. Find its velocity over the ground.

  1. Components are perpendicular: |v| = √(4.02 + 3.02) = 5.0 m s-1
  2. Direction: tan φ = 3.0/4.0, φ = 37° east of north

Beyond vectors

Under rotation of axes, scalars stay the same and vector components mix in a fixed way. Quantities like the stress or the moment of inertia need two indices: they are rank-2 tensors. Pseudovectors (torque, angular momentum, B) behave like vectors under rotation but don't flip under reflection.

Common mistakeSpeed and velocity are not interchangeable: average speed over a round trip is positive, but average velocity is zero.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which is a vector?
    • Momentum
    • Kinetic energy
    • Temperature
    • Power
    Show the answer

    Answer: Momentum. Momentum p = mv has the direction of v.

  2. Forces of 6 N and 8 N act at 90° to each other. What is the size of the resultant?
    • 14 N
    • 10 N
    • 2 N
    • 48 N
    Show the answer

    Answer: 10 N. √(62 + 82) = 10 N.

  3. Two forces of 5 N give a resultant of 5 N. What is the angle between them?
    • 60°
    • 90°
    • 120°
    • 45°
    Show the answer

    Answer: 120°. R2 = 25 + 25 + 50 cos θ = 25, so cos θ = −1/2 and θ = 120°.

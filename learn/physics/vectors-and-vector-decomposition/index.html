Vectors and Vector Decomposition

Vectors are added, scaled and multiplied using components. Resolving a vector into perpendicular components turns 2D and 3D problems into independent 1D ones.

Physics · Mathematics for Physics · about 2 minutes

Components

A = Axi + Ayj + Azk; |A| = √(Ax2 + Ay2 + Az2)
Ax = A cos θ, Ay = A sin θθ measured from the x-axis

Products

A·B = AB cos θ = AxBx + AyBy + AzBzscalar (dot) product
|A × B| = AB sin θ, direction by the right-hand rulevector (cross) product
  • Dot product: work W = F·s, power P = F·v, flux Φ = B·A. Zero when perpendicular.
  • Cross product: torque τ = r × F, angular momentum L = r × p, force F = qv × B. Zero when parallel.
  • The component of A along unit vector n̂ is A·n̂.
Worked example

A 50 N force acts at 30° above the horizontal on a crate moving 4.0 m horizontally. Find the work done.

  1. Horizontal component = 50 cos 30° = 43.3 N
  2. W = 43.3 × 4.0 = 173 J ≈ 170 J (the vertical component does no work)

Choosing axes

Choose axes to make components simple: along and perpendicular to a slope, along the motion, or along a symmetry axis. The physics doesn't depend on the choice, only the algebra does.

Common mistakeThe cross product is anticommutative: A × B = −B × A. Order matters for torque and the magnetic force.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What is (2, 3, 0)·(4, −1, 5)?
    • 5
    • 8
    • 13
    • 3
    Show the answer

    Answer: 5. 8 − 3 + 0 = 5.

  2. What is i × j?
    • −k
    • k
    • 0
    • 1
    Show the answer

    Answer: k. Right-handed axes: i × j = k.

  3. A block sits on a 30° slope. What fraction of its weight acts down the slope?
    • cos 30° = 0.87
    • tan 30° = 0.58
    • sin 30° = 0.5
    • 1
    Show the answer

    Answer: sin 30° = 0.5. The component along the slope is mg sin θ.

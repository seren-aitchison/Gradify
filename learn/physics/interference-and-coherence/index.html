Interference and Coherence

Interference needs coherence: a stable phase relationship between waves. Temporal coherence describes how long a wave stays in phase with itself; spatial coherence describes how phase is correlated across a beam.

Physics · Optics · about 2 minutes

Temporal coherence

lc ≈ λ2/Δλ = c/Δfcoherence length
  • White light: ~1 μm (thin films only). LED: ~10 μm. Sodium lamp: ~1 mm. HeNe laser: ~0.1–1 m. Stabilised lasers: kilometres.
  • Path differences must be shorter than lc for fringes.

Spatial coherence

dc ≈ λL/scoherence width from a source of size s at distance L
  • Starlight is spatially coherent over metres because stars are tiny in angle; Michelson measured Betelgeuse's diameter this way (1920).

Fringe visibility

V = (Imax − Imin)/(Imax + Imin)
  • V = 1 fully coherent; 0 incoherent.
  • Hanbury Brown–Twiss intensity interferometry measures star sizes from intensity correlations.
Worked example

Coherence length of an LED with λ = 630 nm, Δλ = 20 nm?

  1. lc = (630 × 10-9)2/(20 × 10-9) ≈ 20 μm
Common mistakeTwo independent lasers can interfere briefly (beats), but their relative phase drifts; for stable fringes, use beams from one source.

Investigate with the lab. Compare the fringes for different slit separations and screen distances, and note that clear fringes need a steady phase relationship between the slits.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. A narrower spectral linewidth gives:
    • Shorter coherence length
    • No change
    • Longer coherence length
    • Lower intensity
    Show the answer

    Answer: Longer coherence length. lc ∝ 1/Δλ.

  2. Fringe visibility of 0 means:
    • Perfect fringes
    • Maximum intensity
    • Monochromatic light
    • No interference fringes
    Show the answer

    Answer: No interference fringes. Incoherent.

  3. Why must the two sources in an interference experiment be coherent?
    • They need a constant phase difference for a stable pattern
    • They must have different frequencies
    • They must be very bright
    • They must be at different distances from the screen
    Show the answer

    Answer: They need a constant phase difference for a stable pattern. Without a constant phase difference, the fringes move too fast to see.

Ksp

Ksp = product of ion concentrations raised to their formula powers in a saturated solution; it gives solubility through expressions such as s² or 4s³, and comparing the ionic product Q with Ksp predicts precipitation.

Chemistry · Acids, Bases and Aqueous Chemistry · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Write Ksp expressions
  • Calculate solubility from Ksp and vice versa
  • Use ionic products to predict precipitation

Ksp

  • For MₓAᵧ(s) ⇌ xMʸ⁺ + yAˣ⁻: Ksp = [Mʸ⁺]ˣ[Aˣ⁻]ʸ.
  • The solid is omitted.
  • Smaller Ksp means lower solubility for salts of the same formula type.
  • Ksp depends only on temperature.

Calculations

  • For AgCl: solubility s gives Ksp = s².
  • For PbI₂: Ksp = s(2s)² = 4s³.
  • The ionic product Q uses actual concentrations.
  • If Q > Ksp, a precipitate forms; if Q < Ksp, no precipitate.

Comparing Ksp values directly only works for salts with the same formula type, because the relationship between Ksp and solubility depends on the formula. Ksp values guide selective precipitation, separating ions in analysis.

Worked example

Will a precipitate form?

  1. Ksp of AgCl = 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰ mol² dm⁻⁶.
  2. A mixture has [Ag⁺] = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ and [Cl⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol dm⁻³.
  3. Q = (1.0 × 10⁻⁴)² = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸.
  4. Q > Ksp, so AgCl precipitates.
Common mistakeComparing Ksp values of salts with different formula types to rank solubility. The relationship depends on formula.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. For AgBr with solubility s, what is Ksp?
    • 4s³
    • 2s
    • s
    • s²
    Show the answer

    Answer: s². Each mole dissolving gives s of Ag⁺ and s of Br⁻, so Ksp = s × s = s².

  2. Why can't Ksp values of AgCl and PbI₂ be compared directly?
    • They have different formula types
    • Both have identical solubility
    • Ksp does not exist for PbI₂
    • Ksp only applies to AgCl
    Show the answer

    Answer: They have different formula types. The link between Ksp and solubility differs.

  3. How is selective precipitation used in analysis?
    • All ions precipitate at once
    • Ions with smaller Ksp precipitate first as a reagent is added
    • Ksp values are ignored
    • It only works for gases
    Show the answer

    Answer: Ions with smaller Ksp precipitate first as a reagent is added. Different Ksp values allow stepwise separation.

Lewis Acid-base Theory

Lewis acids accept electron pairs and Lewis bases donate them, forming dative bonds; this broadest theory covers reactions without protons, such as BF₃ with NH₃ and metal complexes, and includes all Brønsted-Lowry bases.

Chemistry · Acids, Bases and Aqueous Chemistry · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • State the Lewis definitions of acid and base
  • Identify Lewis acids and bases in reactions
  • Compare Lewis, Brønsted-Lowry and Arrhenius theories

Definitions

  • A Lewis acid is an electron pair acceptor.
  • A Lewis base is an electron pair donor.
  • Reaction forms a dative covalent bond.
  • No protons need be involved.

Examples

  • BF₃ + NH₃ → F₃B←NH₃: BF₃ accepts, NH₃ donates.
  • Metal ions in complexes act as Lewis acids; ligands are Lewis bases.
  • H⁺ is a Lewis acid; OH⁻ is a Lewis base.
  • AlCl₃ acts as a Lewis acid catalyst in Friedel-Crafts reactions.

Every Brønsted-Lowry base is also a Lewis base, because accepting a proton requires donating an electron pair. Lewis theory is the broadest, explaining reactions without protons, such as metal complex formation. G. N. Lewis proposed it in 1923.

Worked example

BF₃ and ammonia.

  1. Boron in BF₃ has only six outer electrons and an empty orbital.
  2. Nitrogen in NH₃ has a lone pair.
  3. The lone pair forms a dative bond to boron.
  4. BF₃ is the Lewis acid and NH₃ the Lewis base.
Common mistakeThinking Lewis acids must contain hydrogen. BF₃ and metal ions are Lewis acids without hydrogen.

In 3 lines: Electron pair acceptors and donors. Dative bonds. Broadest theory.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which is a Lewis acid?
    • Ammonia, NH₃
    • Water, H₂O
    • Boron trifluoride, BF₃
    • The hydroxide ion
    Show the answer

    Answer: Boron trifluoride, BF₃. BF₃ has an empty orbital to accept a pair.

  2. Why are all Brønsted-Lowry bases also Lewis bases?
    • They all contain hydroxide
    • They all accept electron pairs
    • Lewis bases never accept protons
    • Accepting a proton requires donating an electron pair
    Show the answer

    Answer: Accepting a proton requires donating an electron pair. Bonding to H⁺ uses a lone pair from the base.

  3. Why is Lewis theory considered the broadest?
    • It covers reactions without any proton transfer
    • It only applies to water
    • It ignores electron pairs
    • It defines only strong acids
    Show the answer

    Answer: It covers reactions without any proton transfer. Electron pair donation describes far more reactions.

Key terms

Lewis acid
An electron pair acceptor
Lewis base
An electron pair donor
Adduct
A product formed by Lewis acid and base combining

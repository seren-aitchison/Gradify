Hess's Law

Hess's law states that enthalpy change is independent of route, allowing unmeasurable enthalpy changes to be found from cycles using formation data (products minus reactants) or combustion data (reactants minus products).

Chemistry · Chemical Energetics and Thermodynamics · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • State Hess's law
  • Construct Hess cycles using formation and combustion data
  • Calculate enthalpy changes that cannot be measured directly

Hess's law

  • The enthalpy change for a reaction is the same regardless of route, provided start and end are the same.
  • It follows from conservation of energy.
  • It lets chemists calculate enthalpy changes that cannot be measured.
  • Routes can be drawn as Hess cycles.

Using cycles

  • Formation route: ΔrH = ΣΔfH(products) − ΣΔfH(reactants).
  • Combustion route: ΔrH = ΣΔcH(reactants) − ΣΔcH(products).
  • Arrows in the direction of a step use +ΔH; against it use −ΔH.
  • Multiply by coefficients.

Germain Hess published the law in 1840. It allows the enthalpy of formation of methane, which cannot be made directly from carbon and hydrogen, to be calculated from combustion data. Born-Haber cycles are an application of Hess's law.

Worked example

Formation of methane from combustion data.

  1. ΔcH: C −394, H₂ −286, CH₄ −891 kJ mol⁻¹.
  2. Target: C + 2H₂ → CH₄.
  3. ΔfH = [−394 + 2(−286)] − (−891).
  4. ΔfH = −966 + 891 = −75 kJ mol⁻¹.
Common mistakeMixing up the formation and combustion formulae. For combustion data, it is reactants minus products.

In 3 lines: Route does not matter. Formation and combustion routes. Calculate the unmeasurable.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What principle is Hess's law based on?
    • Conservation of charge
    • Le Chatelier's principle
    • Conservation of energy
    • The octet rule
    Show the answer

    Answer: Conservation of energy. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, so routes must agree.

  2. Why can't the enthalpy of formation of methane be measured directly?
    • Methane cannot be made at all
    • Methane has no enthalpy
    • Carbon is not an element
    • Carbon and hydrogen do not react cleanly to form only methane
    Show the answer

    Answer: Carbon and hydrogen do not react cleanly to form only methane. Side reactions and slow rates prevent direct measurement.

  3. In a Hess cycle, why is ΔH reversed when going against an arrow?
    • Reversing a reaction changes the sign of its enthalpy change
    • Arrows show reaction speed
    • ΔH is always positive
    • Cycles ignore direction
    Show the answer

    Answer: Reversing a reaction changes the sign of its enthalpy change. Travelling backwards undoes the energy change.

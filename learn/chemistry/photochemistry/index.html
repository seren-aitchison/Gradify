Photochemistry

Photochemistry follows the Grotthuss-Draper and Stark-Einstein laws; quantum yield measures efficiency and can exceed 1 for chains, and Jablonski diagrams show fluorescence, intersystem crossing and phosphorescence.

Chemistry · Physical Chemistry and Molecular Science · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • State the laws of photochemistry
  • Describe processes of excited states using a Jablonski diagram
  • Calculate quantum yield

Principles

  • Grotthuss-Draper law: only absorbed light causes reaction.
  • Stark-Einstein law: each absorbed photon excites one molecule.
  • Quantum yield Φ = molecules reacted ÷ photons absorbed.
  • Chain reactions can have Φ much greater than 1.

Excited states

  • A Jablonski diagram shows singlet (S) and triplet (T) states.
  • Paths: fluorescence (S₁ → S₀), intersystem crossing (S₁ → T₁), phosphorescence (T₁ → S₀).
  • Excited molecules can react, transfer energy or lose it as heat.
  • Photosensitisers transfer energy to other molecules.

Photosynthesis, vision, sunburn, ozone formation and photographic film all depend on photochemistry. The H₂ + Cl₂ reaction in light has a quantum yield up to 10⁶ because of a chain mechanism. Photodynamic therapy uses photosensitisers to kill cancer cells.

Worked example

Quantum yield of a chain reaction.

  1. 1.0 × 10⁻⁹ mol of photons is absorbed.
  2. 1.0 × 10⁻³ mol of HCl forms.
  3. Φ = 10⁻³ ÷ 10⁻⁹ = 10⁶.
  4. Each photon starts a long chain.
Common mistakeThinking each photon can only cause one reaction. Chain reactions multiply the effect.

In 3 lines: Two laws. Quantum yield. Jablonski diagram.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which emission comes from a triplet state?
    • Fluorescence
    • Rayleigh scattering
    • Phosphorescence
    • Absorption
    Show the answer

    Answer: Phosphorescence. T₁ → S₀ is spin forbidden, so it is slow.

  2. Why can a quantum yield be greater than 1?
    • Photons can be absorbed twice
    • Quantum yields are always 1
    • Light creates extra molecules
    • One photon can start a chain reaction producing many molecules
    Show the answer

    Answer: One photon can start a chain reaction producing many molecules. Each initiation step leads to many propagation steps.

  3. How does photodynamic therapy work?
    • A photosensitiser absorbs light and produces reactive oxygen that kills target cells
    • Light alone heats tumours
    • It uses X-rays only
    • The drug works without light
    Show the answer

    Answer: A photosensitiser absorbs light and produces reactive oxygen that kills target cells. Energy transfer creates singlet oxygen locally.

Key terms

Quantum yield
Molecules reacted per photon absorbed
Intersystem crossing
Transition between singlet and triplet states
Photosensitiser
A molecule that absorbs light and transfers energy

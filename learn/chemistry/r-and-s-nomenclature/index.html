R and S Nomenclature

CIP rules rank groups by atomic number, comparing outward when tied and counting double bonds twice; with the lowest priority pointing away, clockwise 1 → 2 → 3 is R and anticlockwise is S.

Chemistry · Organic Chemistry Foundations · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Assign priorities using the CIP rules
  • Assign R or S configuration to a stereocentre
  • Name compounds with stereocentres

Priority rules

  • Rank the four groups by atomic number of the attached atom: higher number, higher priority.
  • If tied, compare the next atoms out until a difference appears.
  • Double bonds count as two single bonds to the same atom.
  • Example priorities: Br > Cl > OH > NH₂ > CH₃ > H.

Assigning R or S

  • Point the lowest-priority group away from you.
  • Trace 1 → 2 → 3.
  • Clockwise = R (rectus); anticlockwise = S (sinister).
  • If the lowest group points towards you, reverse the answer.

R/S nomenclature describes the actual 3D arrangement around a stereocentre, unlike (+) and (−), which describe light rotation. Names include the descriptor, such as (R)-butan-2-ol. Molecules with several stereocentres list each one, such as (2R,3S).

Worked example

Assigning (R)- or (S)-butan-2-ol.

  1. Groups on C2: OH, C₂H₅, CH₃, H.
  2. Priorities: OH (1) > C₂H₅ (2) > CH₃ (3) > H (4).
  3. With H pointing back, 1 → 2 → 3 traces clockwise.
  4. The configuration is R: (R)-butan-2-ol.
Common mistakeForgetting to reverse the answer when the lowest-priority group points towards you.

In 3 lines: Rank by atomic number. Lowest away. Clockwise R.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which group has the highest CIP priority?
    • –OH
    • –CH₃
    • –Br
    • –H
    Show the answer

    Answer: –Br. Bromine has the highest atomic number of the attached atoms.

  2. What should you do if the lowest-priority group points towards you?
    • Ignore it and keep the answer
    • Always assign the label R
    • Always assign the label S
    • Reverse the R or S answer you trace
    Show the answer

    Answer: Reverse the R or S answer you trace. Viewing from the wrong side reverses the apparent direction.

  3. Why does –CH₂OH outrank –CH(CH₃)₂?
    • At the first point of difference, O beats C
    • Isopropyl has more hydrogens
    • –CH₂OH contains more carbons
    • Isopropyl has a higher molecular mass
    Show the answer

    Answer: At the first point of difference, O beats C. Compare attached atom sets: (O,H,H) beats (C,C,H).

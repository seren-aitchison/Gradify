X-ray Diffraction

Crystals diffract X-rays when Bragg's law nλ = 2d sin θ is met; single-crystal diffraction gives atomic positions and bond lengths, powder diffraction identifies phases and X-rays scatter from electrons, so hydrogen is hard to see.

Chemistry · Analytical Chemistry and Spectroscopy · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Explain how crystals diffract X-rays
  • Apply the Bragg equation
  • Describe what X-ray crystallography reveals

Diffraction

  • X-ray wavelengths (about 0.1 nm) are similar to atomic spacings.
  • Planes of atoms in a crystal diffract X-rays.
  • Constructive interference occurs when nλ = 2d sin θ (Bragg's law).
  • The pattern of spots depends on the crystal structure.

What it shows

  • Single-crystal diffraction gives exact atomic positions, bond lengths and angles.
  • Powder diffraction identifies crystalline phases.
  • X-rays scatter from electrons, so heavy atoms show most strongly.
  • Hydrogen atoms are hard to locate.

X-ray crystallography revealed the structures of DNA, proteins such as myoglobin and many medicines. Dorothy Hodgkin determined the structures of penicillin, vitamin B12 and insulin. Powder diffraction is used to identify minerals and check drug crystal forms.

Worked example

Using Bragg's law.

  1. X-rays of λ = 0.154 nm diffract at θ = 15.0° (n = 1).
  2. d = nλ ÷ (2 sin θ).
  3. d = 0.154 ÷ (2 × 0.259) = 0.297 nm.
  4. The spacing between atomic planes is about 0.30 nm.
Common mistakeThinking X-ray diffraction images individual atoms directly. It records a pattern that must be computed into a structure.

In 3 lines: X-rays meet crystal planes. Bragg's law. Atomic structures.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What does single-crystal X-ray diffraction reveal?
    • Only the colour of the crystal
    • The molecular mass directly
    • The number of hydrogen environments
    • Exact atomic positions, bond lengths and angles
    Show the answer

    Answer: Exact atomic positions, bond lengths and angles. Diffraction data are converted into a 3D electron density map.

  2. Why are X-rays used rather than visible light?
    • Their wavelength is similar to the spacing between atoms
    • They are cheaper than visible light
    • Visible light is diffracted more strongly by atoms
    • X-rays have very long wavelengths
    Show the answer

    Answer: Their wavelength is similar to the spacing between atoms. Diffraction needs wavelength close to the spacing.

  3. How does powder diffraction help the pharmaceutical industry?
    • It measures drug colour
    • It identifies which crystal form of a drug is present
    • It replaces clinical trials
    • It only works on liquids
    Show the answer

    Answer: It identifies which crystal form of a drug is present. Different polymorphs give different patterns and properties.

Key terms

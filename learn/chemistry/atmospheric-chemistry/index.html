Atmospheric Chemistry

The atmosphere has a lower troposphere and an upper stratosphere containing ozone, formed and broken down in the Chapman cycle while absorbing UV; radicals such as •OH clean the air and chlorine radicals destroy stratospheric ozone.

Chemistry · Materials, Environmental and Industrial Chemistry · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe the layers and composition of the atmosphere
  • Explain the formation and destruction of stratospheric ozone
  • Describe the role of radicals in atmospheric chemistry

Structure and composition

  • Troposphere: lowest layer, weather, most of the mass.
  • Stratosphere: contains the ozone layer.
  • Dry air: about 78% N₂, 21% O₂, 0.9% Ar, 0.04% CO₂.
  • Trace gases have large effects.

Ozone and radicals

  • Chapman cycle: O₂ + UV → 2O; O + O₂ → O₃; O₃ + UV → O₂ + O.
  • Ozone absorbs harmful UV-B and UV-C.
  • The hydroxyl radical (•OH) is the atmosphere's "detergent", oxidising pollutants.
  • Chlorine radicals catalytically destroy ozone.

The ozone hole over Antarctica forms in spring because polar stratospheric clouds release reactive chlorine. Ground-level ozone, however, is a harmful pollutant formed in smog. The hydroxyl radical controls the lifetime of methane and many other gases.

Worked example

The Chapman cycle.

  1. UV splits O₂ into two oxygen atoms.
  2. An O atom joins O₂ to form O₃.
  3. O₃ absorbs UV and splits again.
  4. A steady state keeps ozone levels balanced.
Common mistakeThinking all ozone is beneficial. Ground-level ozone is a harmful pollutant.

In 3 lines: Layers and composition. Chapman cycle. Radicals.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which gas makes up about 78% of dry air?
    • Oxygen
    • Argon
    • Nitrogen
    • Carbon dioxide
    Show the answer

    Answer: Nitrogen. N₂ is by far the most abundant atmospheric gas.

  2. Why is the ozone hole worst over Antarctica in spring?
    • Antarctica has the most factories
    • Ozone is only made in summer
    • Cold air destroys ozone directly
    • Polar stratospheric clouds release reactive chlorine when sunlight returns
    Show the answer

    Answer: Polar stratospheric clouds release reactive chlorine when sunlight returns. Ice cloud surfaces activate chlorine reservoirs.

  3. Why is ground-level ozone harmful while stratospheric ozone is helpful?
    • At ground level it damages lungs and plants; high up it blocks UV
    • Ozone is identical in its effects everywhere
    • Stratospheric ozone is toxic
    • Ground ozone blocks UV
    Show the answer

    Answer: At ground level it damages lungs and plants; high up it blocks UV. Location decides whether ozone harms or protects.

Key terms

Troposphere
The lowest layer of the atmosphere
Stratosphere
The layer above the troposphere containing ozone
Hydroxyl radical
•OH, the main atmospheric oxidant

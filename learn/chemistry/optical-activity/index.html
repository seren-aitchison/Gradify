Optical Activity

Chiral compounds rotate plane-polarised light, measured with a polarimeter; specific rotation corrects for path length and concentration, enantiomeric excess compares observed rotation with that of the pure enantiomer and the sign of rotation does not give R or S.

Chemistry · Organic Chemistry Foundations · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe plane-polarised light and its rotation
  • Calculate specific rotation
  • Calculate enantiomeric excess from optical rotation

Measurement

  • A polarimeter passes plane-polarised light through a sample.
  • Chiral samples rotate the plane by an angle α.
  • Dextrorotatory (+) rotates clockwise; laevorotatory (−) anticlockwise.
  • Specific rotation [α] = α / (l × c), with l in dm and c in g cm⁻³.

Enantiomeric excess

  • ee = (observed [α] / [α] of pure enantiomer) × 100%.
  • A racemate has ee = 0%; a pure enantiomer 100%.
  • ee measures the success of asymmetric synthesis.
  • The sign of rotation does not reveal R or S configuration.

Biot discovered optical activity in the early 1800s, and Pasteur separated tartrate crystals by hand in 1848, showing that molecules can be chiral. Sugar concentrations in industry are measured by polarimetry.

Worked example

Calculating ee.

  1. Pure (+)-enantiomer: [α] = +40°.
  2. Sample: [α] = +30°.
  3. ee = 30 / 40 × 100 = 75%.
  4. The sample is 87.5% (+) and 12.5% (−).
Common mistakeAssuming (+) always means R configuration. The direction of rotation is unrelated to the R/S label.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What does dextrorotatory mean?
    • Rotating plane-polarised light anticlockwise
    • Absorbing all polarised light
    • Having no effect on light
    • Rotating plane-polarised light clockwise
    Show the answer

    Answer: Rotating plane-polarised light clockwise. Dextrorotatory compounds are labelled (+).

  2. Why does the sign of rotation not tell you R or S?
    • R/S is a naming rule, while rotation is a measured physical property
    • All R compounds rotate light anticlockwise
    • All S compounds are dextrorotatory
    • Rotation depends only on colour
    Show the answer

    Answer: R/S is a naming rule, while rotation is a measured physical property. The two are not linked by any simple rule.

  3. Why does enantiomeric excess matter in drug synthesis?
    • It measures the colour of the drug
    • It shows how much of the desired enantiomer the product contains
    • It shows the drug's melting point
    • It has no use in medicine
    Show the answer

    Answer: It shows how much of the desired enantiomer the product contains. High ee means little of the unwanted enantiomer remains.

Key terms

Polarimeter
An instrument that measures optical rotation
Specific rotation
Rotation corrected for path length and concentration
Enantiomeric excess
Excess of one enantiomer as a percentage

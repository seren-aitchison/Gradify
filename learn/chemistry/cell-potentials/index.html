Cell Potentials

E⦵cell = E⦵(cathode) − E⦵(anode), with positive values meaning feasible reactions; ΔG⦵ = −nFE⦵cell links cell potential to free energy, potentials are not scaled with equations and kinetics may still make feasible reactions slow.

Chemistry · Redox and Electrochemistry · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Calculate standard cell potentials from electrode potentials
  • Predict feasibility from the sign of E⦵cell
  • Relate E⦵cell to ΔG⦵

Calculating E⦵cell

  • E⦵cell = E⦵(cathode) − E⦵(anode).
  • The cathode is the half-cell with the more positive E⦵.
  • A positive E⦵cell means the reaction is feasible.
  • Half-cell potentials are not multiplied when equations are scaled.

Link to thermodynamics

  • ΔG⦵ = −nFE⦵cell.
  • n is the number of electrons transferred; F = 96,485 C mol⁻¹.
  • Positive E⦵cell gives negative ΔG⦵.
  • Feasible reactions may still be slow because of kinetics.

E⦵ values indicate whether a reaction can happen, not how fast. Changing concentrations or temperature changes the cell potential. Electrode potentials are intensive properties, which is why they are not multiplied when balancing equations.

Worked example

A zinc-copper cell.

  1. E⦵(Cu²⁺/Cu) = +0.34 V; E⦵(Zn²⁺/Zn) = −0.76 V.
  2. Copper is the cathode; zinc the anode.
  3. E⦵cell = 0.34 − (−0.76) = +1.10 V.
  4. ΔG⦵ = −2 × 96,485 × 1.10 = −212 kJ mol⁻¹.
Common mistakeMultiplying electrode potentials when balancing equations. Potentials do not scale with coefficients.

In 3 lines: Cathode minus anode. Positive is feasible. ΔG⦵ = −nFE⦵.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What does a positive E⦵cell indicate?
    • The reaction is feasible
    • The reaction is not feasible
    • The reaction is very fast
    • The cell is at equilibrium
    Show the answer

    Answer: The reaction is feasible. Positive cell potential gives negative ΔG⦵.

  2. Why are electrode potentials not multiplied when equations are scaled?
    • They are always zero
    • They are intensive properties independent of amount
    • Scaling changes the metal
    • They depend on mass
    Show the answer

    Answer: They are intensive properties independent of amount. Potential per electron does not change with quantity.

  3. Why might a reaction with positive E⦵cell not be observed?
    • Positive E⦵cell always means fast reaction
    • It is not feasible
    • It may have a high activation energy, making it very slow
    • The electrodes are too large
    Show the answer

    Answer: It may have a high activation energy, making it very slow. Thermodynamic feasibility does not guarantee a fast rate.

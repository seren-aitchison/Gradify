Filtration

Gravity filtration with fluted paper is gentle and keeps the filtrate, while vacuum filtration with a Büchner funnel is fast and collects and partly dries solids; the choice depends on whether the solid or liquid is wanted.

Chemistry · Synthetic Chemistry and Laboratory Methods · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Describe gravity filtration
  • Describe vacuum (Büchner) filtration
  • Choose the right filtration method

Gravity filtration

  • Uses a filter funnel and fluted filter paper.
  • Slow but gentle.
  • Used to remove solids when the filtrate is wanted.
  • Also used for hot filtration in recrystallisation.

Vacuum filtration

  • Uses a Büchner or Hirsch funnel and a side-arm flask.
  • Suction pulls liquid through quickly.
  • Used to collect a solid product and help dry it.
  • Wash the solid with small portions of cold solvent.

The residue is the solid left on the paper and the filtrate is the liquid that passes through. Vacuum filtration also removes most solvent from crystals, speeding drying. Celite pads help filter very fine solids that clog paper.

Worked example

Choosing a method.

  1. Removing a drying agent from an organic solution: gravity filtration keeps the filtrate.
  2. Collecting recrystallised product: vacuum filtration collects the solid.
  3. Very fine precipitate: filter through Celite.
  4. The goal decides the method.
Common mistakeUsing vacuum filtration for a hot, volatile solution. Reduced pressure can make the solvent boil and crystals form in the funnel.

In 3 lines: Gravity keeps filtrate. Vacuum collects solids. Choose by goal.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which apparatus is used in vacuum filtration?
    • A Büchner funnel and side-arm flask
    • A separating funnel and beaker
    • A condenser and round flask
    • A burette and conical flask
    Show the answer

    Answer: A Büchner funnel and side-arm flask. Suction through the side-arm pulls liquid through the paper.

  2. Why is fluted filter paper used in gravity filtration?
    • It makes the paper waterproof
    • Its folds increase the surface area and speed filtration
    • It traps more liquid in the paper
    • It is needed for vacuum filtration
    Show the answer

    Answer: Its folds increase the surface area and speed filtration. More exposed area lets liquid pass faster.

  3. Why is vacuum filtration avoided for hot volatile solutions?
    • Hot solutions cannot pass through paper
    • Vacuum cools liquids too slowly
    • Reduced pressure makes the solvent boil and crystals block the funnel
    • Vacuum filtration only works for ice-cold water
    Show the answer

    Answer: Reduced pressure makes the solvent boil and crystals block the funnel. Lower pressure lowers the boiling point, so the hot solvent boils off.

Key terms

Filtrate
The liquid that passes through the filter
Residue
The solid left on the filter
Büchner funnel
A funnel used with suction for vacuum filtration

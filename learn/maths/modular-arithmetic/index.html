Modular Arithmetic

Congruence means equal remainders; reduce before multiplying; powers cycle, so reduce exponents by the cycle length.

Mathematics · Number Theory and Cryptography · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Calculate sums, products and powers modulo n
  • Use cycles of powers to find last digits and remainders

Rules

  • If a ≡ b and c ≡ d (mod n), then a + c ≡ b + d and ac ≡ bd (mod n).
  • Reduce early: 37 × 52 mod 5 = 2 × 2 = 4.
  • Division isn't automatic: you can "divide" by a only when a has an inverse mod n (gcd(a, n) = 1).

Powers

  • Powers cycle: 3k mod 10 runs 3, 9, 7, 1, 3, 9, ….
  • To find 32025 mod 10, reduce the exponent by the cycle length 4.
  • Fermat's and Euler's theorems explain the cycles (later in this school).

Uses

  • The time 9 hours after 20:00 is 29 mod 24 = 5:00.
  • Check digits: ISBN-10 uses mod 11, bank card numbers use the Luhn mod 10 check.
  • Hash tables, random number generators and cryptography all use modular arithmetic.
Worked example

Find the last digit of 32025.

  1. Last digits of powers of 3 cycle with length 4: 3, 9, 7, 1
  2. 2025 = 4 × 506 + 1, so 32025 ≡ 31 (mod 10)
  3. The last digit is 3

Investigate with the lab. Set the modulus to 7 and multiply on the clock. Find the inverse of 3, then switch to modulus 8 and find two non-zero numbers whose product is 0.

Common mistakeDividing both sides of a congruence by a number that isn't coprime to the modulus.

In 2 lines: Modular arithmetic wraps numbers around. Powers cycle mod n.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What is 245 × 378 mod 10?
    • 5
    • 8
    • 4
    • 2
    • 0
    Show the answer

    Answer: 0. Only last digits matter mod 10: 5 × 8 = 40 ≡ 0.

  2. Why can't you always cancel a factor in a congruence like 2x ≡ 2y (mod 4)?
    • 2 shares a factor with 4, so x and y may differ by 2
    • Congruences can't be multiplied
    • Cancelling is always allowed
    • Because 2 is prime, so it can never be cancelled
    • The congruence is false
    Show the answer

    Answer: 2 shares a factor with 4, so x and y may differ by 2. x = 1 and y = 3 satisfy 2x ≡ 2y (mod 4) though x ≢ y (mod 4).

  3. Working mod 8 on the clock, what is 4 × 6?
    • 24, since clock arithmetic doesn't reduce
    • 0, since 24 is a multiple of 8
    • 2, since 4 + 6 = 10 ≡ 2
    • 4, since 6 ≡ −2
    • 10, since 4 × 6 needs no reduction
    Show the answer

    Answer: 0, since 24 is a multiple of 8. 24 = 3 × 8, so 4 × 6 ≡ 0 mod 8: two non-zero numbers multiplying to zero, which can't happen with a prime modulus.

Key terms

Congruent modulo n
a ≡ b (mod n) means n divides a − b: a and b leave the same remainder on division by n
Residue
The remainder of a number modulo n, between 0 and n − 1

Eigenvectors

Solve (A − λI)v = 0 for each λ; eigenvectors are defined up to scaling; distinct eigenvalues give independent eigenvectors; dominant eigenvectors describe long-run behaviour.

Mathematics · Linear Algebra and Matrices · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Find eigenvectors for given eigenvalues
  • Interpret eigenvectors in applications

Finding them

  • For each eigenvalue, solve (A − λI)v = 0.
  • Eigenvectors are only determined up to a non-zero scalar multiple.
  • Eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues are linearly independent.

Meaning

  • Along an eigenvector, A acts as simple scaling.
  • Repeated application Aⁿx lines up with the dominant eigenvector (the power method).
  • A Markov chain's steady state is the eigenvector with eigenvalue 1, scaled to sum to 1.

Applications

  • Normal modes of coupled oscillators.
  • Principal axes of stress and inertia.
  • PageRank, population models (Leslie matrices), facial recognition (eigenfaces).
Worked example

Find eigenvectors of A = [[4, 1], [2, 3]] for λ = 5 and λ = 2.

  1. λ = 5: A − 5I = [[−1, 1], [2, −2]], so −x + y = 0 and v = (1, 1)
  2. λ = 2: A − 2I = [[2, 1], [2, 1]], so 2x + y = 0 and v = (1, −2)
  3. Check: A(1, 1) = (5, 5) ✓ and A(1, −2) = (2, −4) ✓

Investigate with the lab. Set a diagonal matrix such as [[2, 0], [0, 3]] and find the directions that are only stretched. Then add a non-zero off-diagonal entry and track how they move.

Common mistakeGiving the zero vector as an eigenvector.

In 2 lines: Eigenvectors are unturned directions. They describe modes and long-run states.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which is an eigenvector of [[2, 0], [0, 5]]?
    • (1, 1)
    • (1, 2)
    • (2, 5)
    • (1, −1)
    • (0, 1)
    Show the answer

    Answer: (0, 1). A diagonal matrix scales each axis: A(0, 1) = (0, 5) = 5(0, 1), an eigenvector for 5.

  2. What does the power method compute?
    • The dominant eigenvector, by repeatedly multiplying by A
    • All eigenvalues at once
    • The determinant
    • The inverse matrix
    • The smallest eigenvalue directly
    Show the answer

    Answer: The dominant eigenvector, by repeatedly multiplying by A. Aⁿx aligns with the eigenvector of largest |λ|.

  3. What are the eigenvectors of [[2, 0], [0, 3]]?
    • Only multiples of (1, 1), with eigenvalue 5
    • Multiples of (1, 0) (λ = 2) and of (0, 1) (λ = 3)
    • Every vector in the plane, with eigenvalue 2
    • None at all, since the matrix is diagonal already
    • Only (2, 3), the vector of diagonal entries
    Show the answer

    Answer: Multiples of (1, 0) (λ = 2) and of (0, 1) (λ = 3). A diagonal matrix stretches each axis by its diagonal entry.

Key terms

Eigenvector
A non-zero v with Av = λv
Dominant eigenvector
The eigenvector for the eigenvalue of largest absolute value, which repeated multiplication approaches

Divisibility

Use last-digit tests for 2, 4, 5, 8 and 10, digit sums for 3 and 9, and combine tests for 6.

Mathematics · Number and Arithmetic · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Apply divisibility tests for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10
  • Explain why the digit-sum test for 3 and 9 works

The tests

  • 2: last digit even. 5: last digit 0 or 5. 10: last digit 0.
  • 4: last two digits divisible by 4. 8: last three digits divisible by 8.
  • 3: digit sum divisible by 3. 9: digit sum divisible by 9.
  • 6: divisible by both 2 and 3.
  • 11: alternating sum of digits divisible by 11 (for 2,728: 2 − 7 + 2 − 8 = −11).

Why the digit-sum test works

10 = 9 + 1, so 10, 100, 1000, … each leave remainder 1 when divided by 9 (or 3). So a number leaves the same remainder as its digit sum. For example 4,725 = 4 × 1000 + 7 × 100 + 2 × 10 + 5, which has the same remainder as 4 + 7 + 2 + 5 = 18 when divided by 9: none.

Worked example

Is 7,128 divisible by 6? By 8? By 9?

  1. 6: it's even, and its digit sum is 7 + 1 + 2 + 8 = 18, divisible by 3, so yes
  2. 8: the last three digits, 128 = 8 × 16, so yes
  3. 9: the digit sum 18 is divisible by 9, so yes
  • Divisibility is transitive: if 4 divides 12 and 12 divides 36, then 4 divides 36.
Common mistakeUsing the digit-sum test for 6 alone: a number also needs to be even.

In 2 lines: Divisibility tests avoid long division. The digit-sum tests come from 10 leaving remainder 1.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Which number is divisible by 9?
    • 5,813
    • 5,812
    • 5,814
    • 5,810
    • 5,815
    Show the answer

    Answer: 5,814. 5 + 8 + 1 + 4 = 18, which is divisible by 9.

  2. Which number is divisible by 6?
    • 2,335
    • 1,334
    • 2,333
    • 2,334
    • 1,331
    Show the answer

    Answer: 2,334. 2,334 is even and its digit sum 12 is divisible by 3.

  3. Which test shows 3,512 is divisible by 8?
    • 3 + 5 + 1 + 2 = 11
    • 12 is divisible by 4
    • It ends in 2
    • 35 is odd
    • 512 = 8 × 64
    Show the answer

    Answer: 512 = 8 × 64. Divisibility by 8 depends on the last three digits.

Key terms

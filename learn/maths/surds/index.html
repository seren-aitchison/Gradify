Surds

Take out square factors, combine only like surds, and rationalise with the conjugate.

Mathematics · Algebra · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Simplify surds and calculate with them
  • Rationalise denominators

Rules

√(ab) = √a × √b; √(a/b) = √a / √b; √a × √a = a
  • Simplify by taking out square factors: √72 = √36 × √2 = 6√2.
  • Add only like surds: 3√2 + 5√2 = 8√2; √2 + √3 can't be combined. Simplify first: √50 + √8 = 5√2 + 2√2 = 7√2.
  • Expand brackets as usual: (3 + √2)(3 − √2) = 9 − 2 = 7.

Rationalising

  • Single surd: multiply top and bottom by it: 6/√3 = 6√3/3 = 2√3.
  • a + √b in the denominator: multiply by the conjugate a − √b, using the difference of two squares: 4/(3 + √5) = 4(3 − √5)/(9 − 5) = 3 − √5.
  • Exact values: sin 60° = √3/2 and a square of side 1 has diagonal √2.
Worked example

Simplify (√12 + √27)/√3.

  1. √12 = 2√3 and √27 = 3√3, so the top is 5√3
  2. 5√3/√3 = 5
  3. Answer: 5
  • Surds appear in exact trigonometric values, Pythagoras' theorem and quadratic formula answers.
  • A rational number times a surd is still irrational (unless the rational number is 0).
  • Like surds behave like like terms: 4√3 − √3 = 3√3.
Common mistakeWriting √(a + b) = √a + √b: √(9 + 16) = 5, not 7.

In 2 lines: Surds keep answers exact. Conjugates rationalise denominators.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Simplify √75.
    • 5√3
    • 3√5
    • 25√3
    • 15√5
    • 75√1
    Show the answer

    Answer: 5√3. 75 = 25 × 3 and 25 is a square number with √25 = 5, so √75 = 5√3.

  2. Rationalise 10/√5.
    • 10√5
    • 2√5
    • 5√2
    • 2
    • √50/5
    Show the answer

    Answer: 2√5. Multiply top and bottom by √5: 10√5/5, which simplifies to 2√5.

  3. Expand (2 + √3)2.
    • 7
    • 4 + 3
    • 7 + 4√3
    • 7 + 2√3
    • 13
    Show the answer

    Answer: 7 + 4√3. 4 + 4√3 + 3 = 7 + 4√3: the middle term is 2 × 2 × √3.

Key terms

Surd
An irrational root, such as √3 or ∛5, left in root form to keep it exact
Rationalise the denominator
Rewrite a fraction so its denominator has no surds

Expanding Brackets

Every term multiplies every term; squares of brackets have a middle term; a minus before a bracket changes every sign.

Mathematics · Algebra · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Expand single, double and triple brackets
  • Recognise and use the expansions of (a ± b)2 and (a + b)(a − b)

Single and double brackets

  • Single: multiply every term inside by the term outside: −3(2x − 5) = −6x + 15.
  • Double: every term in the first bracket multiplies every term in the second: (x + 4)(x − 7) = x2 − 7x + 4x − 28 = x2 − 3x − 28. A grid keeps track of all four products.
  • Expand then simplify: 3(x + 2) − 2(x − 1) = 3x + 6 − 2x + 2 = x + 8.

Special products

(a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2; (a − b)2 = a2 − 2ab + b2; (a + b)(a − b) = a2 − b2
  • (x + 5)2 isn't x2 + 25: the middle term 10x is easy to forget.

Triple brackets

  • Expand two brackets first, then multiply the result by the third: (x + 1)(x + 2)(x − 3) = (x2 + 3x + 2)(x − 3) = x3 − 7x − 6.
Worked example

Expand and simplify (2x − 3)(x + 4) − (x − 1)2.

  1. (2x − 3)(x + 4) = 2x2 + 8x − 3x − 12 = 2x2 + 5x − 12
  2. (x − 1)2 = x2 − 2x + 1
  3. 2x2 + 5x − 12 − x2 + 2x − 1 = x2 + 7x − 13 (the minus sign applies to every term of the square)
Common mistakeWriting (x + 5)2 = x2 + 25.

In 2 lines: Expanding uses the distributive law. Special products save time and avoid errors.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. Expand 4(3x − 2).
    • 12x − 2
    • 7x − 6
    • 12x + 8
    • 12x − 8
    • 3x − 8
    Show the answer

    Answer: 12x − 8. Multiply both terms by 4: 4 × 3x = 12x and 4 × (−2) = −8.

  2. Expand (x − 9)(x + 2).
    • x2 + 7x − 18
    • x2 − 7x + 18
    • x2 − 11x − 18
    • x2 − 18
    • x2 − 7x − 18
    Show the answer

    Answer: x2 − 7x − 18. x × 2 = 2x and −9 × x = −9x combine to −7x; −9 × 2 = −18.

  3. What is the coefficient of x in (2x − 5)2?
    • −20
    • −10
    • 10
    • 25
    • 4
    Show the answer

    Answer: −20. (2x − 5)2 = 4x2 − 20x + 25: the middle term is 2 × 2x × (−5).

Definite Integrals

F(b) − F(a); split at x-intercepts for total area; area between curves is ∫ (top − bottom).

Mathematics · Calculus · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Evaluate definite integrals
  • Find areas under curves and between curves

Evaluating

∫ab f(x) dx = [F(x)]ab = F(b) − F(a)
  • ∫13 (2x + 1) dx = [x2 + x]13 = 12 − 2 = 10.
  • No + c is needed: it cancels.
  • Properties: ∫aa f = 0; ∫ba f = −∫ab f; ∫ab f + ∫bc f = ∫ac f.

Areas

  • For area (not signed area), split at the x-intercepts and add the absolute values of each part.
  • ∫02π sin x dx = 0, but the area between sin x and the axis is 4.
  • Area between curves: ∫ab (top − bottom) dx, where a and b are the intersection points.
Worked example

Find the area enclosed by y = x2 and y = 2x.

  1. Intersections: x2 = 2x gives x = 0 and x = 2
  2. On [0, 2], 2x is on top: area = ∫02 (2x − x2) dx = [x2 − x3/3]02
  3. = 4 − 8/3 = 4/3
  • Even functions: ∫−aa f = 2∫0a f; odd functions: ∫−aa f = 0.
  • The average value of f on [a, b] is (1/(b − a)) ∫ab f(x) dx.

Investigate with the lab. Integrate over a region where f is negative and see the sum come out negative: definite integrals measure signed area.

Common mistakeReporting a negative area, or letting parts above and below the axis cancel.

In 2 lines: Definite integrals give net signed areas. Split at roots for total area.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. What is ∫02 (x3 + 1) dx?
    • 4
    • 5
    • 8
    • 6
    • 2
    Show the answer

    Answer: 6. An antiderivative is x4/4 + x; evaluating from 0 to 2 gives 4 + 2 − 0 = 6.

  2. What is the total area between y = x − 1 and the x-axis from x = 0 to x = 2?
    • 0
    • 2
    • 1/2
    • −1
    • 1
    Show the answer

    Answer: 1. Each triangle has area 1/2, one below and one above the axis; the total area is 1, though the integral is 0.

  3. What is ∫ x dx from −1 to 1?
    • 0, since the negative and positive areas cancel
    • 1, the total area between the line and the axis
    • 2, the length of the interval of integration
    • −1, since the left half dominates the right
    • It is undefined, since the line crosses the axis
    Show the answer

    Answer: 0, since the negative and positive areas cancel. The area below the axis on [−1, 0] is −½ and the area above on [0, 1] is +½, so the signed areas cancel to 0.

Key terms

Limits of integration
The values a and b in ∫ab
Signed area
Area above the x-axis counts positive; below counts negative

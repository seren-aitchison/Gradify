Job Satisfaction

Job satisfaction is influenced by situations and dispositions and moderately correlates with performance; causation runs in both directions.

Psychology · Applied Psychology · about 2 minutes

What you'll be able to do

  • Define and describe ways of measuring job satisfaction
  • Explain dispositional and situational influences on job satisfaction
  • Evaluate the relationship between job satisfaction and performance

Measuring job satisfaction

Job satisfaction is a positive evaluation of one's job. It can be measured as global satisfaction ("Overall, how satisfied are you with your job?") or facet satisfaction (pay, colleagues, supervision, the work itself, promotion). Common measures include the Job Descriptive Index and the Minnesota Satisfaction Questionnaire.

Influences

Situational influences include job characteristics, pay fairness, relationships, workload and job security. Dispositional influences matter too: Staw, Bell and Clausen (1986) found adolescents' emotional dispositions predicted job satisfaction decades later, and core self-evaluations (self-esteem, self-efficacy, locus of control, emotional stability) predict satisfaction (Judge and Bono, 2001).

Worked example

Satisfaction and performance

  1. Many managers assume happy workers are productive workers.
  2. Judge et al. (2001) meta-analysed 312 samples and found a moderate correlation of about 0.30 between job satisfaction and performance.
  3. The direction of the relationship is unclear: performance may lead to satisfaction (through rewards) as well as the reverse.

Evaluation

Job satisfaction is linked to performance, lower absence and lower turnover. But satisfaction measures rely on self-report, and organisations can't control dispositional influences. Improving fair pay, job design and relationships remains important for wellbeing and performance.

Common mistakeAssuming happier workers are always more productive. The relationship is moderate and may run both ways.

In 2 lines: Job satisfaction can be global or facet-based and is measured by questionnaires. Situations and dispositions shape it; it correlates about 0.30 with performance.

Practice questions

Answer each one in your head before you open it: recalling beats rereading.

  1. "Overall, how satisfied are you with your job?" measures…
    • Global job satisfaction
    • Facet satisfaction
    • Job performance
    • Burnout
    Show the answer

    Answer: Global job satisfaction. A single overall rating measures global satisfaction.

  2. Adolescents' emotional dispositions predicted their job satisfaction decades later. This supports…
    • Situational influences only
    • Dispositional influences
    • Scientific management
    • Equity theory
    Show the answer

    Answer: Dispositional influences. Stable traits shape how people feel about jobs.

  3. Why might performance lead to satisfaction?
    • Performance never affects feelings
    • Satisfaction is fixed
    • Good performance brings rewards and recognition
    • Rewards reduce satisfaction
    Show the answer

    Answer: Good performance brings rewards and recognition. Success can produce satisfaction through rewards.

Key terms

Job satisfaction
A positive evaluation of one's job
Facet satisfaction
Satisfaction with specific aspects of a job such as pay or colleagues
Core self-evaluations
Basic beliefs about one's worth, capability and control

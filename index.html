      Questions

      What is Gradify?

      A family of learning sites, each built for one kind of learner: a stage of life like UnderGradify, a profession like MedGradify, or a subject like LanguageGradify. getgradify.com is the home of all of them, and of Gradify LifeOS.

      What is Gradify LifeOS?

      One place for your whole learning life. Every Gradify site has its own learning path and heatmap, and everything you do on any of them comes together in LifeOS: one heatmap, one set of goals, XP and streaks, your knowledge map on Gradify Earth, and every course you've completed.

      Can I use LifeOS yet?

      Not yet. The LifeOS section on this page is a preview with example data, so you can see how it will work. Accounts come first, then ForgeLab will be the first site connected to it.

      Will Gradify courses count as qualifications?

      That's the aim. Every completed course will come with a certificate that has a unique ID and a public page where anyone, such as an employer or a university, can check it's genuine. Which organisations recognise them will build up over time.

      How much will it cost?

      Pricing hasn't been decided yet. It will be published before accounts open.

      Which sites are live?

      Each site's card shows whether it's live or coming soon. Turn on “Live only” in the directory to see just the live ones.

      What happens to my data?

      LifeOS is being designed so your progress belongs to you: you'll be able to export it or delete it, and each Gradify site will only see the data it needs.

Privacy

A plain-English account of what Gradify keeps about you and where. It describes how the site works today.

Not yet final. This page hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, and the name of the organisation responsible for your data will be added.

Contact. For anything about your personal data (a copy of it, a correction, removal from a launch list, or a question), email privacy@getgradify.com. For anything else about Gradify, email hello@getgradify.com.

No account: everything stays in this browser

Gradify works without an account. Everything you do is saved in this browser's storage, on this device:

  • notes, flashcards, reviews and plans (gradify.v1)
  • progress in each course (gradify.course.…)
  • your set-up answers and display settings (gradify.learner)

Gradify doesn't set cookies. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes all of it.

With a Gradify account

When accounts are switched on and you sign in, the same things are saved to your account so they follow you between devices:

  • your profile and set-up answers
  • notes you upload, with their extracted text
  • study packs, flashcards and reviews
  • course progress, your answers to questions, and the lab challenges you meet or try (which challenge, whether you met it, and how long you worked at it)
  • study plans and activity
  • any SportGradify training logs or NurseGradify revalidation records you add

The data is stored with Supabase. Every table is protected so that only you can read your own rows: other learners can't, and neither can visitors who aren't signed in. You can download everything, or delete it, from Account in the Gradify app.

Gradify AI

When you use a Gradify AI feature, the text it needs is sent from Gradify's server to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer. Depending on the feature, that's:

  • your notes
  • your written answer
  • the lesson's notes, which the server reads from the site itself
  • what an interactive lab on the page shows when you ask about it: its settings, results and challenges
  • the conversation so far
  • a summary of your progress

Gradify saves the study packs it makes from your notes to your account, with a fingerprint of the notes so the same notes are never processed twice. It doesn't keep tutor conversations. For each AI request it records which feature you used, on which site, whether it worked, how much text it took (in tokens) and its estimated cost, so it can apply your monthly allowance and keep Gradify's spending in check. AI features need an account and only run when you choose to use them. Gradify AI is not switched on yet.

Don't put personal details about other people, such as patients or clients, into notes, answers or questions.

Launch lists

If you ask to be told when a Gradify site or Gradify Pro opens, Gradify keeps:

  • your email address (stored in lower case),
  • which list you joined (for example "Gradify Pro" or "LawGradify"),
  • when you joined.

Nothing else is collected: no account, no payment details, no name. The list is kept in Gradify's database (Supabase, in the UK).

What it's for: one email to tell you that thing has opened. It isn't used for newsletters or marketing, and it isn't shared or sold. Why Gradify can keep it: you asked to be told (your consent), and you can withdraw that at any time.

How long it's kept: your entry is deleted once that launch email has been sent, and in any case within 12 months of joining if it never opens.

Nobody can read the list through the website, including you, and joining the same list twice keeps one entry. To have your entry removed before then, email privacy@getgradify.com from the address you joined with.

Usage counts

To see whether Gradify is used, the site counts page views, finished lessons, finished exams and tutor requests. Each count records:

  • the site
  • the page address, without anything after # or ?
  • the website that linked to it, if any
  • whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer
  • the date

It records no name, account, cookie or identifier of any kind, and no time of day, so counts can't be linked to you or to each other. Nothing is counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or if you turn counting off here.

Your choices

  • Download or delete everything from Account in the Gradify app.
  • Ask about, correct or remove anything Gradify holds about you: privacy@getgradify.com.
  • Use Gradify without an account, and nothing leaves this browser except the anonymous counts above.
  • Turn the counts off on this page.

What is fSRS?

FSRS is an open-source spaced repetition algorithm that predicts how likely you are to remember each card and schedules the next review for when that chance drops to a target, typically 90%.

Why it matters

It models each card's difficulty, stability and retrievability, and was trained on millions of real reviews. Anki adopted it as an option in 2023, and it generally needs fewer reviews than older SM-2 schedules for the same retention.

How to use it in Gradify

Gradify uses FSRS-5 for every flashcard.

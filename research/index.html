Where students slip up: common mistakes across 33 subjects

Gradify's lessons flag the mistakes students commonly make with a "common mistake" warning. We counted them: 9,646 warnings in 9,883 lessons across 33 subjects. Medicine has the most per lesson (1.08), then Accounting and Finance and Health and Wellbeing.

What this measures: the warnings our course writers put in each lesson, not the mistakes real students made in exams. A high number means a subject has many well-known traps worth checking before an exam. Counted from the course notes on 23 November 2026; free to cite with a link to this page (CC BY 4.0).

How we counted

Each lesson's notes mark common mistakes with their own tag, flashcards and check questions with theirs, and key terms with a term and definition. We counted every tag in every written lesson of each live course with at least 20 lessons. Flashcards here are the cards written into the notes; the app makes more from your own notes.

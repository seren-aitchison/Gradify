Ten free design briefs to practise on

Real, small problems to practise the design process on: understand who it's for, define the problem, generate ideas, prototype and test. Pick one, give yourself a weekend, and use CreateGradify's free lessons on design thinking and prototyping as you go.

  1. A bus stop for a rainy town

    Redesign a bus stop for a town where it rains 200 days a year. Who waits there, for how long, and what do they need?

    Practises: Empathy interviews, rapid sketching

  2. A medicine reminder for a grandparent

    Design a reminder for someone who takes five medicines a day and doesn't own a smartphone.

    Practises: User research, low-tech prototyping

  3. The first five minutes of a library visit

    Map the journey of a first-year student entering the library for the first time. Fix the worst moment.

    Practises: Journey mapping

  4. A packaging-free lunch

    Design a way for a school canteen to serve lunch with no single-use packaging.

    Practises: Systems thinking, constraints

  5. A sign nobody ignores

    A sign asks people not to cycle on a busy path; everyone ignores it. Design a better intervention.

    Practises: Behaviour design

  6. An app for a busy carer

    A young carer has 10 minutes a day for homework. Design something that helps.

    Practises: Problem framing, How Might We

  7. A kitchen tool for one hand

    Design a kitchen tool for someone who can only use one hand.

    Practises: Inclusive design, prototyping

  8. A calmer exam waiting room

    Students wait 20 minutes outside the exam hall. Make that time calmer.

    Practises: Environment design

  9. A repair café flyer that works

    Design the flyer (or something better) that gets people to bring broken things to a repair café.

    Practises: Communication design

  10. A playground for all ages

    Design a small public space that a toddler, a teenager and a grandparent all want to use.

    Practises: Co-design, ideation

How to work through a brief

  1. Empathise: talk to two people the brief affects.
  2. Define: write one "How might we…?" question.
  3. Ideate: sketch ten ideas in ten minutes.
  4. Prototype: make the roughest version you can test.
  5. Test: watch someone use it; note what confuses them.

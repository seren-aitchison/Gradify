Three revision tips for English Literature

  1. Turn each topic into exam-style questions and plan answers from memory in five minutes.
  2. Make flashcards for the key evidence, cases, dates and quotations your arguments need.
  3. Write at least one timed answer a week and compare it with the mark scheme or model answers.

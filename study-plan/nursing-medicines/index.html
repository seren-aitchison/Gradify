Medicines management study plan

Pharmacology, the administration process, calculations and medicines safety. Education only: you give medicines under your local policy and supervision. At four lessons a study day, five days a week, the 102 lessons in this plan take about 6 weeks, with flashcards and practice questions in every lesson.

Three revision tips for Nursing

  1. Learn definitions word for word with flashcards: mark schemes reward exact terms.
  2. Draw every process (cycles, pathways, mechanisms) from memory on a blank page, then check it.
  3. Mix calculation and data questions from different topics in one session so you learn to spot which method each needs.

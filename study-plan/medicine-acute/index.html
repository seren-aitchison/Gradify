Acute and emergency medicine study plan

Recognising and managing the acutely unwell patient: ABCDE, NEWS2, sepsis, shock, arrests and the emergencies of each system. At four lessons a study day, five days a week, the 92 lessons in this plan take about 5 weeks, with flashcards and practice questions in every lesson.

Three revision tips for Medicine

  1. Learn definitions word for word with flashcards: mark schemes reward exact terms.
  2. Draw every process (cycles, pathways, mechanisms) from memory on a blank page, then check it.
  3. Mix calculation and data questions from different topics in one session so you learn to spot which method each needs.

Learning disability nursing study plan

The shared core, then learning disability nursing: communication, reasonable adjustments, capacity and advocacy, positive behaviour support, health inequalities, epilepsy and complex needs, families and inclusion. At four lessons a study day, five days a week, the 332 lessons in this plan take about 17 weeks, with flashcards and practice questions in every lesson.

Three revision tips for Nursing

  1. Learn definitions word for word with flashcards: mark schemes reward exact terms.
  2. Draw every process (cycles, pathways, mechanisms) from memory on a blank page, then check it.
  3. Mix calculation and data questions from different topics in one session so you learn to spot which method each needs.

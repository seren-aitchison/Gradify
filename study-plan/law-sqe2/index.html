SQE2: practical legal skills study plan

Interviewing and attendance notes, advocacy, case and matter analysis, research, writing and drafting, in the five practice contexts, with realistic case files. At four lessons a study day, five days a week, the 188 lessons in this plan take about 10 weeks, with flashcards and practice questions in every lesson.

Three revision tips for Law

  1. Turn each topic into exam-style questions and plan answers from memory in five minutes.
  2. Make flashcards for the key evidence, cases, dates and quotations your arguments need.
  3. Write at least one timed answer a week and compare it with the mark scheme or model answers.

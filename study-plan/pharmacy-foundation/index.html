Three revision tips for Pharmacy

  1. Learn definitions word for word with flashcards: mark schemes reward exact terms.
  2. Draw every process (cycles, pathways, mechanisms) from memory on a blank page, then check it.
  3. Mix calculation and data questions from different topics in one session so you learn to spot which method each needs.

Other plans in Pharmacy

  • MPharm: the sciencesThe professional foundations, then how drugs act, how the body handles them, their chemistry and their formulation, with calculations throughout.
  • MPharm: therapeuticsDisease, drugs and patients system by system: cardiovascular, respiratory, endocrine, mental health and neurology, infection, gastrointestinal and renal, inflammation and skin, cancer and immunology, and special populations.
  • MPharm: practice and prescribingLaw, ethics and governance, clinical pharmacy and medicines optimisation, and the clinical assessment and prescribing skills you'll need as an independent prescriber at registration.
  • Registration assessment preparationThe assessment format and framework, calculations, law and the therapeutics that come up most, with single-best-answer practice. Check the GPhC's current framework before you sit.
  • Pharmacy techniciansThe professional foundations, medicines law, dispensing, formulation, calculations and medicines optimisation, within the GPhC's standards for pharmacy technicians and your local scope.
  • Pharmacist prescribersClinical assessment, prescribing decisions and governance, with the therapeutics and special populations behind them, for pharmacists preparing to prescribe or extending their scope.

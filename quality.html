What these scores are, and aren't. Each one checks that what good teaching needs is there and well formed: learning outcomes, prerequisites, worked examples, application questions, dated sources, readable sentences, flashcards for spaced review. They can't tell whether an explanation is right or whether you'll learn from it. Accuracy is capped at 60 for any lesson a subject expert hasn't yet reviewed, which is currently every lesson: each course page says so. Real evidence of learning comes from learners' own answers over time.

Which qualifications Gradify covers

Gradify only says it covers a qualification when it has checked its lessons against the official specification, outcome by outcome, and recorded when. Until then it says exactly how strong each claim is.

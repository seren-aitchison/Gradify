Computer Science: a free course on TechGradify · ForgeLab

TechGradify · ForgeLab's free Computer Science course runs from Computational Thinking and Problem Solving to Security, AI and Society: 179 lessons in 12 schools, each with notes, flashcards and check questions at three levels (Foundation, Application, Evaluation). It's written for developers, data scientists and AI engineers.

Study material, not a qualification or legal advice. ForgeLab teaches computer science for GCSE, A level and the first year of university, but it isn't an exam board resource or an accredited course: check the details of your own specification with your teacher or exam board. The lessons were drafted with AI assistance and haven't yet been reviewed by subject experts, so they may contain errors. Security lessons explain attacks so that you can defend against them: trying them on systems you don't own or have permission to test can be a criminal offence under the Computer Misuse Act 1990. The law lessons describe UK law for study; for a real data protection question, see the ICO or a qualified adviser.

Critical analysis checklist

Descriptive writing says what a source says; critical writing says how good the evidence is, how it compares with other sources and why it matters for your argument. Check each paragraph for all five.

What is critical analysis in an essay?

Going beyond describing sources to evaluating them: how strong the evidence is, where sources disagree and why, and what that means for your argument.

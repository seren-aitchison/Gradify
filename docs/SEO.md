# Search, AI search and free growth

What's built into getgradify.com to help it rank in Google and Bing and get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, and the jobs that need a person with an account. Everything here is free.

## Built into the site

All of it is generated by `node scripts/build-seo.mjs` from `scripts/seo/`, then listed with `node scripts/build-sitemap.mjs`. CI fails if either is out of date (`tests/seo.test.mjs`, and the "Search pages are up to date" step).

Every generated page has: one `h1`, a two-sentence answer straight under it (what Featured Snippets and AI answers quote), a self-referencing canonical, Open Graph and Twitter cards (good previews in WhatsApp, Discord and iMessage), visible breadcrumbs with `BreadcrumbList` data, relevant JSON-LD (`WebApplication`, `HowTo`, `FAQPage`, `Article`, `Course`, `DefinedTerm`), "Last updated" on guides and tools, a "Share with your study group" button (adds `utm_source=share`), a free-signup call to action and, on guides, tools and templates, a study-tips email opt-in (list "Study tips" in `public.waitlist`, described on the privacy page).

Elsewhere:

- **Homepage**: `EducationalOrganization`, `WebSite`, `WebApplication` and `FAQPage` JSON-LD (the FAQ data must match the visible FAQ; a test checks), two new direct-answer FAQs, and a "Free tools and guides" strip.
- **Every indexed page** (hub pages and every subject-site page): a canonical URL and link-preview tags are added if missing (`withHeadTags` in `scripts/build-seo.mjs`).
- **Footer** on every hub page: a "Free resources" column and links to About, Ambassadors and the site map (`scripts/sync-headers.mjs`).
- **robots.txt** explicitly welcomes search and AI crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended …).
- **AI referrals**: page views keep the referring host (`kit/core/analytics.js`), and a learner's first touch records which AI assistant sent them (`ai` in `user_metadata.first_touch`, `kit/core/attribution.js`).

### Adding pages

- A guide: add an entry to `GUIDES` in `scripts/seo/guides.mjs` (open with a two-sentence `answer`; link to the pillar and the glossary).
- A glossary term, template or comparison: `scripts/seo/pages.mjs`.
- A tool: `TOOLS` in `scripts/seo/tools.mjs` and its logic in `assets/study-tools.js` (with a test).
- Topics and study plans follow the courses automatically.

Then run `node scripts/build-seo.mjs && node scripts/build-sitemap.mjs` and commit.

### Watching AI referrals

```sql
-- page views by AI assistant, last 30 days
select referrer, count(*) from page_events
where event = 'view' and day > current_date - 30
  and referrer ~* '(chatgpt\.com|openai\.com|perplexity\.ai|gemini\.google\.com|claude\.ai|copilot\.microsoft\.com)'
group by 1 order by 2 desc;

-- sign-ups that first came from an AI assistant
select raw_user_meta_data->'first_touch'->>'ai' as ai, count(*) from auth.users
where raw_user_meta_data->'first_touch'->>'ai' is not null group by 1;
```

## Needs a person with an account (do once)

- [ ] **Google Search Console**: verify both the Domain property (DNS TXT on Cloudflare) and the `https://getgradify.com` URL-prefix property; submit `sitemap.xml`; switch on email alerts. Weekly: Indexing → Pages, and nudge anything stuck in "Discovered – not indexed" with a link from the homepage or a guide.
- [ ] **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import from Search Console; check the AI Performance report and Topics portfolio for queries where competitors are cited and Gradify isn't, and add those pages.
- [ ] **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** (free once verified): run Site Audit monthly.
- [ ] **Cloudflare**: DNS is already there. Turning on the proxy (orange cloud) for GitHub Pages adds caching, Brotli and the bot report; check AI crawlers aren't blocked under Security → Bots (Cloudflare can block AI bots by default).
- [ ] **PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse**: check the homepage, `/app.html`, a course page and `/tools/` on mobile (LCP < 2.5 s, good INP).
- [ ] **Google Alerts** for "getgradify" and "GetGradify": ask for a link wherever Gradify is mentioned without one.
- [ ] **Entity profiles**, all with the same one-line description and link: Crunchbase, G2, Capterra / GetApp, LinkedIn company page, Product Hunt.
- [ ] **About page**: add Seren's background, photo and LinkedIn (then add `sameAs` links to the `Person` in `scripts/seo/pages.mjs`).
- [ ] **Ambassadors page**: it offers free Gradify Pro, the title and a reference. Confirm you're happy with those terms before promoting it.

## Ongoing (weekly or monthly)

- **Search Console "striking distance"**: queries at positions 8–20 → put the phrase in an `h2` on that page, add 200–300 words, link to it from the homepage or a guide.
- **Low CTR, high impressions**: rewrite the `<title>` (numbers, "free, no signup") and compare 14 days later.
- **Seasonal**: refresh and re-date guides 4–6 weeks before January, May/June and September peaks (Google Trends).
- **Journalist requests** (Featured.com, Qwoted, Source of Sources): two or three relevant answers a week.
- **Listicle outreach**: ask the authors of "best study apps" articles to add Gradify, pointing to the source-check feature.
- **Communities**: r/UniUK, r/studytips, r/GCSE, r/6thForm, r/medicalschooluk, r/PhD, The Student Room, TES. Help first; link only when it answers the question.
- **Student newspapers and society resource pages**, **podcasts**, **newsletter swaps** with student Substacks.
- **Free platforms that rank quickly**: a GitHub "awesome-study-resources" list, Notion template gallery (a revision dashboard linking to `/templates/`), SlideShare/Scribd PDFs of the guides, DEV/Hashnode/Indie Hackers build logs, YouTube walkthroughs (then embed them on the matching guide).
- **OER directories** (OER Commons, MERLOT): submit the templates and guides.
- **Original research**: a small student survey, published at `/research/` with charts. Only publish real data.
- **Prune**: pages with no impressions after three months: improve them or 301 them into the closest guide.
- Say "search GetGradify" in videos and printables to grow branded search.

Not built yet (bigger product work): a Chrome extension, public shared study packs (`/pack/<topic>`), "Study Wrapped" progress cards inside the app, and an email sender for the study-tips list.

# Gradify AI: estimated Anthropic cost per learner

Generated by `scripts/ai-cost-model.mjs` on 2026-11-10. **Estimates, not measurements of real use.** Input sizes are measured from Gradify's real prompts and lesson notes; reply lengths, usage, the exchange rate and fees are assumptions (listed at the end). Once Gradify AI is in use, `ai-usage.html` and `scripts/ai-usage.mjs` show the real cost per request: replace these assumptions with those figures.

**Modelled:** tutor on Opus 5.5, explain on Opus 5.5, pack on Sonnet 5.5, quiz on Sonnet 5.5. Allowances a month: Free 5 / 5 / 1 / 2, Pro 40 / 25 / 5 / 8 (tutor / explanations / packs / quizzes). Fair-use caps: Free £1.00, Pro £5.00 a month. £0.79 per $.

## Cost of one use: Opus 5.5 against Sonnet 5.5

Same prompts, same assumed reply lengths; Sonnet 5.5 costs half as much per token ($2 / $10 against $4 / $20 per million). Bold is the model each feature now uses.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI tutor conversation | **5.6p** | 2.8p | **7.8p** | 3.9p |
| Explanation | **1.2p** | 0.6p | **2.5p** | 1.2p |
| Study pack (25k characters of notes) | 21.5p | **10.7p** | 40.5p | **20.2p** |
| AI-written quiz | 12.1p | **6.0p** | 21.6p | **10.8p** |

Moving packs and quizzes to Sonnet saves about 10.7p a pack and 6.0p a quiz. Keeping tutoring and explanations on Opus costs about 2.8p more a conversation and 0.6p more an explanation than Sonnet would.

## Quality: what is and isn't known

The cost side is estimated from real prompts; the quality side can't be estimated from prompts and **has not been measured yet** (Gradify has no Anthropic key set, so no comparison could be run). What is known:

- **Study packs are checked mechanically whichever model writes them.** Every card carries a quote from the learner's notes and is scored against them (`groundingScore`), structured output fixes the shape, and the deck shows how much of each card was found in the notes. A weaker pack shows up as fewer grounded cards, which can be measured.
- **Tutoring and explanations are judged by people**, so they stay on Opus 5.5 until real conversations say otherwise.
- **Before relying on Sonnet for packs and quizzes,** run `node scripts/eval-notes.mjs --compare --long` (with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set): the same notes through both models, comparing valid output, grounded cards, cards and topics per pack, why/apply questions, real tokens and real cost. A run (three short sample notes and one lecture-length sample, through both models) costs about £0.64 at the typical estimates. If Sonnet's packs are clearly worse, switch back without a deploy: `update public.ai_feature_models set model = 'claude-opus-5-5' where feature in ('pack', 'quiz');`

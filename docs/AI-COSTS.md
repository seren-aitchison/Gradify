# Gradify AI: estimated Anthropic cost per learner

Generated by `scripts/ai-cost-model.mjs` on 2026-11-10. **Estimates, not measurements of real use.** Input sizes are measured from Gradify's real prompts and lesson notes; reply lengths, usage levels, the exchange rate and fees are assumptions (listed at the end). Once Gradify AI is in use, `scripts/ai-usage.mjs` and the owner's page (`ai-usage.html`) show the real cost per request, and these assumptions should be replaced with those figures.

Model: **claude-opus-5-5** for every feature, at $4 / $20 per million input / output tokens ($0.2 cached reads, $5 cache writes), £0.79 per $.

## What each use costs

| Use | Typical | High | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI tutor conversation (the mix below) | 5.6p | 7.8p | output tokens: thinking plus the reply, over several turns |
| Explanation | 1.2p | 2.5p | the lesson's notes (690 tokens median) and the answer |
| Study pack (25k characters of notes) | 21.5p | 40.5p | a long structured pack at high effort: output is 88% of the cost |
| AI-written quiz | 12.1p | 21.6p | re-reading the notes, then the questions |

A pack from short notes (10k characters) is about 20.1p; from the Pro maximum (40k characters on Free's limit, 120k on Pro's) 22.9p or more.

Tutor conversation by mode (typical): socratic 8.3p, examiner 6.2p, roleplay 9.7p, diagnose 1.4p, challenge 2.8p, connect 1.2p, mark 4.3p, director 2.7p.

## Against Gradify Pro's income

What one Pro subscriber brings in a month after VAT (20%) and Stripe's fees: **£6.28** on monthly billing (£7.99), **£4.31** on yearly billing (£63.99 ÷ 12).

Free learners bring in nothing: an active Free learner costs about £0.61 a month, so each typical Pro subscriber's margin on monthly billing covers about 2 active Free learners.

Not included: Supabase, hosting and email costs, refunds, chargebacks, and income tax.

## What changes the picture

Cost a month for a typical and an every-use Pro learner (typical reply lengths) under different choices. These are options for the owner, not changes made: Gradify AI currently uses claude-opus-5-5 everywhere.

| Option | Pro, typical | Pro, every use | Left from yearly (typical) |
|---|---|---|---|
| As built: claude-opus-5-5, Pro allowance 150 / 100 / 20 / 30 | £4.56 | £17.44 | £-0.25 |
| Packs and quizzes at medium effort (assumed 40% less output) | £3.88 | £14.79 | £0.43 |
| Packs and quizzes on claude-sonnet-5-5, the rest on Opus | £3.54 | £13.48 | £0.77 |
| Everything on claude-sonnet-5-5 | £2.28 | £8.72 | £2.03 |
| Pro allowance 150 / 100 / 8 / 12 (fewer packs and quizzes) | £4.56 | £12.69 | £-0.25 |

The per-learner fair-use cap (public.ai_plan_caps, now £4.00 Pro and £1.00 Free a month) stops any one learner costing more than that, whatever the allowance says. At today's model and allowances, a Pro learner who uses most of their allowance reaches the cap first.

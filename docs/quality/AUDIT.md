# Gradify quality audit

Run 2026-11-18 by `node scripts/quality-audit.mjs`, against the [Quality Constitution](../QUALITY-CONSTITUTION.md).

> **Read this first.** Every score is a structural proxy: it checks that what good teaching needs is present and well
> formed. It can't tell whether an explanation is right, or whether students learn from it. **9979 of 9979 lessons
> have not been reviewed by a subject expert**, so their accuracy is reported as unverified and capped at 60. Real
> evidence of learning will come from learners' own answers over time, with consent (constitution articles 9 and 10).

## The constitution, article by article

- **1. Accuracy before quantity.** Partly. Mechanical errors (broken notation, arithmetic that doesn't add up, contradictory answers) are caught automatically; factual accuracy needs a subject expert, and the audit says so instead of guessing.
- **2. Verified sources for factual claims.** Yes: sources, their kinds and dates, and evidence-status labels per lesson.
- **3. Every resource linked to explicit learning outcomes.** Yes: objectives per lesson, questions tied to objectives, and verified versus unverified curriculum coverage (kit/curriculum/coverage.js).
- **4. No publishing without quality checks.** Yes: CI blocks the deploy when a check fails.
- **5. Accessible by design.** Partly: automated checks (scripts/check-a11y.cjs, the audit's accessibility dimension). Real assistive-technology and user testing has to be done by people.
- **6. Personalisation based on evidence, not assumptions.** Yes: the priority engine and the session planner carry 'why' and 'evidence' on every item (tests/session.test.mjs).
- **7. No silent degradation of existing content.** Yes: the curriculum registry (permanent ids and versions) and the audit's baseline comparison.
- **8. Student privacy and security by default.** Yes: the RLS audit (scripts/supabase-admin.mjs rls-audit), database tests, the secrets test.
- **9. Real learning matters more than engagement.** Partly: retention and mastery are measured from learners' own answers. Learning outcomes across learners need consented, privacy-preserving analytics, which don't exist yet.
- **10. Every improvement must be measurable.** Yes for content structure (the audit's baseline); learner-outcome measurement is still to be built.

# Competitor intelligence: a first search sample

**Source and limits.** Ten priority searches, run on 24 November 2026 with a web search tool that searches from the US. These are **not** Google UK positions: the order and mix will differ for a UK student on Google. Use this as a picture of *who competes and with what kind of page*, not as rankings. Repeat the sample monthly, and replace it with Search Console data (which pages actually outrank Gradify for queries it appears on) once that's imported. Nothing here copies competitors' content: it records what kind of page wins, and where Gradify can be more useful.

| Search | Who shows up (first results) | What kind of page wins | Where they're weak | Gradify's angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GCSE biology revision | Save My Exams, Tes, STEM Learning, school PDFs, Study Mind, Knowt, Doc Brown | Exam-board-specific topic hubs; teacher resource libraries; checklist PDFs | Mostly read-only notes; checklists are static PDFs; board-specific paywalls | Interactive lessons + spaced flashcards. **But** Gradify's biology isn't mapped to a GCSE specification, so it can't target "GCSE" honestly yet: map it first (coverage.js) |
| A level chemistry revision notes | Save My Exams, Study Mind, MyEdSpace, ChemRevise, Knowt, Scribd uploads | Notes split by exam board and topic | Notes without practice; user-uploaded copies of varying quality and legality | Same as above: needs specification mapping before claiming "A level" |
| DNA replication explained | OpenStax, LibreTexts, Wikipedia, JoVE, yourgenome, Osmosis, open textbooks | Open textbook chapters; one-concept explainer pages | Long, textbook-style; few diagrams + practice + mistakes on one page | **Strong fit.** The lesson pages (`/lessons/`) give the answer first, the Meselson–Stahl diagram, a worked example, the common mistake and explained questions. Biology u04 is in the next pilot batch |
| Electrophiles and nucleophiles | Master Organic Chemistry, LibreTexts, Brilliant, BYJU'S, Vedantu, university pressbooks | University explainers; Indian exam-prep (JEE/NEET) sites | Little UK-school framing; few explained practice questions | Strong fit for a lesson page with practice. The lesson (chemistry u13-02, Organic Reaction Mechanisms) clears the quality floor; its unit isn't in the next batch, so consider it for the one after |
| Revision timetable generator | Canva, Taskade (AI), GoConqr, Chartered College, Twinkl, Save My Exams, Ayoa, template sites | Design tools and AI generators; printable templates; advice articles | Generators don't know what you've learned or forgotten | Smart Plan / time blocking that schedules by what's fading. Competition high: win "revision timetable that adapts" and the guide, not the head term |
| Free online flashcard maker | Visme, Canva, CapCut, GoConqr, Quizlet, FlexClip, puzzel.org, AI generators | Design-template makers; big brands | Design-first, not learning-first; most have no spaced repetition or source checking | Notes Studio's checked cards from your own notes. Very crowded: compete on "flashcards checked against your notes" |
| PDF to flashcards | Wondershare, Taskade, UPDF, RemNote, Brainscape, Quizgecko, Scholarly, many AI tools | AI converters with free-use limits (8–12 cards or pages free) | Cards aren't traceable to the source; tight free limits; privacy unclear | Every card linked to its source line, no account to try. Competition is **high** (revised from medium) |
| How to revise for exams | University study-skills pages (Sheffield, Warwick, Bath, Open University…), NI Direct, Prospects, Twinkl | Authoritative .ac.uk / .gov.uk guidance | General advice, no tools to act on it | The active-recall guide + tools: link the advice to doing it. Hard to outrank universities; earn links *from* them instead (see the playbook) |
| SQE1 revision | SRA, Legal Cheek, Legal Futures, law providers | News about the SQE1 changes from January 2027; provider blogs | Little free, structured practice | **Act on the news:** the SRA has changed SQE1 from January 2027 (170 questions per paper, updated assessment specifications). Re-check LawGradify's SQE mapping (`docs/SQE_SPEC_MAPPING.md`) against the new specification before promoting it |
| Nursing student revision (NMC) | NMC standards documents, university PDFs, RCNi | Regulator documents (the search read "revision" as the standards' review) | Almost no student revision resources ranked | **Possible gap**: low competition for revision resources mapped to the NMC proficiencies. Validate demand in Keyword Planner before investing |

## What this changes

- `scripts/seo/keywords.mjs`: "pdf to flashcards" competition raised to high; six clusters now carry `sampled: "2026-11-24"`, so the opportunity database labels their competition "search sample", not "estimate".
- Two actions for the owner: (1) LawGradify's SQE mapping against the January 2027 specification; (2) whether to map biology and chemistry to a GCSE/A level specification, which would unlock the largest school searches honestly.

## When to add the next batch

Scale on evidence, not on a timer: check Search Console from the first week (URL inspection on a sample of pilot pages shows indexing within days). Add the next batch (`docs/growth/CONTENT-BACKLOG.md`: 11 units, 281 pages) when all of these hold:

| Criterion | Status |
|---|---|
| At least 70% of the pilot's indexable URLs are indexed (Indexing › Pages, filtered to the lessons sitemap) | **unknown**: needs Search Console |
| At least 40% of indexed pilot pages have had an impression | **unknown**: needs Search Console |
| Under 20% are 'Crawled – currently not indexed' (Google saw them and declined) | **unknown**: needs Search Console |
| No pilot page below the quality floor is indexable | **not met** |

**Decision today:** wait for Search Console data; meanwhile fix the pages below the floor, which is needed whatever the data says.

If Google indexes the pilot quickly, the next batch can follow within days. If most pages sit in "Crawled – currently not indexed", improve them first: more pages of the same kind won't fix that.
